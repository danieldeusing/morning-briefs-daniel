Where we stand & why: a weekend without a trigger

FX in detail · the causal path · Update since Sat

Correction Saturday's brief said "Sun Oct 11, 9:00 PM BRT first debate (Record)". That debate is not happening. Record canceled it already on Friday evening and gives "incompatibilidade de agendas dos candidatos" as the reason. According to SBT News, Lula and Flávio had not confirmed. Valor, Poder360, SBT News and Exame report the cancellation.[1]

Your rate is the product of EUR/USD × USD/BRL — two gears. Since Friday evening both have stood still. So the question is: where does Monday start?

Chain 1 — the starting point sits below the fix.

ECB fix 09:15 BRT: 5.6086→ PTAX 13:07 BRT: 5.5844→ Close ~17:00 BRT: ~5.581→ Monday starts at ~5.58

The fix is a snapshot from the morning. After that the dollar fell to R$ 4.985. The week brought −4.44%; Valor calls it the biggest weekly move after a first round since the real was introduced (headline).[2]

Chain 2 — a holiday makes Monday thin.

Holiday in Brazil + US + Japan→ B3 closed, real traded only abroad→ low volume→ small but random swings in the fix
Thin trading: few buyers and sellers in the market. Like a flea market in the rain — few people, and one big buyer visibly moves the price. So Monday's fix says little about the trend. The real test comes Tuesday, when São Paulo trades again.

Chain 3 — the dollar is strong globally, the real stronger locally.

DXY 4th weekly gain→ EUR/USD near 17-month low→ your euro gear weak+ real gear strong (election)→ EUR/BRL pushed down twice

Normally a strong dollar and a weak euro partly cancel out in EUR/BRL. This time both gears pulled against you. The euro suffers from France: 10-year yield 4.87%, near a 20-year-plus high.[3]

Look back: none today. Without a new fix, no range can be checked.

Right: EUR/BRL at a euro of 1.12 (about today) and 1.20 (BofA target end-2027), my own calculation. The series rises slowly: Brazil has more inflation than the US, so the real loses a little value over the years. For 2028 the economists' range runs from 4.50 to 6.00.[9]

Interest advantage (carry) over years: today Brazil pays 13.75%, the ECB 2.50%. The Focus sees the Selic at 10.50% in mid-2028. Like two savings accounts: the smaller the interest gap, the less reason to send money to Brazil. That weakens the real slowly.

My view: the center of mass in two to three years lies above today's rate, at around 5.8–6.1. Two forces lift it: the shrinking interest advantage and a euro recovering from its low. The counter-argument: Commerzbank saw the dollar at 4.80 at end-2027 (Aug 19), Goldman's fair value is also 4.80 — both equal only ~5.38. That needs a fiscal plan that holds. Important: the whole Focus series is from before the election. Tuesday's Focus shows whether economists lower their long-term targets.

My dissent: the discount is already in the price

The consensus says "dollar toward 4.50" — I see it getting there only later

  • The market is running ahead of the result. USD/BRL 4.985 already sits at BTG's target for a Flávio win, below the Focus median of 5.20 and only 3.9% above the lowest Focus value of 4.80.
  • Morgan Stanley and UBS say so themselves. Morgan Stanley ties 4.50 to a credible fiscal plan, UBS ties 4.50–4.60 to a runoff like round 1 and a stable debt ratio. Neither is in place yet.
  • The funds have already sold. The CFTC data show: speculators cut their real bets in election week.
  • No bank has lowered its year-end target after round 1. For Itaú, Bradesco, XP, Santander and BTG, the research found no new dollar figure.

So I see 4.50 as a target for 2027, not for October. If Flávio names a credible finance minister before Oct 25, I am wrong — then your rate points toward 5.30.
