Where we stand & why: both gears turn your way

FX in detail · the causal path · Thursday = diff vs. Monday

Your rate is the product EUR/USD × USD/BRL — two gears. On Tuesday only the real gear turned. Since Wednesday evening both turn, and both lift your rate. That is rare: usually the effects partly cancel out.

Chain 1 — hot US inflation, yet a weaker dollar.

CPI: core 2.6% instead of ~2.4–2.5%→ Dollar rally from the 18-month high runs out→ DXY −0.47% to 102.06→ EUR/USD 1.1215 (+0.52% TE day change, ~+0.2% vs. the fix)→ EUR/BRL ~+0.6% vs. the fix (cross 5.6608, +0.64%)
Core rate: inflation without energy and food. These prices often jump because of weather or war. The core rate shows the stubborn part — like a fever without the brief flush after exercise.

US prices rose 0.6% from the previous month in September, and 3.7% over twelve months. The core rate rose 0.3% on the month and 2.6% on the year.[1] Expected were 0.2% and 2.4–2.5%.[2] Normally such a number strengthens the dollar, because it makes another Fed hike more likely. According to TradingEconomics, the market now prices ~73% for a pause in October.[3] Still, the DXY fell. TradingEconomics only speaks of a pullback from the 18-month high; no source names a clear reason. My reading: the dollar had already priced in the hot number. What counts for you is the result: the euro recovers from its weakest level in a year and a half.[3]

My dissent: Goldman chases the market

The bank cuts its target on the day the race tightens

  • The timing. Goldman cuts to 4.70 on Oct 13 — one day before Quaest shows a tie. The report could not yet know the Quaest number.
  • 4.70 needs two things. A Flávio win and a credible fiscal plan. I see the first part at ~75%; for the second there is still no finance minister and no program.
  • The economists in the country are more cautious. Focus 2027: 5.26, Itaú 5.50. The gap to Goldman (4.80) is large — one side is clearly wrong.

That is why I see 4.70 as a target for the good 2027 scenario, not for January. If Flávio names a credible finance minister with a plan before Oct 25, I am wrong — then your rate heads toward 5.30.
