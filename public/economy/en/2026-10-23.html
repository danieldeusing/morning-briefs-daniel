Where we stand & why: two gears against each other

FX in detail · the causal path · Friday = diff, last issue before the election

Your rate is the product EUR/USD × USD/BRL. On Thursday at fix time both gears turned, but against each other. The dollar in reals rose from 5.0233 to 5.0490 (+0.51%). The euro in dollars fell from 1.1234 to 1.1205 (−0.26%). Net, you were left with a gain of 0.25%. All my own calculation from ECB fixes.

Chain 1 — tech crash in New York, flight from risk in São Paulo.

Meta plans higher AI investment→ Nasdaq −2.60%→ Investors pull money from emerging markets→ Ibovespa −1.15%, DI +8 to +15 bp→ Real −0.5% at fix time

According to InfoMoney, election uncertainty and the US tech sell-off pressed together; Vale fell about 4%.[1] Unlike on Wednesday, rates and the state's credit insurance rose as well. The Nasdaq figure has a single source; but the TE dollar page confirms a "tech and AI sell-off".[3]

Flight from risk ("risk-off"): when risky assets in the US fall on one day, many funds also sell their other risky holdings — for example Brazilian stocks and the real. Like a thunderstorm at the public pool: when one person runs out of the water, everyone follows, no matter which pool they were in.

Chain 2 — oil and flight to safe havens press on the euro.

Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia→ Brent $113 (+4%)→ Europe pays more for energy→ EUR/USD 1.1234 → 1.1205→ slows the rise of your rate

According to TradingEconomics, reports of possible new US strikes against Iran added to this.[2] Expensive oil usually helps the real, because Brazil exports oil; on Thursday the flight from risk was stronger. Without the weaker euro your rate would have risen 0.5% instead of 0.25%.

Chain 3 — Datafolha after the close. The tie came at 19:07 BRT. B3 has not traded it yet. The first price is early trading: 5.0518 (+0.14%). Today at 09:00 BRT the market opens, at 09:15 BRT the ECB measures.

Look back, honestly: yesterday I expected the fix at 5.60–5.70, most likely 5.65. It came in at 5.6574 — a hit again. The weekly range 5.58–5.77 holds through today.

My dissent: 77% for Flávio despite a tie

The betting market stays far ahead of the polls

  • The two largest polls say "open". Datafolha 50 to 50, Quaest 45 to 44. Both gaps are smaller than the margin of error.
  • The betting market hardly moves. −1.45 points on a tie. Anyone betting 77% apparently believes the polls underestimate Flávio.

That is why I think the Lula case is undervalued in the rate. I am wrong if a final poll on Saturday again shows a gap of three or more points.
