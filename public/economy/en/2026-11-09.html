Where we stand & why

[BR] FX in detail · as of Fri Nov 6 · Monday = full rewrite

The month in one sentence: Lula's unexpected win in the Oct 25 runoff devalued the real by about a tenth, and it has not recovered since. The ECB fix jumped from 5.6485 (Oct 23) to 6.2523 (Oct 26), dipped briefly to 5.93 and has been climbing again since Oct 28, with a small dip on Nov 5.[1] The day after the election the dollar rose as much as 12.62% to R$ 5.6056.[7]

Chain 1 — Fiscal fear is the main driver.

Lula win, against the polls→ Doubts about austerity→ Risk premium ↑: 10Y yield 15.18% (+2.23 pp)→ Dollar +10.4% in 1 month→ EUR/BRL 5.65 → 6.14

Before the runoff, polls had Flávio Bolsonaro narrowly ahead; the win came as a surprise to the market.[7] On Oct 29, Guilherme Boulos wrote on X that the "tesouraço" had been defeated at the ballot box — the market read that as a rejection of hard austerity. [9] According to Estadão, the campaign promises add up to over R$ 400 billion; the 2027 budget needs at least R$ 40 billion in adjustment.[10] The finance minister is Dario Durigan, the planning minister Bruno Moretti.[10] A report that Rafael Fonteles (Piauí) is to take over the finance ministry is unconfirmed.[9]

Risk premium: the extra interest investors demand from a country whose finances are uncertain. Like a higher rate for a friend who has already paid you back late twice. As long as the premium stays high, the real stays weak.

Chain 2 — Friday: strong US jobs, the dollar rises everywhere.

US jobs +165,000 (expected 65,000)→ Fed hike Dec. ~81%→ Dollar stronger→ Real −0.37%, euro weaker→ EUR/BRL only +0.15%

The US economy added 165,000 jobs in October, almost three times as many as expected; unemployment fell to 4.1%.[15] After a hike in September, the Fed stands at 3.75–4.00% and held on Oct 28 — with two dissents in favor of a hike.[16] Now the market prices a December hike at ~81% (TE; I did not check the CME FedWatch tool).[5] ING also expects a hike.[3] The dollar closed at 5.5087, its highest close since December 2025.[3] Because the euro fell on the same day too, your rate barely rose.

Chain 3 — The high interest rate is the net under the real.

Copom holds 13.75% (Nov 4)→ Easing cycle over→ Carry stays attractive→ slows further real slide

On Nov 4 the Copom left the Selic unanimously at 13.75% and ended the cuts.[12] Its 2026 inflation forecast rose from 5.2 to 5.6% — above the 4.5% ceiling.[12] The Focus of Nov 3 (data Oct 30) still expects 13.50% for end-2026.[18] That would only work with a cut in December. I expect the median to rise to 13.75% today.

Carry: borrowing money cheaply abroad and investing it in Brazil at 13.75%. Like a savings account with a high rate that only pays off as long as the real does not fall. A high rate keeps this money in the country.
My view A speech without numbers does not lower the risk premium. According to TE, Lula already "calmed" the markets around Nov 6 — the 10-year yield eased only 0.04 points.[11] The market wants a finance minister who controls spending, and a package with numbers.[3] Both come no earlier than Nov 16. Until then your rate is more likely to stay up.
