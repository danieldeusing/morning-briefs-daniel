New Chain 1 — Nobody knows who will manage the money.

No finance minister for the next term→ Mercadante rumor (Poder360), denied→ Investors demand a bigger premium→ Dollar +2.13% to 5.777→ EUR/BRL in the market ~6.36

According to Reuters, the portal Poder360 named BNDES chief Aloizio Mercadante among the candidates for finance minister. Mercadante denied it and says he is staying at BNDES. The unease remained, because a name is still missing.[9] StoneX writes that the rumor raised the risk.[7] Trader Fernando Bergallo (FB Capital) to Reuters: "o investidor está cobrando prêmio mais alto por aqui, isso está bem evidente."[9] BTG chief André Esteves expects Lula to deliver on the budget.[11]

Risk premium (prêmio de risco): the extra interest or extra discount investors demand before lending money to an uncertain borrower. Like a landlord who wants a bigger deposit when he doesn't know who is moving in.

Update Chain 2 — The Fed gets tougher, the euro weaker too.

December hike >95% priced in (TE)→ DXY 103.6→ Real and euro weaker→ EUR/BRL rises, but dampened

TE sees +25 basis points in December priced in at over 95% (Mon: ~92%) and "four more hikes by next September".[5] Deutsche Bank: 4.50–4.75% by March.[15] Counterweight: ECB Vice President Vujčić considers further hikes in 2027 possibly "justified".[4] That is why the euro falls less than the real.

Dollar future: a contract that locks in the dollar rate for a later day, today — like a pre-order at a fixed price. The December contract closed Mon at 5.80.[9]

New Chain 3 — Weak growth, high rates, capital leaves.

IBC-Br Q3 −0.9%, inflation 4.63%→ Morgan Stanley: "underweight"→ Ibovespa −1.83%→ fewer dollars flow into the country

The economy shrank 0.9% in the third quarter, 0.2% in September.[14] Morgan Stanley cut Brazil in its Latin America equity portfolio from "equal-weight" to "underweight", citing, per Reuters, a weaker economy, high interest rates and a missing budget framework. Ibovespa target 2027: 160,000.[10]

Underweight: a fund holds less of a country than its benchmark index calls for. Like a cook who uses an ingredient more sparingly than the recipe says. If funds follow the advice, they swap reais for dollars.

New Focus catches up, the banks don't. Monday's Focus (data to 11/13) raises the dollar for 2026 to 5.40 and for 2027 to 5.45. Those who reported most recently see 2026 at 5.50, 2027 at 5.40 — peak, then a partial recovery.[12][13] Our research found no bank revision after 11/12 in four days of searching.

My view Monday was not a data shock but a confidence shock: the market is pricing the gap in the economic team. That can turn fast. A credible name can win back 2–3% for the real within hours. A name the market reads as a spending friend pushes the dollar toward 6.00. That is why this week is binary — and the euro below 1.10 dampens both paths only partly.

It's Tuesday, November seventeenth. The Brazilian real tumbled on Monday afternoon. According to Reuters, the dollar closed at five point seven seven seven reais, up a little over two percent and the highest since April of twenty twenty-five.

The European Central Bank's fix from Monday came in at six point two eight three eight. But it was set in the morning, before the slide. In the market this morning, the euro already costs around six point three six reais, a bit more than one percent higher. For a calculation today, that's the more realistic number.

The trigger was confidence, not new data. Lula's next term still has no finance minister. Reuters reports that the outlet Poder360 floated the head of the development bank BNDES, Aloizio Mercadante, as a candidate. He denied it, and the unease stuck around. On top of that, Morgan Stanley downgraded Brazilian stocks, and the economy shrank zero point nine percent in the third quarter.

The U.S. is adding pressure too. The dollar index sits at one hundred three point six, its highest since April of twenty twenty-five. A Fed rate hike in December is seen as almost certain. Because that weakens the euro as well, your rate rises less than the dollar does.

The Focus survey raised its dollar forecast for twenty twenty-six to five point four zero and for twenty twenty-seven to five point four five. No bank has changed its forecast since the slide. All bank targets sit below today's rate.

My outlook, as my own confidence and not a market figure: for today's fix, most likely six point three five. For the week, six point three six. For the month, six point three zero. Three to six months out, six point one eight, and twelve months out, six point two zero. Over the next two to three years, the center of gravity stays between six and six point six.

Today the Senate votes on changes to the savings package, the Fed minutes come out Wednesday, and on Friday the stock exchange and banks in Brazil are closed. The biggest lever is the name of the next finance minister. A thousand euros brings about six thousand three hundred sixty reais at this morning's market rate.
