New Chain 1 — The Senate delays, the numbers get worse.

Senate postpones four amendments to today→ Prisma: 2027 deficit higher→ Package "bem tímido"→ Premium stays→ Real doesn't recover, dollar ~5.78

Senate president Alcolumbre gave rapporteur Eduardo Braga more time to reach a deal on four open points. Two of them aim to strip savings items: the deferral of payments to states and municipalities (Lei Kandir) and the cap of R$ 3 billion a year on a federal pension compensation payment. The government estimates "uma melhora de até R$ 56 bilhões" in the 2027 primary balance; the Senate passed the main text on 11/12.[8] Reuters cites up to R$ 60 billion.[6] In parallel, the Prisma survey worsened the 2027 deficit to R$ 45.288 billion (previously 44.0) and 2027 gross debt to 87.31% of GDP (single source).[9] Marco Mecchi (Azimut Brasil) told Reuters the package is "bem tímido" and the dollar could run toward R$ 6 "em algum momento" — with no time frame.[6]

Destaque: a motion to hold a separate vote on a single sentence of a bill. Like "the menu yes, but without dessert?" — then only the dessert is argued over.
Primary balance: government revenue minus spending, excluding interest. A deficit means: the state is borrowing money for everyday costs, not just for old debts.

Update Chain 2 — The Fed gets tougher, both currencies fall.

Musalem: up to four hikes→ December ~96% priced in→ DXY 103.6→ Euro 1.0989, real 5.78→ EUR/BRL rises, damped

St. Louis Fed president Musalem says the Fed "may need to raise interest rates as many as four times in the coming months". TE sees +25 basis points in December priced in at ~96% (yesterday: over 95%; source: TE citing CME, the CME page itself not read).[5] US retail sales rose 0.2% in October, exactly as expected; excluding volatile items +0.6%, the strongest gain since August.[37] That supports the Fed hawks. Deutsche Bank sees the Fed at 4.50–4.75% through March.[12] Counterweight: the market also still prices three hikes for the euro.[4] That is why your rate rises more slowly than the dollar.

Fed minutes: the record of the last US rate meeting, published three weeks later. Like the class register after the teachers' conference: you read who wanted to be how strict. If it sounds strict, the dollar rises.

New Chain 3 — The fix catches up, not the market.

Mon: fix 10:15, slump in the afternoon→ Tue: dollar calm at 5.78→ Tue fix reflects Monday→ +1.12% to 6.3539

The jump in the fix is not a new shock, it shows Monday. On Tuesday itself the dollar barely moved (+0.08%), and the December future even dipped slightly to 5.7865.[6] Reuters mentions a seasonal year-end dollar shortage that may keep up the pressure.[6]

New The Focus monthly path (data through 11/13) shows a trough: December 2026 5.40, March–May 2027 5.38, June 5.40, December 2027 5.45.[11] For November it shows 5.409 — the market is at ~5.78, about 7% above. Focus lags the market.

My view Tuesday was a breather, not an all-clear. No name, no Senate result, worse Prisma numbers, a tougher Fed. That the real still didn't fall further shows: a lot of fear is already in the price. Today the week is triple binary — Senate, Fed minutes, and a name at any moment. I stay with "high, two-sided".
