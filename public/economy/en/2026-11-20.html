In the morning everything matched: PTAX euro 6.4672, 0.14% below the fix.[1][2] Not in the evening: Reuters close × TE euro = 6.514 (+0.57%), the TE table shows 6.5254 (+0.76%).[3][4][6] For a calculation this means: the fix of 6.4765 underestimates the market by roughly R$ 40–50 per €1,000. Today the fix catches up — with the domestic market closed.
PTAX: the official daily rate of the Brazilian central bank, an average of four readings between 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. Like the average grade from four tests in the morning — the afternoon does not count. Today: no PTAX.

New Chain 1 — Lula says "no rush".

Lula: no time pressure, no cuts to rights→ DI Jan. 2028 +53 basis points (13:06)→ Dollar above 5.92→ Central bank sells US$ 1 bn→ briefly 5.85, close 5.9323

At a ceremony in the Palácio do Planalto, Lula said he wants to balance the accounts, but "não haverá redução de direitos". On the ministers: "Eu não tenho pressa."[6][8] That is exactly what the market is waiting on. From 12:03 to 12:08 the central bank sold US$ 1 bn on the spot market; the dollar briefly fell to 5.85 and then rose above 5.93.[6] The central bank billion held only for minutes.

Spot auction (leilão à vista): the central bank sells dollars outright from its reserves. Like a baker who puts out one more basket of bread when a line forms: the line gets shorter for a moment, but if everyone is still hungry, it is soon back.

New Chain 2 — The package is too small, says the yield curve.

Market expected ~R$ 80 bn→ Government: "over R$ 50 bn"→ DI 2028 14.815% (+30), DI 2035 15.495% (+25)→ Risk premium on everything in reais

The Senate passed the package on Wednesday evening; now the Chamber is missing.[6] Long rates rose sharply anyway: DI January 2028 to 14.815%, +55 basis points on the week.[7] Gabriel Redivo (Aware Investments) told Reuters that the market had previously expected a package of "cerca de R$80 bilhões".[7] According to TE, R$ 33–38.3 bn of the estimated R$ 46–56 bn comes from higher revenue, not from spending cuts.[3] Planning Minister Moretti held out further measures, without a figure; the Treasury did not intervene on the curve.[7]

DI future: a bet on how high the interbank rate will be on average until a given date. When it rises, investors demand more reward for their risk with Brazil. Like a contractor who adds a surcharge for an unreliable client.

New Chain 3 — The world moves into the dollar.

Selling in AI stocks, Anthropic IPO→ Flight to the "safe" dollar, dollar purchases for ANT shares→ DXY ~103.8–103.9→ Peso (MX, CO), rand fall with it

TE explains the dollar rise with selling in AI stocks. In addition: for Anthropic's record IPO, foreign investors reportedly swapped currency into dollars.[5] Anthropic raised US$ 81.3 bn and debuted at a valuation above US$ 2 trillion; the STOXX 600 closed at its lowest since Oct 23.[9] The peso (MX, CO) and rand fell too[6], but the real more: the larger part is homemade. (AI details in the ai-dev brief.)

New The bright spot — a name moves closer. Reuters (sources in the Planalto): for the Fazenda, Piauí governor Rafael Fonteles and Vice President Geraldo Alckmin are ahead, and the decision could come "já na próxima semana". Fonteles is seen favorably by financial markets; about Alckmin, one source says: "Um pacote de ajuste mediano com ele à frente teria mais impacto". Durigan is not expected to stay.[8]

My view The real no longer trades the package, but the trust in the person behind it. Lula's "no rush" was costlier yesterday than any number. That is why the name for the Fazenda next week is the biggest single lever — in both directions. Until then the central bank only catches the spikes, not the trend.

It's Friday, November twentieth, and it's a holiday in Brazil. Thursday's European Central Bank fix came in at six point four seven seven, up nearly one and a half percent in a single day. Yesterday we guessed six point four two. The range held, but the point estimate came in too low.

This fix was also set in the morning. In the afternoon the real kept sliding. According to Reuters, the dollar closed at five point nine three reais, up one percent, its highest level since April twenty twenty-five. Over the shortened week it climbed almost five percent. Converted to euros, the market now sits at about six point five one.

The trigger was Lula himself. He said there would be no cuts to rights, and that he is in no rush to pick his ministers. That's exactly the news the market is waiting for. Long-term rates jumped by about thirty basis points. The central bank sold one billion dollars, but the effect lasted only minutes.

On top of that, the dollar is strong worldwide. According to Trading Economics, investors fled into the dollar after selling in AI stocks. For Anthropic's record IPO, foreigners also swapped money into dollars. But the real fell harder than its neighbors, so most of the move is homegrown.

There is one bright spot. According to Reuters, Piauí Governor Rafael Fonteles and Vice President Geraldo Alckmin are the frontrunners for finance minister. A decision could come as soon as next week. A credible name is the single biggest lever for the exchange rate.

My own take, as my confidence and not a market figure: for today's fix, most likely six point five one. For the next seven days, six point four eight. For the month, six point three eight. Over three to six months, six point two five, and over twelve months, six point three. Over two to three years, my center of gravity is between six and six point seven.

A thousand euros brings roughly six thousand four hundred seventy-seven reais at Thursday's fix. Today the stock market and banks in Brazil are closed and trading is thin. The market reopens Monday, with the Focus report and perhaps the name for the finance ministry. We'll look at that in the next brief. The decision is yours.
