Where we stand & why

[BR] [USA] [EUR] FX in detail · Monday full rewrite · As of Mon Nov 23, ~02:15 BRT

Last week in numbers: ECB fix Monday Nov 16 6.2838, Friday Nov 20 6.5259 — +3.85%. Since the last fix before the runoff (Oct 23: 5.6485) that is +15.5%.[1] The PTAX shows the same for the dollar: 5.7324 (Nov 16) to 5.8889 (Nov 19), +2.7%; Friday was a holiday.[7]

PTAX: the official daily rate of the Brazilian central bank, an average of several bank quotes until about 1 p.m. Like the official calibration mark on a measuring cup: many contracts in Brazil settle against it.

Chain 1 — Fiscal doubts beat the central bank.

Doubts about the austerity package→ DI Jan 2028 +30 bp to 14.815%→ BCB sells US$ 1 bn→ Dollar 5.9323 anyway (+1.10%)→ Fri fix 6.5259

On Thursday long-term rates rose sharply, because of "desconfiança sobre ajuste fiscal".[14] The central bank sold one billion dollars; the dollar closed at its highest level since April 2025 anyway.[8] Signal of the week: central bank money alone does not turn the rate.

DI futures: bets on how high the Brazilian policy rate will be on average in the future. Like a thermometer for confidence in the state: the higher, the more interest investors demand for their risk.

Chain 2 — Monday starts without a catch-up effect.

Fri: offshore trading only→ Mon early: dollar 5.9487 · euro 1.0983→ Cross-rate 6.5336→ +0.12% vs. fix

The cross-rate from two independent TE values is only 0.12% above Friday's fix.[3][5] So today's fix depends on new news, not on old backlog.

Chain 3 — The austerity package in the Chamber.

Senate: 69 to 2 (Nov 18)→ Chamber: Mon Nov 23 or Tue Nov 24→ Text unchanged → Lula signs→ lower risk premium→ Real firmer, your rate lower

Per Gazeta do Povo (Nov 19), PLP 299/2026 will be debated and voted "na próxima segunda-feira (23), em sessão extraordinária". The package brings "até R$ 56,1 bilhões em 2027"; the rapporteur is Mauro Benevides Filho (União-CE).[9] SBT News, by contrast, expected the vote on Tuesday, Nov 24.[10] We found no confirmation that the session takes place today. Per Portal de Prefeitura, the rapporteur wants to adopt the Senate text in full (search summary only, not checked in full text).[11] Range for 2027: R$ 45.1–56.1 bn.[12]

Risk premium: the extra interest investors demand because they trust the state less. Like a loan for someone with a shaky job: the bank wants more. A passed austerity package is a bit like an employment contract.

My view: a clean passage is partly priced in after the clear Senate vote. Dangerous are changes, a postponement or a narrow majority. Commerzbank considers even the package at full size (BRL 50–60 bn) "unlikely to be enough to fully allay market concerns".[28]

Chain 4 — The global dollar slows your rate.

Fed hike Dec 9 priced in at ~93%→ DXY near 19-month high→ Euro 1.098 (month −1.70%)→ EUR/BRL rises more slowly than USD/BRL

The real fell almost 10% against the dollar over the month, less against the euro.[3][5][6]

Understanding, not a recommendation

On €1,000, Friday's fix (6.5259) yields, on paper, R$ 6,525.90 — R$ 242 more than at the Nov 16 fix (6.2838) and R$ 877 more than before the runoff (Oct 23: 5.6485).[1] You are above every converted bank target and just above the TE model for end of Q4 (6.50). My view: this week has two dates that can push the rate in either direction. Whoever exchanges a portion now takes a historically high rate before the events. Whoever waits is betting on a stalling Chamber. The decision is yours.

It's Monday, November twenty-third. After a three-day break, the market in Brazil reopens. Friday's last fix from the European Central Bank stands at six point five two six, the highest level in the series. The offshore market calculates six point five three on Monday morning, so almost the same.

The week before sent a clear signal. On Thursday the central bank sold one billion dollars, and the dollar still climbed to five point nine three reais, its highest since April twenty twenty-five. According to Reuters, that came down to doubts about the government's austerity package.

That very package now sits in the Chamber of Deputies. Per Gazeta do Povo, a vote is due today in an extraordinary session, while SBT News named Tuesday. The Senate approved it sixty-nine to two. If the Chamber changes the text, it has to go back to the Senate. A clean passage takes pressure off more than it pushes the rate down sharply.

Then there's the new finance minister. According to Reuters, Rafael Fonteles and Geraldo Alckmin are out in front, and a decision could come as soon as this week. ING writes that a name like Alckmin could give the real a breather.

In the U.S., the market prices in about ninety-three percent odds of a Fed rate hike on December ninth. On Wednesday, growth and inflation data are due. On that same December ninth, Brazil's central bank also decides.

Over twelve months, the banks mostly argue about the euro. Commerzbank sees it at one point zero six dollars at the end of twenty twenty-seven, Nordea at one point one four, ING at one point one eight. For your rate, that makes a difference of around eleven percent.

My assessment, as confidence and not as a market figure: for today's fix, most likely six point five three. For the week, six point four seven. For the month, six point four zero. Over three to six months, six point two five, and over twelve months, six point three zero, in a wide range.

One thousand euros at Friday's fix comes to roughly six thousand five hundred twenty-six reais. Tomorrow we'll look at the first fix of the week, the new Focus report and the outcome in the Chamber. The decision is yours.
