ECB fix: the reference rate the European Central Bank measures once a day at 14:15 CET (10:15 BRT). Like a photo of traffic: it shows the jam at 10:15, not the evening.

Chain 1 — The fix caught the peak.

Fiscal worry in the morning→ Dollar 6.0043 (+1.21%)→ ECB measures 10:15 BRT: 5.998→ Fix 6.5611→ Afternoon: name rumors→ Close 5.9293 · evening ~6.47

For the morning Reuters cites "preocupações dos investidores com a política fiscal", for the afternoon "especulações de que nomes amigáveis ao mercado poderão ocupar cargos na área econômica".[8] The ECB values imply a dollar of 5.9979 at the fix — almost the day's high.[2] In the evening: dollar 5.9298 × euro 1.09084 = −1.41% vs. the fix.[3][5] TE direct (6.4676) and the 13:06 PTAX (6.5076) lie on the same path downward.[4][7] 6.56 was the price at 10:15, not in the evening.

Chain 2 — The austerity package is through the Chamber.

Senate 69 to 2 (11/18)→ Chamber: 353 to 128, text unchanged→ straight to sanction→ one risk less→ today: first reaction

Vote on Monday evening, after the market close: "Sim: 353; Não: 128; Abstenção: 5". The 90 amendments failed by 129 to 319. Closing line: "A matéria vai à Sanção (PLP 299-A/2026)."[9] According to the government, the package brings R$ 45.1–56.1 bn in 2027.[28] Open: the day Lula signs.

Sanction: the president's signature that puts a passed law into force. Like the stamp from the building office: the plan is approved, but you can only build once it is stamped.

My view: the market priced in the result. Rates fell even before the vote (DI January 2028 −20 basis points to 14.63%)[10], and in Asia the dollar barely moved (−0.02%).[3] No big jump expected; Commerzbank considers the package "unlikely to be enough" anyway.[26]

DI futures: bets on how high the Brazilian policy rate will be on average in the future. Like a fever thermometer for trust in the state: the higher, the more interest investors demand.

Chain 3 — The survey catches up with the market.

Dollar rises to 5.9→ Focus end-2026: 5.40 → 5.55→ Selic end-2027: 12.75 → 13.00%→ Rates stay high longer→ supports the real

Focus of 11/23 (data 11/19): dollar at end-2026 5.55, fresh responses from the last 5 days 5.65; end-2027 5.50; inflation 2027 5.12%.[11] The Finance Ministry cuts 2027 growth to 1.8% (from 2.3%) and raises 2027 inflation to 4.5% (from 3.8%), because of the "depreciação cambial".[12] The survey follows the rate; it does not lead it.

Focus report: weekly central bank survey of over 100 institutions. Like a weather report from many meteorologists: reliable on average, often a day late.

Chain 4 — The strong dollar slows your rate.

Fed hike 12/09 ~95% priced in→ DXY 104.27→ Euro 1.0908 (month −2.37%)→ EUR/BRL rises slower than USD/BRL

Month: dollar +9.53% against the real, euro −2.37%.[3][5][6] The euro weakness takes part of the gain away from you again.

It's Tuesday, November twenty-fourth. The biggest news came last night, after the market close: Brazil's Chamber of Deputies passed the government's austerity package without changes. It now goes straight to President Lula for his signature. When he signs is still open.

Monday's fix from the European Central Bank came in at six point five six one one, the highest in this series. But a word of caution: the central bank takes its reading at ten fifteen Brazil time. That was exactly when the dollar briefly topped six reais. According to Reuters, that was down to worries about the public finances.

In the afternoon the market turned. Rumors about market-friendly picks for the Finance Ministry helped the real, and the dollar closed at five point nine three. By evening, the euro-to-real calculation showed only about six point four seven. That's one point four percent below the fix. So Monday's record was a snapshot of the peak.

The central bank's survey of economists is catching up with the market. It now sees the dollar at five point five five at year-end, instead of five point four zero, and the benchmark interest rate at thirteen percent at the end of twenty twenty-seven. Rates that stay high longer support the real.

The dollar is strong around the world. The dollar index is at its highest level in twenty months, and markets price in roughly a ninety-five percent chance of a Fed rate hike on December ninth. The euro slid to one point oh nine. That slows your rate.

One important note on the outlook: the latest bank forecast dates from November eighteenth, before the test of the six-real mark. Commerzbank and ING expect the real to recover somewhat, especially if a name like Alckmin emerges.

My call, as my own confidence and not a market figure: for today's fix, most likely six point four six. For the week, six point four five. For the month, six point four zero. Three to six months out, six point two five, and twelve months out, six point three zero, within a wide range.

A thousand euros brings about six thousand five hundred sixty-one reais at Monday's fix, but only about six thousand four hundred sixty-eight at the evening rate. Time of day counts.
