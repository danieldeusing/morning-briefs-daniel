ECB fix: the reference rate that the European Central Bank measures once a day at 14:15 CET (10:15 BRT). Like a photo of traffic: it shows the jam at 10:15, not the evening.

Chain 1 — The fix came before the news.

10:15: ECB measures, fix 6.3272→ 11:32: dollar low 5.7115→ 17:00: close 5.7464→ ~17:30: Trabuco in Brasília→ 18:00: future 5.657→ ~20:00: Planalto confirms→ Fri early: cross 6.20

The spot dollar closed at 5.7464 (−1.23%); Agência Brasil and InfoMoney confirm 5.74.[4][5][6] Around 17:30 word came that Trabuco was in Brasília. The December future closed at 18:00 at 5.657 (−2.83%). At ~20:00 the Planalto confirmed.[5][6] Today 05:15 UTC: dollar 5.652, euro-real 6.2035 — 1.96% below the fix.[8]

Dollar future: a contract that fixes the dollar rate for a later day. It trades one hour longer than the spot market. Like a weather forecast for tomorrow morning: it shows where the rate wants to open today.
Cross rate: work out the euro-real rate from dollar-real times euro-dollar. Like converting a price via a third currency. If it differs strongly from the fix, the market has moved since the photo.

Chain 2 — Why a name moves the rate.

Banker in the Finance Ministry→ Expectation: the budget gets taken seriously→ Risk premium falls→ DI Jan-35 −28 bp · Ibovespa +6%→ Foreign money buys reais

Trabuco is 75 and chairman of the Bradesco board. According to the Planalto he runs the ministry "no próximo mandato"; Durigan "permanecerá à frente da pasta até a transição".[6][7] Trabuco defends the "tripé macroeconômico" and calls the fiscal situation a cause for concern, but "não … um problema de vida ou morte".[6] Persevera: "Esse era o sinal que o mercado estava esperando."[5] Bradesco BBI upgrades Brazilian equities; the real stays "depreciado" even at 5.70.[14]

Risk premium: the surcharge investors demand for uncertainty. Like a higher interest rate for a friend who has paid back late before. When a reliable treasurer arrives, the surcharge falls.
Tripé macroeconômico: Brazil's three rules since 1999 — inflation target, floating exchange rate, budget target. Like a three-legged stool: if one leg is missing, it tips over.

My view: in three hours the market priced in a name. Trabuco only comes in 2027, with no date. The test until then is Durigan: he is working on "novas medidas" and wants a constitutional amendment still in 2026, while Congress is friendly.[13] In 2014 Trabuco rejected Dilma; she took Joaquim Levy, who left at the end of 2015.[5][6] A name is a promise, not a law.

Chain 3 — Good inflation, "good" rate cut.

IPCA-15 0.22% (exp. 0.34%)→ no new hike debate→ DI Jan-28 −9 bp→ real stronger anyway

The inflation preview rose only 0.22% in November; 12 months 4.64% instead of the expected 4.78%. Food +1.51%, transport −0.71%.[12] Neat: it "adia o debate sobre um novo ciclo de alta de juros".[11] Falling rates normally weaken a currency. Here, though, mostly the fear premium fell, not the rate advantage.

DI futures & basis point: bets on the future policy rate; one basis point (bp) is 0.01 percentage points. Like a fever thermometer for confidence in the state.

It's Friday, November twenty-seventh. Wednesday's rumor has turned into an appointment. On Thursday evening the presidential palace confirmed that Luiz Carlos Trabuco, the chairman of Bradesco's board, will run the Finance Ministry starting in twenty twenty-seven.

The news came only after the market close, around eight in the evening. So the European Central Bank's fix from Thursday still stands at six point three two. This morning the market already prices the euro at about six point two zero. That is almost two percent lower. Today's fix will most likely catch up.

The day before was already strong. According to Reuters, the dollar fell by a little more than one percent to five point seven four. The dollar future, which trades an hour longer, dropped to five point six five after the first report. The stock market in São Paulo rose six percent, its biggest jump since the first round of the election.

Good inflation news came on top. The preview for November showed just zero point two two percent, instead of the expected zero point three four. That pushes another rate hike further away. Normally that weakens a currency. This time, though, it was mostly the fear over the budget that eased, and the real gained.

My view: in three hours the market priced in a name, but not yet a plan. Trabuco only arrives next year. Until then, the current minister, Durigan, has to deliver. He is working on new measures and wants a constitutional amendment this year.

In Europe, the central bank's minutes show a rate hike on December seventeenth is all but decided. In the U.S., a Fed hike in December is seen as nearly certain. The euro held steady against the dollar, and so did the dollar index. The whole move came out of Brasília.

My outlook, as confidence and not a market number: for today's fix, most likely six point two one. For the week and the month, six point two zero each. Over three to six months and twelve months, also around six point two zero, in wide ranges. One important point: since the appointment, there is no new bank forecast.

One thousand euros at Thursday's fix come to about six thousand three hundred twenty-seven reais. At this morning's rate, that would be roughly one hundred twenty-four reais less. On Monday the first Focus survey after the appointment comes out. Then we'll see whether the professionals follow. The decision is yours.
