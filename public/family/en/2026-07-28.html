German-Brazilian corner: why the best kids' programming runs at "Sesc"

Sesc: "Serviço Social do Comércio" — an institution funded by the retail trade sector that runs culture, sports, and education venues. Think of it as a community center that looks like a municipal theater: exhibits, library, concerts, sports — much of it explicitly gratuito.

The German instinct looks for kids' programming at the municipal theater, the museum, or the adult-education center — and so systematically overlooks the place where most of it actually happens here. On Sesc Londrina's current program page, the large majority of listed offerings are marked "Gratuito," from storytelling hour to exhibits. Second rule of thumb for the calendar: closed on Mondays (open Tue–Fri 9 AM–9 PM, Sat/Sun 9 AM–6 PM) — an outing on a Monday is a dead end.
