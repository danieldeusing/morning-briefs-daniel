Monday, August 3 — and the week has a very clear shape. From today through Wednesday it's dry, the rain comes back starting Thursday, and of all days, Dia dos Pais on Sunday, 08/09, is the wettest day of the week in the model: 23.4 mm. Anyone planning something outdoors this week should do it in the next three days. Anyone setting their calendar by that is setting it by 2025.

Health: Paraná is out of the growth group [11][12]

Correction since yesterday This brief cited an InfoGripe bulletin from 07/16 on Sunday and concluded from it that Paraná was in the group with a growth signal. The more recent bulletin already existed at that point: SE 29/2026, published 07/30/2026. It paints a different picture — a better one.

What SE 29/2026 says Only 3 of 27 states now show a SARI incidence at alert, risk, or high-risk level with a growth signal in the long-term trend: Maranhão, Rio Grande do Sul, and Santa Catarina. Paraná isn't among them — it belongs to the 12 states with elevated but no-longer-rising incidence. For comparison: the bulletin for SE 26/2026 (07/09) still listed six growth states, and it stated explicitly, "Os registros de SRAG associados ao VSR continuam aumentando em toda a Região Sul (Paraná, Rio Grande do Sul e Santa Catarina)." That growth in Paraná is over.

Elevated is not the same as rising — and that difference is the whole point. The number of severe respiratory cases in Paraná is still above what counts as normal; it's just no longer growing. For a household with a one-month-old, that means: the curve is finally working in our favor, but the level isn't yet. Winter isn't over — it's passed its peak.

Metapneumovirus: a respiratory virus from the same family as the better-known RSV, and with an almost identical course — a stubborn cold in adults, a hospital case in infants, because it clogs the finest airways. Picture a garden hose and a drinking straw: a millimeter of mucus doesn't bother the hose and completely blocks the straw. A newborn breathes through the straw. What's new in the current bulletin: for this exact virus, InfoGripe reports "um aumento das hospitalizações por metapneumovírus em todo o Sul" — rising admissions across all of southern Brazil. In practice, that doesn't change the rule, only its reason: wash your hands before anyone picks up the baby, and anyone with a cold skips kissing her these weeks.

Region & culture: EnCena is off by a year [14]

Regional search results these days prominently show: "A 20ª edição do festival de teatro EnCena começa no dia 21 de agosto" — the Jacarezinho theater festival, 50 km away, with Vera Holtz on stage at the Conjunto Amadores de Teatro. That would be the culture pick of the month in the core ring. Unfortunately, it's the culture pick of last August.

The proof is in the weekdays: the program announcements say "sexta-feira e sábado, dias 22 e 23" — Friday and Saturday, the 22nd and 23rd. August 22, 2026, is a Saturday; in 2025 it was a Friday. The report describes the 20th edition, from 2025. The 21st edition (XXI EnCena) ran this year from May 18 to 29, 2026 — with international ambitions, companies from Chile, Uruguay, and Canada, and a program largely directed or written by women. It's been over for two and a half months. That also resolves the "20th or 21st edition" contradiction that jumps out immediately when you search: both numbers are correct, they just belong to different years.

For the calendar, that means: there's no theater festival running in Jacarezinho in August. The next big date in the Norte Pioneiro is the 54ª EFAPI Expo in Santo Antônio da Platina, 08/19–08/23, with an agricultural and trade fair, rodeo, and food row — family-friendly by day, show business by night. The admission price isn't published.

School & calendar [15]

Nothing changes the rhythm. The 2nd trimester runs without interruption from 05/18 to 09/04/2026 — there's no break window in August, and Brazil has no public holiday at all this month. Dia dos Pais falls on Sunday, 08/09, is explicitly not a holiday, and Monday is a regular school day. 08/07 (Dia do Servidor da Educação) and 08/11 (Dia do Estudante) are commemorative dates with no day off. In practice, the Sunday date means two things: school crafts projects happen this week, and anyone wanting to eat out at midday on 08/09 should book now — with 23 mm of rain forecast, everyone will want to be indoors that day.

It's Monday, August third, and this week has an unusually clean shape. From today through Wednesday, it stays dry. Starting Thursday, the rain comes back. And of all days, Father's Day — Dia dos Pais — on Sunday the ninth, turns out to be the wettest day of the whole week in the forecast model.

On the numbers: Climatempo has Ribeirão Claro today at fifteen to twenty-six degrees with zero rain. Tuesday is the same, Wednesday a touch cooler at fifteen to twenty-three. All three days sit at zero percent chance of rain, and the second portal, theweather, comes to the same conclusion independently for Jacarezinho. If something outdoors is on the agenda this week, now's the time.

Thursday is when it turns. Climatempo expects eight point three millimeters on Thursday and seven point two on Friday. That lines up with a cold air mass the state weather service, Simepar, had already flagged for this weekend back in late July — eight degrees in Curitiba on Saturday, four on Sunday.

For plans, Saturday is the day. In Londrina, the Churrasco do Paizão runs from eleven in the morning at Norte Shopping — free, with bounce houses, classic cars, and live music. And right around the corner, in Parque Ney Braga, sits the Aquário de Londrina, open year-round and also free, Saturdays nine to five, with fifteen tanks and more than forty fish species. That's our weekend pick, because it works no matter what the weather does. Closer to home, the artisan fair in Jacarezinho stays outdoors and uncrowded — the better call if the baby's coming along.

One last warning for the calendar. Search results are currently pushing the claim that the EnCena theater festival in Jacarezinho starts August twenty-first. That's a year old. The weekdays in those program announcements line up with 2025, not this year. The twenty-first edition already ran from May eighteenth to the twenty-ninth, and it's over. There's no theater festival in Jacarezinho this August. The next big regional date is the fifty-fourth EFAPI Expo in Santo Antônio da Platina, August nineteenth through twenty-third.
