The model just flipped Dia dos Pais [1][2][3]

Correction since yesterday It's rare for a seven-day forecast to shift this much overnight, which is why it leads this brief: for Sunday, 08/09, Climatempo reported 18–24°C and 23.4 mm of rain for Ribeirão Claro yesterday — the wettest day of the week. Today, the same page, the same place: 20–31°C, 0.0 mm, 0% and the plain text "Sol com algumas nuvens. Não chove." At the same time, Saturday has come down from 32°C to 26°C. So the warm weekend window hasn't vanished, it's just slid one day later — right onto Dia dos Pais.

The week in detail (Climatempo, accessed this morning BRT): today 15–26°C at 0.0 mm, Wednesday 15–24°C at 0.0 mm — both days 0%. Thursday turns with 0.6 mm at 48%, Friday, at just 22°C, becomes the actually wet day with 7.3 mm at 43%, and Saturday follows with 3.1 mm at 44%. From Monday it calms down again: 16–23°C with morning fog. The second portal, theweather.com for Jacarezinho, sees the same shift starting the same Thursday — just stronger: 60%, 80%, 80% for Thu/Fri/Sat, each with thunderstorms. Two portals, one direction, different intensity.

Where the portals contradict each other — Sunday On the high for 08/09, they agree remarkably well: Climatempo 31°C, theweather 89°F — that's about 32°C. On rain, though, it's 0% ("Não chove") against 50% ("light rain"). The low is also five degrees apart (20°C vs. 15°C). We're not picking a value here: Dia dos Pais will be warm, that's confirmed twice over; whether a shower rolls through is an open question. For planning, that doesn't mean "stay indoors," it means "keep a plan B within reach" — and pack sun protection, because with four kids, 31°C is the harder number to manage than a 50% shower risk.

And the cold snap that was announced? This brief cited a Simepar forecast on Sunday putting a new cold air mass "a partir de 8 de agosto" — starting August 8 — with 4°C in Curitiba on 08/09. The newer report from 08/03 dates the low point differently: "Em Curitiba, a quarta-feira, dia 12 de agosto, tem previsão de mínima de 3 ºC e máxima de 17 ºC," plus 1°C in General Carneiro on the 12th and 13th. So the cold air has slid roughly four days later — which is exactly what explains why Sunday suddenly turned warm. And for us: Climatempo shows lows of 14–16°C right through Wednesday, 08/12. The cold snap hits the south and the highlands of the state, not the Norte Pioneiro. Monday's yellow INMET storm and hail alert applied to western and southwestern Paraná and expired at 11:59 PM the same day — it never touched us, and it's history now.

Multivacinação: four weeks to close out the vaccination booklet [4]

Since yesterday and running through September 1, Paraná is holding the Campanha Nacional de Multivacinação 2026 — across all 399 municipalities in the state, at over 1,800 vaccination sites, for children and teens under 15. That puts all four kids in the household in the target group. On offer: the measles vaccine (tríplice viral), polio, flu, and HPV for 9- to 19-year-olds — plus any routine shot missing from the booklet. All free through the SUS.

Multivacinação: not a campaign for one vaccine, but four weeks in which every vaccination site catches up on every missing shot — no appointment, no explanation needed, no having to justify why the booster from two years ago never happened. Picture the one day of the school year when every overdue homework assignment can be turned in without a single question asked: it isn't about a new assignment, it's about closing the gaps that quietly piled up. That's exactly what this campaign is for — measles comes back the moment vaccination coverage in an age group slips below the threshold.
Practical for us The window is deliberately long, but the crowds won't be evenly spread: "Dia D," the nationwide mobilization day, falls on Saturday, 08/22 — most sites extend beyond normal hours that day, but it's also packed. With four kids, one of them an infant, a quiet weekday in the next two weeks makes more sense than the Dia D rush. Pull out every child's Caderneta de Vacinação beforehand — staff only administer what's documented as missing.
