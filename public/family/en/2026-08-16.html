Today in Londrina: Three Events Became Six [2]

The Sesc Cadeião Cultural schedule page lists six events for today, the closing day of the 45th Semana Literária — yesterday, the same page showed three. Update since yesterday Newly added: an Oficina de Mini Livros para crianças at 4 PM, a literary talk at 4:30 PM, and at 6 PM the play „Menino João – Encantador de palavras" with Cia. TPK. Every entry is listed as Gratuito.

For our family's split, that means: at 4 PM two formats run in parallel, and both work. The mini-book workshop is craft work with paper and scissors — a perfect fit for the seven- and eight-year-old, and the two-year-old can join the mess without needing to sit still. Next door, Nina Brondi tells „Pedro Malasartes," the Brazilian Till Eulenspiegel the school kids already know from class. The 6 PM play is the bonus for the older ones.

The 9 AM workshop („Era uma vez, a magia de contar histórias," also Cia. TPK) isn't realistically reachable from Ribeirão Claro — that would mean leaving before seven. A more realistic plan: leave around 9:30 AM, arrive in Londrina around 11 AM, spend the morning in the city (see next section), lunch, then Sesc from 4 PM.

For: Three free formats back-to-back in one place — one drive covers the afternoon and early evening. Indoors, so shaded and quiet for the newborn. The morning can be filled with one of the three free fairs in the city, at no extra cost.
Against: 110 km each way. No age ratings, no mention of a changing room or nursing space on the source page. Anyone taking in the 6 PM theater drives back in the dark. And from Monday on, the same page has nothing left for kids — the packed week ends today.

The Other Sunday: Market at Eight, Two Fairs at Eleven [3]

Alongside the Sesc program, Londrina's city agenda runs four free events, all family-friendly. The Parada do Limoeiro opens from 8 AM on Estrada do Limoeiro: a producers' market with food stalls and live music — the classic Sunday morning outing, though for us the timing only works with a very early start.

The other two fit better into our day, because they run from 11 AM and both take place in shopping malls — meaning air conditioning, restrooms, a food court, and parking, which with a one-month-old baby and a two-year-old is really the whole point. Londrina Criativa at Boulevard Shopping is a crafts and design fair; HipHopé Vermelho at Londrina Norte Shopping is an urban fair with DJ sets and battles, with extra competitions on Sundays — loud, but exactly right for an eight-year-old. In the same mall, a pet adoption day runs from noon to 6 PM; free as a program item, though driving home with a dog is a separate discussion.

Health: Vaccination Saturday, and a Dengue Figure With No Update [5][6]

The Multivacinação 2026 campaign is already underway and runs through September 1; the national Dia D is Saturday, 08/22. The goal is to update „a caderneta de crianças e adolescentes menores de 15 anos" — the vaccination record for everyone under 15. For us, that means three kids' records and, depending on the calendar, the first entry for the baby. A Saturday appointment at the health post costs nothing and skips the weekday wait.

Epidemiological Week (SE): Health authorities don't count cases by calendar month, but in numbered weeks — „SE 14" is the fourteenth week of the year. Think of a tally on the fridge where you don't write „in March" but „week fourteen": that's the only way to line up two years exactly. That's why every health figure here carries an SE number — without it, nobody knows what period it means.

On dengue, the situation stays structurally calm: for SE 1–14/2026 — January through March 14 — SESA Paraná reports 2,028 confirmed cases and one death, against 52,193 cases and 90 deaths in the same window in 2025. That's −96.1%.

This figure is five months old A newer bulletin exists: the official list ranks Informe Epidemiológico No. 24 from 08/04/2026 at the top. Its case figures, though, weren't accessible today — several entries on the SESA dengue page respond with „Conteúdo indisponível devido ao período eleitoral," and no direct PDF link could be found. We're therefore still citing the SE 1–14/2026 figure and explicitly not extrapolating it. Practically, that changes nothing about the house rule: tip out standing water once a week, especially now that four dry days let the watering cans and plant saucers fill up.

It's Sunday, August sixteenth, and today has a rare combination going for it: zero percent chance of rain, thirty degrees Celsius, and in Londrina, ten events that all happen to be free.

The biggest change from yesterday is on the schedule page for Sesc Cadeião in Londrina. Yesterday it listed three events for today; today it lists six. And the three new additions happen to be exactly the ones that matter for kids: at four in the afternoon, a craft workshop where children make their own mini books; at half past four, a literary talk; and at six in the evening, a play by Cia. TPK. All free, all indoors, all on the last day of the forty-fifth Semana Literária.

At four o'clock, two things happen at once, and both work for us. The craft workshop is a perfect fit for the seven- and eight-year-old, and the two-year-old can join the mess without needing to sit still. Next door, Nina Brondi tells the story of Pedro Malasartes, Brazil's answer to Till Eulenspiegel, a character the school kids already know from class.

Then there's the city's own lineup, published by the chamber of commerce, ACIL. Starting at eight in the morning, a producers' market runs on Estrada do Limoeiro, with food and live music. From eleven on, there are two fairs, both inside shopping malls: a crafts fair at Boulevard Shopping and a hip-hop fair with DJ sets at Norte Shopping. A shopping mall doesn't sound exciting, but with a one-month-old baby, it's actually the whole appeal — air conditioning, restrooms, a food court, parking. That same mall also hosts a pet adoption day from noon to six.

On weather, three separate queries agreed today. A newer bulletin from August fourth exists, but its case numbers weren't accessible today — several pages on the health department's dengue site are blocked with a note about the election period.
