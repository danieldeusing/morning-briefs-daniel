BR-153: Seven Detours Between Us and EFAPI [2]

This is today's most practically important story, because starting tomorrow it affects every drive south. The concession holder EPR Litoral Pioneiro has put three more construction detours into operation on the BR-153: km 18 in Jacarezinho („próximo à JBS"), km 24 also in Jacarezinho („no acesso à PR-431, em direção a Cambará"), and km 45 in Santo Antônio da Platina („próximo às empresas JL Diesel e Serraria Montanheiro"). The report is dated 08/12/2026. Per the same source, the detours at km 17, 23, 39, and 41 have already been in place since late July.

Added together, that's seven detour points on the stretch between Jacarezinho and Santo Antônio da Platina — exactly on the route to the EFAPI fairgrounds and on toward Londrina. The reason isn't damage, it's progress: the detours are needed to enable „a construção segura dos novos viadutos" — the safe construction of the new viaducts that are part of the BR-153 duplication.

BR-153 duplication→new viaducts→lanes shift onto temporary lanes→7 bottlenecks
What This Means for Our Drives The official instruction to drivers is to „redobrem a atenção ao passarem pelos pontos em obras, reduzam a velocidade e respeitem a sinalização" — pay extra attention through the work zones, slow down, and follow the signage. In practice: build in extra time — seven lane shifts over barely 30 km cost minutes, and on festival evenings the return traffic backs up right there. It's worse after dark: temporary routing is harder to read at night, and the EFAPI shows end late. Driving with kids is much less stressful during the day than at midnight. Concession emergency line: 0800 277 0153.

This is explicitly not a closure — the road stays passable, just with lane shifts and speed limits. The source doesn't say how long the detours will stay in place; with viaduct construction underway, it's safer to plan for the rest of the year.

Weather: The Cold Front Is Coming, the Rain Isn't [1]

Update since yesterday This is the most pleasant correction in weeks. Yesterday's issue flagged an open contradiction: Climatempo called for Sunday, 08/23, rain of 21.0 mm at a 40% chance, while theweather.com had 0% for the same day. This morning, Climatempo lists every day from today through Sunday for Ribeirão Claro at 0.0 mm and 0% — the contradiction has resolved in favor of the dry scenario, and both portals now agree.

Otherwise, the week's arc stays split in two, as forecast. Today 19–31°C, „Sol o dia todo sem nuvens no céu," tomorrow 17–31°C with early fog, Thursday the peak at 18–32°C. Then the break: Friday 15–23°C. The commercial portal theweather.com confirms the plunge for Jacarezinho from a second source — from 91°F (≈33°C) on Thursday to 73°F (≈23°C) on Friday, with lows around 54°F (≈12°C).

More interesting than Friday is what comes after: Saturday 12–24°C, Sunday 12–25°C — both sunny and dry. So not cold and wet, but the classic dry northern-Paraná winter: crisp mornings, mild afternoons. For festival and outing weekends, that's actually the best weather of the year. What to bring: layers. There's a twelve-degree spread between 7 AM and 3 PM, and the two-year-old is just as reliably cold at eight as he's sweaty by three.

It Doesn't Turn Wet Until Monday The new week's first rain is due Monday, 08/24: 0.4 mm at a 38% chance, along with the coolest day at just 13–18°C. Anyone with standing water in the yard — saucers, watering cans, tarps — has four dry days until then to tip it over. That's the cheapest dengue prevention there is.

The 54th EFAPI Expo opens tomorrow at the Parque de Exposições in Santo Antônio da Platina and runs through Sunday: exposição agropecuária, rodeio, feira comercial, praça de alimentação, with one evening show each day — Eduardo Costa, Zé Neto & Cristiano, Alok, Pedro Paulo & Alex, Felipe & Falcão. Weekdays stay hot at 31–32°C, dusty, and tough on toddlers — the cool-down starting Friday is a real win, not a loss, for a fairground full of animals, dirt, and crowds.

Against: Admission prices are still not published, half a year after the announcement — you'd go without knowing what the evening costs. Seven construction detours on the way in. Londrina on Sunday: A Kids' Program, at Last [4]

The search for a dated kids' event ran into a dead end for a long time this run — the „Agenda Cultural" pages circulating for Londrina were all from February and June. A direct look at the Sesc Cadeião program then turned up two solid Sunday dates. At 3 PM there's an „Experimentação sensorial para crianças," listed by Sesc itself as Infantil and marked Gratuito. Sensory experimentation is the rare format with no lower age limit: it requires no reading, no sitting still, and no knowledge of rules, so it works for a two-year-old just as well as for a school-age kid, just on different levels. Two hours later, at 5 PM, the same venue hosts the concert „Yakã: Narrativas Instrumentais" by the Tramandaí Trio, also free.

This chaining is exactly why it became the Weekend Pick. On its own, neither date justifies a 220-kilometer round trip; together they add up to a full afternoon with a clear structure and zero admission. If you still have time: the exhibit on Japanese immigration hangs there through October 25, also free, and the Contação de Histórias at the library runs through 08/30.

To be honest: Sesc publishes no age ratings. The assessment that the workshop works for the two-year-old is ours, and it's based on the format, not on anything stated by the organizer.

It's Tuesday, August eighteenth, and two things have changed since yesterday — both affecting the same drive.

First, the road. On Federal Highway one-fifty-three, between Jacarezinho and Santo Antônio da Platina, three more construction detours have opened. They're at kilometer eighteen and twenty-four in Jacarezinho, and kilometer forty-five in Santo Antônio da Platina. Four more have been there since late July, at kilometers seventeen, twenty-three, thirty-nine, and forty-one. Together, that's seven bottlenecks over barely thirty kilometers.

This isn't a closure, it's progress: crews are building new viaducts for the highway's duplication. The concession is asking drivers to slow down and follow the signage. In practical terms for us: build in extra time, and drive during the day rather than late at night, since the temporary routing is harder to read in the dark. This exact stretch is the route to EFAPI and on to Londrina.

Second, some good weather news. Yesterday there was a contradiction here: one portal called for twenty-one millimeters of rain on Sunday, the other said zero percent. This morning, Climatempo resolved it in favor of dry. From today through Sunday, every day in Ribeirão Claro now shows zero point zero millimeters and zero percent chance of rain.

The cold front is still coming, though. Today it's nineteen to thirty-one degrees, tomorrow similar, and Thursday is the warmest day at thirty-two. Friday, the high drops to twenty-three degrees — a nine-degree plunge in a single day, confirmed independently by the second weather portal. Over the weekend it's twelve to twenty-four degrees Saturday and twelve to twenty-five Sunday, both sunny. So not cold and wet, but the classic dry winter of northern Paraná. Bring layers — there's a twelve-degree swing between morning and afternoon.

Tomorrow the fifty-fourth EFAPI Expo opens in Santo Antônio da Platina and runs through Sunday. Fifty-one kilometers, forty-eight minutes away. Livestock show, rodeo, trade fair, food court, and a concert every evening. Thanks to the new forecast, Saturday afternoon goes from a compromise to a genuinely good choice: twenty-four degrees, sunny, no rain. Admission prices, though, are still not published, half a year after the announcement.

Our pick for the weekend, though, is Londrina. Sesc doesn't publish age ratings, though — that assessment is ours.

Saturday is also the national vaccination-record day, Dia D of Multivacinação, for everyone under fifteen. It's a normal school week, with no break window in August. And for our youngest, what we said here yesterday still holds: Paraná was under respiratory alert in the latest bulletin, so packed indoor spaces are still best avoided.
