EFAPI Opens Today — the Good Festival Weather Doesn't Arrive Until Friday

Tonight the 54th EFAPI Expo opens in Santo Antônio da Platina, the region's biggest event, 51 km from here. And the obvious question — when do we go? — has an unusually clear answer this week: not today. Today and tomorrow bring 32°C to a dusty fairground, on Friday a cold front knocks that down by nine degrees, and the weekend settles at 24°C, cloudless, zero rain — both weather portals agree on that. From the perspective of four kids, Saturday afternoon is by far the best slot of the whole week.

EFAPI: Five Days, One Good Slot [2][5]

The 54th EFAPI Expo — the Exposição-Feira, Agropecuária e Industrial of Santo Antônio da Platina — opens today and runs through Sunday. The program is the classic one: exposição agropecuária, rodeio, feira comercial, praça de alimentação, plus one evening show each night (today Eduardo Costa, then Zé Neto & Cristiano, Alok, Pedro Paulo & Alex, closing with Felipe & Falcão). For us, the distance is what matters: 51 km, roughly 48 minutes. That's day-trip range — head over in the afternoon, home by evening.

The honest tally after today's research: the festival itself is well documented, its fine print isn't. Six months after the program announcement, admission prices still aren't published — neither for the grounds nor for the evening shows. Two npdiario articles with detailed programming (among them an appearance by Palhaço Furunfo, of interest to the kids, and rodeo times on the final day starting at 10:30 am) were only visible as search hits today; the site itself rejected direct access with HTTP 403. These details are therefore flagged here explicitly as "reported, not read by us."

For: The region's biggest event, reachable in 48 minutes, five days to choose from instead of a single date. The livestock show and food court work during the day with no set time. From Friday on, a pleasant 23–24°C instead of today's 32°C.
Against: Prices unknown. No source documents a changing area, a nursing space, or parking. Seven construction detours on the way in. And police reported a weapons-and-drugs seizure at the EFAPI's cavalgada on 08/17 — no reason to stay away, but a reason to skip the late evening hours with kids.

Weather: Two Hot Days, Then Nine Degrees Less [1]

The week splits cleanly in two, and both portals tell the same story. Today Climatempo lists 18–32°C for Ribeirão Claro, „Dia ensolarado, com nevoeiro ao amanhecer," tomorrow the same temperature band. Then the break: Friday 15–23°C. Nine degrees in a single day. After that comes the classic dry winter of northern Paraná — Saturday 12–24°C („Sol o dia todo sem nuvens no céu"), Sunday 13–24°C. Rain: on none of these days, 0.0 mm across the board.

The second portal, theweather.com for Jacarezinho, independently confirms the trend and the dry spell: today 88°F/63°F (≈31/17°C) at a 0% chance of rain, weekend highs of 76–78°F (≈24–26°C) with lows around 52–54°F (≈11–12°C). The two sources differ by 1–2°C on the highs — that's normal model spread between two locations, not a contradiction, and the publication rule "two portals must agree" is satisfied.

What This Means in Practice Layer up. From Friday on, there's roughly a twelve-degree spread between seven in the morning and three in the afternoon. The two-year-old is as reliably cold at eight as he's sweaty by three. And today and tomorrow, the opposite applies: 32°C plus a fairground is the combination that wears a toddler out within the hour.

Health: The Alert Stays, the Dengue Picture Stays Dark [4][6]

SRAG: Brazil's umbrella category for severe acute respiratory illness — anything that lands someone in the hospital because of breathing trouble, whether flu, RSV, or COVID. Like a smoke-detector statistic: it doesn't say exactly what's burning, but it's very precise about how often. RSV: a cold virus that gives adults the sniffles and swells shut the fine bronchi in infants — hence its own age-group statistic.

The current InfoGripe bulletin from Fiocruz for SE 30/2026 (07/26–08/01) counts 130,054 SRAG cases nationally in the current epi-year, of which 69,895 (53.7%) are lab-confirmed. The national trend points downward — but 14 states, Paraná among them, remain in the alert or risk zone. By age group: RSV hospital admissions among children under four are declining, influenza A and B among children under two remain elevated. For a household with a one-month-old, that's the sentence that matters — the curve points the right way, but the age group it hits is ours.

Dengue: no new figure, for the third time running. The country's official bulletin list now shows Informe Epidemiológico No. 25 from 08/18/2026 as the most recent entry — its case figures aren't accessible via the list page. The only solid data therefore remains the SE 1–14/2026 count: 2,028 confirmed cases against 52,193 in the same window in 2025 (−96.1%), and 1 death instead of 90. Those figures are four months old and describe the summer season, not August. We're tracking them as a structural trend, not this week's numbers, and extrapolating nothing.

Road: BR-153, as of 08/12 On the stretch between Jacarezinho and Santo Antônio da Platina — exactly on the way to EFAPI — seven construction detours are active (km 17, 18, 23, 24, 39, 41, 45), needed for the new viaducts of the highway's duplication. The report is dated 08/12; the source's homepage was checked again today, nothing newer. It's not a closure — but build in extra time, slow down, and better to head back while it's still light. Concession emergency line: 0800 277 0153.

It's Wednesday, August nineteenth. Tonight the fifty-fourth EFAPI Expo opens in Santo Antônio da Platina, the region's biggest event, fifty-one kilometers from here. So the question isn't whether we go, it's when. And this week the weather gives an unusually clear answer.

Today and tomorrow bring highs of thirty-two degrees. A dusty fairground at thirty-two degrees is a rough call for a two-year-old. Then on Friday a cold front knocks the high down to twenty-three degrees — nine degrees lower in a single day. Saturday brings twelve to twenty-four degrees under a cloudless sky, Sunday thirteen to twenty-four. No rain is forecast on any of these days, zero point zero millimeters across the board, and a second weather portal confirms that independently with a zero percent chance of rain.

So our pick for the weekend is Saturday afternoon at EFAPI. The livestock show, trade fair, and food court all work during the day with no set time, and at twenty-four degrees it's a good afternoon instead of a lot to ask. We're going in the afternoon on purpose, not the evening: the shows run late, and the drive back crosses seven construction detours on Federal Highway one-fifty-three, which are much harder to read in the dark.

What's honestly still missing at EFAPI is the pricing. Six months after the program was announced, admission and the evening shows still have no published cost anywhere. There's also no documented changing area, nursing space, or parking. And police reported a weapons and drugs seizure at the EFAPI's opening cavalgada on Monday — not a reason to stay away, but a reason to skip the late evening hours with the kids.

For Sunday, the alternative is Londrina. Sesc Cadeião has a sensory workshop for kids at three in the afternoon and the concert Yakã by the Tramandaí Trio at five, both free. We cross-checked that directly on the program page today, and it stands unchanged. There's no need to choose between the two days: fifty-one kilometers on Saturday, one hundred ten on Sunday, both dry.

On health, the respiratory alert is still in effect. The latest Fiocruz bulletin for epidemiological week thirty counts one hundred thirty thousand severe respiratory cases nationwide this year, just over half of them lab-confirmed. The trend is downward, but fourteen states remain in the alert zone, Paraná among them. In children under two, flu remains elevated. For our youngest, who's one month old, that simply means: no festival crowds, even in nice weather.

On dengue, we're still in the dark for the third time running. The newest state bulletin, number twenty-five from August eighteenth, exists, but its case figures aren't accessible through the overview page. The only solid number is from the spring: just over two thousand confirmed cases against fifty-two thousand in the same period last year. That's four months old and describes the summer, not August. We're tracking it as a trend, not the current picture.

The school week runs normally, with no break window in August. Saturday is the national vaccination-record day of the Multivacinação campaign, for everyone under fifteen. The next break doesn't come until October, when Teachers' Day gets attached to the holiday on the twelfth.

São Paulo would offer four family-friendly options this weekend: a food festival at Villa-Lobos Park with its own kids' program, a lantern festival at the Botanical Garden, a monster-truck show, and Cirque du Soleil, also at Villa-Lobos Park.
