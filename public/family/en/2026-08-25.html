BR-153: "Stop and Go" Now Has Kilometer Markers [3]

Getting there On the BR-153, as part of the duplication project, three additional detours have been running since 2026-08-12: km 18 (near Jacarezinho, at JBS — traffic on the service-road lane in both directions), km 24 (service-road lane toward Santo Antônio da Platina), and km 45 near Santo Antônio da Platina. Already active since late July: km 17, 23, 39, and 41. The concessionaire asks drivers to reduce speed and pay extra attention; emergency number 0800 277 0153.

This brief previously covered the construction as a general "Stop and Go" note from DER. Now it has numbers — and they cover exactly the corridor this family drives toward Platina and toward Londrina. Seven detour points across roughly 45 kilometers mean, in practice: build in buffer time, don't cut it to the minute, and plan the weekend return drive for while it's still light.

Service-road lane: Alongside many Brazilian highways runs a narrow parallel road for local residents, tractors, and local traffic — like the side lane next to a country road that's normally only used by the farmer heading to his field. During a duplication project, all through-traffic is temporarily rerouted onto this side lane while the main roadway gets widened. The result is a narrower lane, tighter curves, and oncoming traffic in places where there normally isn't any.
