What "82% rain" actually means — and what it doesn't: The figure is the probability that measurable rain falls at all — at this location, on this day — at least 0.1mm, sometime between midnight and midnight. It does not mean it rains 82% of the day, and it doesn't mean 82% of the region gets wet. Think of it as a bag of a hundred marbles: 82 of them are blue. The model reaches in once — with 82 blue ones, missing is already quite lucky. In practical terms for Saturday: there could still be a morning window to get outside. What the figure rules out is a day outdoors you can actually plan around — and that's exactly why the right response is to move the outdoor program up to Friday, instead of waiting for a window on Saturday.

Dengue: Now Confirmed — With Figures for Our Region for the First Time [6]

For two issues, the dengue figures here carried the note "plausible, not verified." As of today, that's resolved: the Sesa Paraná Informe Epidemiológico No. 26 from Sep 1, 2026 was opened directly as a PDF and read. It covers Epidemiological Weeks 01/2026 through 34/2026, with the SINAN database as of Aug 25, 2026. Statewide: 5,962 confirmed cases and 6 deaths, against 89,829 cases and 141 deaths in the same period of 2025 — −93.4% and −95.7%.

What's new — and what actually matters to us — is the breakdown by health region. Ribeirão Claro belongs to the 19th Regional de Saúde, Jacarezinho, and it looks about as good as one could hope: 108 confirmed cases among 300,313 residents, zero deaths, circulating serotype DENV-2. But the two regions this family regularly drives to look different.

Why "incidence per 100,000" instead of the raw case count: Cornélio Procópio has 514 cases and Londrina has 1,637 — by the raw number, Londrina would look like the more dangerous place. It isn't, though, because it also has roughly three times as many residents. Incidence factors that out: it asks "how many cases would there be if everywhere had the same number of people?" — here normalized to 100,000 residents. It's like comparing two school classes: five sick kids in a class of ten is a very different thing from five sick kids in a class of fifty. Read that way, the picture flips: Cornélio Procópio, at 232.98, has roughly eight times the infection density of our own region (29.64); Londrina, at 128.24, has about four times. It's calm at home — not on the way to run errands.
Heads up Wednesday remains the yard-round day, and this week the timing lines up especially well. Aedes eggs stick dry to the inner walls of saucers, buckets, gutters, and old tires and survive there for months. They only hatch once water floods over them — after that it takes seven to ten days to a flying mosquito. Last week's rain days filled every container on the property at the same time, and Wednesday falls cleanly inside that window and is dry: empty, flip over, tighten tarps, clear gutters. And one point from the regional table belongs here too: the good situation at home is no protection while traveling. Anyone driving to Cornélio Procópio or Londrina is driving into eight times or four times the infection density respectively — repellent belongs in the bag for those trips, even if it isn't needed at home.

Weekdays are our own calendar calculation

It's Tuesday, September fifteenth. Yesterday's headline was that the week turns dry. Today, for the first time, both weather portals reach far enough out to show what's waiting behind that dry block — and it's rain.

Saturday, climatempo has it at eighty-two percent; tempo.com says eighty. Sunday, eighty-one and ninety. Two independent sources, four numbers, every one of them above eighty. That changes what Wednesday through Friday actually means. This isn't the start of a nice stretch — it's this week's entire outdoor budget.

Today itself is the transition day, and it's wetter than expected. Fourteen to twenty degrees, seventy-one percent, one and a half millimeters. Showers in the morning, clearing by evening. What stands out is that tempo.com had today at just thirty percent yesterday and jumped to seventy overnight — and of all days, that's the one where the two portals now agree.

The dry block in between is even better than yesterday. Climatempo now has Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday all at zero percent — Wednesday was at forty percent as recently as yesterday. Temperatures dip and climb: twenty-four on Wednesday, twenty-one on Thursday, twenty-five on Friday. That makes Friday afternoon the pick of the week — the warmest day, and the one temperature both portals agree on to the degree.

One conflict remains. Both portals agree on Saturday's rain, but not its temperature: twenty-one degrees against twenty-eight, a seven-degree gap that's too wide to call rounding. So this brief carries no Saturday temperature — only the rain. Simepar, normally the first call for Paraná weather, has been blocking access for four days running.

The second headline today comes from the dengue bulletin. For two issues, the numbers here carried a caveat: plausible, but not verified. That's resolved now — Informe number twenty-six was opened directly as a PDF today. For weeks one through thirty-four, Paraná reports five thousand nine hundred sixty-two confirmed cases and six deaths, against just under ninety thousand cases and a hundred forty-one deaths in the same span last year — a decline of ninety-three point four percent.

What's genuinely new is the breakdown by health region, and for the first time it includes ours. Our region, Jacarezinho, sits at a hundred eight cases, zero deaths, and an incidence of twenty-nine point six per hundred thousand — one of the calmest numbers in the state. But the two places this family regularly drives to look very different: Cornélio Procópio is at two hundred thirty-two, roughly eight times our rate; Londrina is at one hundred twenty-eight, about four times. It's quiet at home — not on the way to run errands. Repellent belongs in the bag for those trips. Wednesday morning is still the yard round: empty saucers, flip buckets, clear gutters — seven to ten days after last week's rain and squarely inside the hatching window.

On the event front, the core ring stays a confirmed negative finding for the ninth day running — meaning nothing found, not nothing there. Local sites keep returning blank page scaffolding to the search. With over eighty percent rain on both weekend days, anything without a roof is off anyway. The one thing that survives is indoors: free live music Sunday evening at seven at a shopping mall in Londrina — though that's only confirmed through an event aggregator, not double-checked.

School stays quiet this week, and the October bridge hasn't moved: Monday, October twelfth, is a confirmed holiday worth three days off in a row. Whether a fourth joins it is still unconfirmed.
