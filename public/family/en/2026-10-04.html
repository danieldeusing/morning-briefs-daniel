Both portals agree for the next three days. climatempo puts rain chance in Jacarezinho today at 94 %, Monday at 97 %, Tuesday at 95 %; SIMEPAR has 98 % on all three days.[1][2] Today climatempo writes "Nublado com chuva forte durante o dia. À noite, chuva diminui de intensidade, mas não para" — heavy rain by day, weaker by evening, but without a break. Tuesday brings the most: 32.4 mm by climatempo, 44.0 mm by SIMEPAR. Together that adds up to roughly 68 to 75 mm by Tuesday evening.

What does 44 millimeters mean? One millimeter of rain is one liter of water on every square meter. So 44 mm is 44 liters per square meter — like dumping more than four full ten-liter buckets over every spot in the yard. The ground is already wet from Sunday and Monday and absorbs less. That's why puddles and ditches fill up fast on Tuesday.

Wednesday's the gap — then uncertainty. SIMEPAR sees 0 % and not a drop on Wednesday, climatempo 58 % with only 3.4 mm and "tempo firme" by evening.[1][2] For Thursday the portals split, see below. climatempo has Thursday and Friday running up to 32 °C — humid, with showers.

Anyone with outdoor plans on Thursday should check again Wednesday evening. As of now, only Wednesday is reliably dry.

The long weekend 10/10–10/12 — worth planning ahead now [6][7]

Children's Day falls on a Monday this year. October 12 is a nationwide holiday for Our Lady of Aparecida, and Dia das Crianças falls on the same day. It's the only national holiday in October 2026.[6] For the family that means Saturday the 10th through Monday the 12th off in a row.

No school the Tuesday after? Open. Press reports mention an October recess starting October 13. But one of the sources describes the calendar for Mato Grosso do Sul, not Paraná, and the resolution itself wasn't readable. So this stands as unconfirmed. Elementary schools and daycares are mostly municipal or private anyway and run their own calendars. Worth asking in the school group this week — if the 13th is off too, that's four days.

Health & On the Road [8]

Dengue stays low. Paraná counts 6,120 confirmed cases and six deaths in epidemiological weeks EW 01–38/2026, per the Sesa bulletin dated 09/29/2026, as reported by hojepr.com.[8] The Londrina region leads the state with 1,631 cases; Cornélio Procópio ranks fifth with 523. The figure rests on this one source, and no newer bulletin is out yet. October comes before the real dengue season, which runs roughly December through May.

Why rain now means mosquitoes in November: the Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of standing water — a saucer under a plant pot is enough. The eggs survive dry for months. When rain comes, larvae hatch, and after about a week new mosquitoes fly. Dumping that water out beforehand breaks the cycle.
Appointment: Wednesday, 10/07 On the dry day, a walkthrough of the yard and garden — empty saucers, flip over buckets and toys, tighten tarps, check the gutter. The school-age kids can treat it as a scavenger hunt. For the baby: a mosquito net over the crib and stroller; insect repellent for infants only after checking with a doctor.

On the road: with 30 to 44 mm on Tuesday, dirt roads and low spots flood fast. No dated closure turned up today for BR-153, BR-369, or PR-092. Anyone driving to Londrina should still build in a buffer if it's raining.
