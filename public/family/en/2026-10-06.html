The long weekend of Oct 10–12 [3][4]

Children's Day falls on a Monday this year. On 10/12, it's a national holiday for Our Lady of Aparecida, Brazil's patron saint, and Dia das Crianças falls on the same day. "O feriado de 12 de outubro cairá em uma segunda-feira, garantindo um fim de semana prolongado de três dias," writes todamateria.[3] The holiday lists for Ribeirão Claro and Jacarezinho show the same day. So: Saturday the 10th through Monday the 12th off in one stretch.

And Tuesday the 13th? Unconfirmed. Several secondary sources report that Paraná's education authority (Seed-PR) moved the October recess for state schools to the 13th, per Resolução 6.494/2025.[4] That would make it four days off. We could not open the text of the resolution itself. On top of that, elementary schools and daycares are often municipal or private and run their own calendars. The reliable answer is in the school group chat or the school office.

What favors a trip
Three days off in a row. After a wet week, everyone wants to get out, and many cities put together a Children's Day program on short notice.
What's against it
No weather forecast yet for 10/10–12. Holiday traffic, crowded parks and inns. With an infant, long drives make for a very full day.

Our suggestion for planning: don't book anything big yet. Starting Thursday, there will be a weather forecast for the weekend, and the local programs should surface by then too. A nearby plan — Londrina or a local event in the region — fits better with a baby and a two-year-old than four hours on the highway. Worth noting: 10/28 (Dia do Servidor Público) also appears on the region's holiday lists, but only as an optional day off.[3]

Health & Safety [6]

RSV — last verified data: September In the most recent InfoGripe bulletin we found, Epidemiological Week 34/2026 (Aug 23–29, published Sep 3), RSV circulation is still elevated in seven states — Bahia, Minas Gerais, Mato Grosso do Sul, Santa Catarina, Sergipe, Rio Grande do Sul, and Paraná.[6] That's about five weeks old, not a current alarm, but newer than previously stated.
What is RSV, and why does it matter for the baby? RSV is a cold virus. In older kids and adults, it usually just causes a runny nose. But an infant has very narrow airways — like a thin straw that clogs quickly. That's why RSV can lead to breathing trouble in babies. The main season in southern Brazil is winter, so May through August; now in spring it usually tapers off.

What helps regardless: wash hands when the older kids come home from school. Visitors with a cold should keep their distance from the baby. Fast breathing, rib retractions, or poor feeding in the infant are reasons to see a doctor right away.

After the rain: a lot of rain means standing water soon. If Thursday is dry enough, empty plant saucers, buckets, and toys in the yard once — that removes breeding spots for the dengue mosquito. No current dengue bulletin for Paraná was available today.
