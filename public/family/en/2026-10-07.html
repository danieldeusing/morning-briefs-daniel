Health & Safety [3]

Sun extreme, mosquitoes high climatempo reports a UV index of "extreme" and a high mosquito risk for Ribeirão Claro today.[3] Humid days with warmth up to 31 °C are exactly the weather in which the dengue mosquito breeds.
Why rain plus warmth makes mosquitoes happy The dengue mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of small pools of water — a flowerpot saucer is enough. When it's warm, larvae hatch quickly, and soon new mosquitoes are flying. Emptying the water beforehand breaks this cycle before it starts.

What helps:

  • On dry Thursday, empty saucers, buckets, and toys in the yard once.
  • For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller. Whether mosquito repellent is allowed at this age is on the packaging, or ask the pediatrician.
  • For the older kids on Thursday, sunscreen and a hat, and avoid the midday sun.

Before longer drives over the holiday, it's worth checking official severe-weather warnings at alertas2.inmet.gov.br, especially for the thunderstorm night into Tuesday.
