Health & Safety [8][9]

Heat, then rain: mosquito weather Two days up to 32 °C followed by a week of showers — exactly the weather in which the dengue mosquito breeds. In the 2025 season, the Jacarezinho health region was among the five hardest-hit regions in Paraná.[9]
Why the dry day before the rain matters The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the waterline, for example in a flowerpot saucer. The eggs can wait dry for a long time. When rain comes, larvae hatch within a few days in the warmth. If you empty and scrub the containers today, on the dry day, you take away the eggs' bath before it rains.

What helps:

  • Today, empty and turn over saucers, buckets, kiddie pools and toys in the yard.
  • For the baby, a mosquito net over the bed and stroller. The package or the pediatrician says whether a mosquito spray is allowed at this age.
  • Friday at 32 °C: drink plenty, avoid the midday sun, never leave the baby in a hot car.
