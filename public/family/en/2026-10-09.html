El Niño, explained simply Every few years the sea in the Pacific off South America gets much warmer than normal. That shifts the big air currents over the whole continent. For southern Brazil this often means: more and heavier rain in spring. For us: probably more wet days than usual in October.

Three days off, and an open Tuesday [5]

Children's Day falls on a Monday. "O feriado de Nossa Senhora Aparecida, celebrado em 12 de outubro, cairá numa segunda-feira em 2026" — the holiday of Brazil's patron saint falls on a Monday in 2026.[5] On the same day Brazil celebrates Dia das Crianças. So the time off runs from Saturday Oct 10 to Monday Oct 12. Banks, schools and offices are usually closed on Monday.[5]

Tuesday Oct 13 stays open. Whether schools make it a bridge day is not confirmed by any source. Elementary school and daycare often have their own calendars. The school group can give the safe answer.

What speaks for a plan at home
From Sunday it rains according to all sources. Short distances spare the baby. A self-planned party is often more fun for the big kids than a crowded mall. And it costs almost nothing.
What speaks against it
Three days indoors are long with four kids. So make full use of Saturday morning. For a rainy day the mall in Ourinhos or Londrina is the best fallback — check the program first.

Our advice: Use the last sun this afternoon for water play and the garden, go out Saturday morning, celebrate indoors Sunday and Monday. Next week the free movies at Sesc beckon.

Health & safety [10][11]

Heat, then a rainy week: mosquito weather Up to 35 °C today, then rain every day through Oct 16 — exactly in this swing the dengue mosquito breeds best. We found no official dengue warning for our region, but also no current figures (see below).
Why today is the important day The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the waterline, for example in a plant saucer. The eggs can wait dry for a long time. When rain comes, larvae hatch within a few days in the warmth. Whoever empties and scrubs the containers today, on the last dry day, takes away the eggs' bath.

What helps:

  • Today, empty, scrub and turn over saucers, buckets, wading pools and toys in the garden.
  • For the baby, a mosquito net over the bed and stroller. Whether a mosquito spray is allowed at this age is stated on the package or by the pediatrician.
  • At 33–35 °C today: keep the baby in the shade, nurse or give fluids often, never leave in the car. The big kids with a hat and water bottle.
  • With heavy rain next week: postpone trips during thunderstorms. Warnings are at alertas2.inmet.gov.br.

So there is no case count here — we do not extrapolate from old data.
