What do “87%” and “2 mm” mean? The percentage says how sure the model is that it rains at all. The millimeters say how much. 2 mm is a short shower that barely makes a puddle. 65 mm is like pouring more than six buckets of water onto every square meter.

Health & Safety [6][7]

Rainy week: mosquito weather Warmth up to 28–29 °C and rain on many days through Thursday — this mix is a good breeding ground for the dengue mosquito. We found no official dengue warning for our region, but also no current figures (see below).
Why emptying helps The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the waterline, for example in a plant saucer. The eggs wait a long time while dry. When rain comes, larvae hatch within a few days in warm weather. Anyone who empties and scrubs the containers takes away the eggs' bath.
  • Today, empty, scrub and turn over saucers, buckets, wading pools and toys in the yard.
  • For the baby, a mosquito net over the bed and stroller. Whether mosquito spray is allowed at this age is answered by the label or the pediatrician.
  • With gusts up to 50 km/h on Monday: secure loose garden furniture and toys.[1]
  • With heavy rain on Thursday: postpone trips during thunderstorms. Warnings are at alertas2.inmet.gov.br.
