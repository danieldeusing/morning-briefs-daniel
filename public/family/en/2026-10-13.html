Health & Safety [1][5][10]

Storm with a baby: prepare by Tuesday evening For Wednesday INMET expects gusts of 60–100 km/h and hail.[1]
  • Set out flashlights, power bank and drinking water. Plan milk or bottles so that a few hours without power are fine.
  • Secure the trampoline, wading pool and patio furniture.
  • Stay indoors during thunderstorms, do not drive through flooded streets.
  • After every rainy day, empty saucers, buckets and toys in the yard — that is where the dengue mosquito breeds.

Respiratory: According to the state health department Sesa, Paraná fell from “Alerta” to “Segurança”, the lowest level, in the InfoGripe bulletin of Sep 10, 2026.[5] The report names no epidemiological week, and we found no newer bulletin. So we give no case numbers.

Vaccination protects the baby too The flu shot is available in Paraná from 6 months at every vaccination point.[5] The baby is still too young. If parents and siblings are vaccinated, it is less likely that anyone brings the virus home.
What is a “circle of protection”? A very small baby cannot yet be vaccinated against some diseases. So you vaccinate the people around the baby. Then it is like a fence: the virus does not even reach the baby.

Dengue: We could not read a current dengue bulletin for Paraná today either. So no case number appears here.

Road: On the BR-153 near Jacarezinho, viaduct construction at km 17 and km 18 is under way with detours.[10] The report carries no date. On trips toward Santo Antônio da Platina, allow a bit more time.

It's Tuesday, October thirteenth. Children's Day is over, but the rain sticks around through Friday.

The weather service INMET has Ribeirão Claro and Jacarezinho under a yellow warning today: up to fifty millimeters of rain, wind up to thirty-seven miles per hour, and possible hail. Tomorrow, Wednesday, it goes orange: fifty to one hundred millimeters of rain over the day, gusts up to sixty-two miles per hour, and hail. So set out flashlights and a power bank today, secure the trampoline, and put off any trips to Londrina on Wednesday.

The two weather portals agree on the rain, but not on the heat. For today, SIMEPAR says twenty-seven degrees Celsius, climatempo says twenty-four. Part of that is because they forecast for different towns.

The good news: from Saturday the seventeenth, both portals show no rain. Sixteen to twenty-three degrees, a nice day to get outside. Don't forget rain boots for the little one.

Until then, the kids' movies at Sesc Jacarezinho are the safe bet. On Wednesday and Thursday there are Trapalhões films at nine thirty and two thirty, free of charge. The Friday and Saturday showings are no longer on the page today, so check before you go.

For the weekend, we haven't yet found a verified kids' event nearby or in Londrina. We'll keep looking through Friday. Also open is whether there's school on Thursday, Teachers' Day. The school group will know best.
