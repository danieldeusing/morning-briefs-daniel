Health & Safety [1][6]

Storm with a baby The power often goes out during thunderstorms.
  • Have flashlights, a power bank and drinking water ready. Plan milk or bottles so a few hours without power are fine.
  • Stay indoors during thunderstorms, do not drive through flooded streets.
  • Better to postpone trips to Londrina or Ourinhos today.
  • After the rain, by Saturday at the latest: empty plant saucers, buckets and toys in the yard.
Why empty buckets? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles of clean water, for example in a plant saucer. After about a week a new mosquito flies out. If you pour the water out beforehand, the eggs come to nothing.
