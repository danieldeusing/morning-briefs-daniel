Health & Safety [1][6]

Rain with a baby Thunderstorms often knock out the power.
  • Keep flashlights, a power bank and drinking water ready. Plan milk or bottles so that a few hours without power are fine.
  • Stay indoors during thunderstorms, and do not drive through flooded streets.
  • Better to move longer trips to Londrina or Ourinhos to Sunday.
  • From Saturday: empty saucers, buckets, tarps and toys in the garden.
Why empty buckets? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles of clean water, for example in a saucer. After about a week, a new mosquito flies out of it. Whoever pours out the water beforehand stops this cycle. After several rainy days and before warm, dry days, right now is exactly the right moment.
