Sunny Saturday outdoors, Trapalhões at Sesc in the evening

Health & Safety [5][6]

Dengue: a quiet year From EW 01 to EW 40/2026, Paraná counts 6,191 confirmed dengue cases and 6 deaths.[5] In the same period of 2025 there were 90,723 cases and 142 deaths, so 93.2% fewer today. The bulletin of 2026-10-13 (Informe no. 29) lists 71 more cases than the previous bulletin (EW 01–38). Important: the figures are cumulative since January, not weekly numbers.

The figures for our regional office, the 19ª RS Jacarezinho, are in the bulletin. We could not assign the table columns with confidence, so we give no regional figure here.

Why empty the buckets anyway? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles of clean water, for example in a flower saucer. After about a week, a new mosquito flies out. Now four warm, dry days are coming, and from Wednesday a lot of rain again. If you empty everything over the weekend, you take away the mosquito's nursery before the next wave of rain fills it again. Few cases does not mean no mosquitoes.
  • Baby: mosquito net over the crib or stroller, especially in the morning and late afternoon. For a one-month-old infant, no insect repellent without the pediatrician's advice.
  • Vaccinations: The multi-vaccination campaign in Paraná has ended (771,994 doses per Sesa-PR, as of 2026-09-02).[6] The UBS vaccination rooms stay open all year. The baby's next appointments are in the vaccination card.
  • Flu/RSV: We did not find a current InfoGripe bulletin for October. The winter season is over, though.
