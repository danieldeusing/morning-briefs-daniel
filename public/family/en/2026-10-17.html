Health & Safety [6]

Dengue: a quiet year From EW 01 to EW 40/2026, Paraná counts 6,191 confirmed dengue cases and 6 deaths.[6] In the same period of 2025 there were 90,723 cases and 142 deaths, so 93.2% fewer cases. This is the bulletin of 2026-10-13 (Informe nº 29); since the previous bulletin (EW 38), 71 cases were added. Important: the figures are cumulative since January, not weekly numbers.
Why still empty the buckets? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles of clean water, for example in a flower-pot saucer. After about a week a new mosquito flies out of it. Now four warm, dry days are coming, with rain again from Wednesday. Anyone who empties everything today or tomorrow takes away the mosquito's nursery before the rain fills it again. Few cases does not mean no mosquitoes.
  • Sun and baby: The UV index is "very high" today. A one-month-old infant does not belong in direct sun. Shade, light long clothing and a hat help more than sunscreen, which is given to babies this small only on a pediatrician's advice.
  • Mosquitoes: Mosquito net over the crib or stroller, especially in the morning and late afternoon.
  • Flu/RSV: We found no current InfoGripe bulletin for October. According to press reports on the bulletin of 2026-09-10, Paraná is at the "Segurança" level, so no alert. We did not read the Fiocruz page itself. The winter season is over.
