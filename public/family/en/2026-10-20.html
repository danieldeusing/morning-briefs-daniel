Health & Safety [7][8]

No new numbers The latest Paraná dengue bulletin is still Informe nº 29 of 2026-10-13.[7] We found no newer one today. There is also no October bulletin with a calendar week for flu and RSV. On 2026-09-18, Sesa reported that Paraná dropped from the "Alerta" level to "Segurança" according to InfoGripe.[8] The winter season is therefore over.
Why rain wakes up the mosquitoes The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles of clean water, for example in a flower saucer or a toy in the yard. After about a week a new mosquito flies out of it. From Thursday it rains for four days. Turn over or bring under cover anything in the yard that can collect water, today or tomorrow. Then the rain does not fill a new nursery for the mosquito.
  • Baby and mosquitoes: mosquito net over the crib or stroller, especially after the rainy days.
  • Thunderstorms: SIMEPAR speaks of a higher thunderstorm risk from Friday.[4] If there is lightning, do not let the kids play in the yard. Better postpone trips on wet country roads.
