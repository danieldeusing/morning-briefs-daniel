Health & Safety [6]

No new figures The latest Paraná dengue bulletin is the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 29 - 13/10/2026".[6] We did not read the figures in it today. So we give no case count. Important for context: since 2025 Paraná counts dengue by calendar year, no longer by season. Search hits with figures from 2025 do not apply to this year.
Why the rain wakes up the mosquitoes The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles of clean water, for example in a flower saucer or a toy in the yard. After about a week a new mosquito flies out. From tomorrow it rains for four days. Anyone who turns over everything that can collect water today takes away the mosquito's nursery.
  • Baby and mosquitoes: mosquito net over the crib or stroller, especially in the week after the rain.
  • Thunderstorms and wet roads: if there is lightning, do not let the kids play in the yard. Better to postpone drives on Sunday.
