Where the sources disagree Whether it rains, both say the same: yes Thursday through Sunday, no Monday and Tuesday. On the amount, they differ. On Friday SIMEPAR reports 11.4 mm, climatempo 27.8 mm. On Sunday it is 58.1 mm (SIMEPAR) against 82.1 mm (climatempo). Yesterday the Sunday values were still 39.4 and 121.4 mm; so the two forecasts are moving closer together. SIMEPAR's 25 °C for today is a partial-day value; climatempo says 21 °C. That is why we always give both numbers.
Sunday Oct 25 Both sources see Sunday as the wettest day, with 58 to 82 mm. climatempo adds a heavy-rain note. We found no official warning from INMET, CEMADEN or Defesa Civil; we did not check INMET today. Better not to plan long drives on country roads on Sunday.
What does 58 millimeters mean? One millimeter of rain means one liter of water falls on every square meter. At 58 mm, 58 liters land on one square meter, about the size of a bath towel. That is almost six full mop buckets. At 82 mm it is over eight buckets. That is enough for overflowing gutters and slippery dirt roads.

For planning, this means: put big shopping trips on Monday or Tuesday. The two dry days are also the window for playground, garden and laundry.

Health & Safety [6]

No new numbers The latest dengue bulletin from Paraná is the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 29 - 13/10/2026".[6] We could not read the PDF file today. So we give no case count. For context: search hits with over 92,000 cases belong to 2025, not 2026. The last bulletins came about every two weeks (09/15, 09/29, 10/13).
Why mosquitoes come after the rain The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the water line in small containers: plant saucers, buckets, toys in the garden. When rain fills them, the larvae hatch. After about a week, a new mosquito flies. Anyone who empties and flips everything on the dry Monday takes away the mosquito's nursery.
  • Baby and mosquitoes: mosquito net over the crib and stroller, especially in the weeks after the rain.
  • Thunderstorms: when there is lightning, do not let the kids play in the garden. climatempo sees thunderstorms on Saturday night.
  • Wet roads: better to postpone drives on Sunday.
