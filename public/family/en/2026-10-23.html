Pick of the Week

Old treasures, coffee and history at the Clube

Where: Centro Turístico Recreativo Municipal (Clube), Ribeirão Claro (core ring, in town). Street not given. When: Mon Oct 26 to Thu Oct 29, 11 a.m.–5 p.m. and 6:30–9 p.m. Age: best for the 7- and 8-year-olds; the toddler and the baby in a carrier come along fine Price: unchecked (the report gives none)

The exhibition is called “Relíquias da Nossa Terra / Objetos antigos, café e história.”[4] Old objects, coffee and the history of our town: it is more exciting for school kids than it sounds. How did people roast, grind and brew coffee in the old days? Best to pick the dry Monday or Tuesday and drop by right after school. One hour is enough. Afterward, head to the playground, which will finally be dry again.

Pro: in town, short, indoors, falls on the two dry days, long opening hours including evenings.
Con: price, changing room and parking not confirmed; old objects and toddler hands need an eye on them.

Where the sources disagree Both say the same on whether it will rain. Not on the amount: on Saturday SIMEPAR expects 16.5 mm, climatempo 5.6 mm. On Sunday it is 46.5 versus 68.6 mm. Yesterday the Sunday values were still 58.1 and 82.1 mm; so both have come down somewhat. Today only climatempo gives temperatures. The climatempo page also says elsewhere “no rain in the next 15 days”; that contradicts its own daily values, and we use the daily values.
What does “temperature drop” mean? When a cold front arrives, cold air slides like a wedge under the warm air. Then it can get several degrees cooler within a few hours. For the baby, that means: keep one more layer ready on Sunday, even if the morning is still warm.

For planning: move shopping and laundry to Monday and Tuesday. On Wednesday it is almost dry according to both sources; on Thursday it rains again.

Sunday: Feira do Calçadão in Santo Antônio da Platina. The Prefeitura reports the Feira “normalmente neste domingo, 25 de outubro, das 9h às 14h,” despite the runoff election.[3] There is a brechó with secondhand clothes and shoes, handicrafts, artists and a Praça de Alimentação, “com entrada gratuita.” Location: Calçadão Manoel Arrabaça Ribeirete downtown. The stalls stand outdoors on the street. With 46 to 68 mm of rain, that is hardly fun for the two-year-old and the baby. With the older ones, a short visit in the morning works if the rain pauses.

Pro: free, day-trip ring, food on site, brechó for children's clothes.
Con: under open sky on the wettest day of the week; street stays open to cars; shade, changing room and parking not confirmed.

Only if you really feel like traveling: Curitiba. The Biblioteca Pública do Paraná offers a free reading hour in the children's section on Saturday, Oct 24, from 10 to 11 a.m.[8] But that is about 400 km in the rain. For one hour it is not worth it; we mention it only for completeness.

And at home? A rainy Saturday is a good day for playing coffee detective, as preparation for the exhibition: with the older kids, compare a bean, ground coffee and a cup, and guess how people got from bush to cup in the old days. Or build a blanket fort, bake pão de queijo, take a puddle walk in rain boots.

Health & Safety [9][10]

Dengue: no new figures The latest dengue bulletin from Paraná is the “Informe Epidemiológico nº 29 - 13/10/2026.”[9] We could not read the PDF file, so we give no case count and no calendar week. Caution with search hits: the often-cited 92,620 cases and 145 deaths belong to the 2025 season, not 2026. So far the bulletins have come roughly every two weeks; the next would be due around Oct 27.
Why mosquitoes come after the rain The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the water line in small containers: plant saucers, buckets, toys in the yard. When rain fills them, the larvae hatch. After about a week, a new mosquito flies. Whoever empties and turns everything over on the dry Monday takes away the mosquito's nursery.
Vaccinations Paraná's multi-vaccination campaign ran from Aug 3 to Sep 1, 2026. According to the health department, the vaccines remain available at the health posts (UBS); the measles vaccine applies from 12 months to 59 years.[10] A good reason to bring the vaccination cards of all four kids on your next UBS visit.
  • Baby and mosquitoes: mosquito net over the crib and stroller, especially in the weeks after the rain.
  • Thunderstorms: when there is lightning, do not let the kids play in the yard.
  • Wet roads: better to postpone trips on Sunday.
