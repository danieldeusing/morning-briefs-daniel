Weekend Pick

Rainy Saturday at home: coffee detectives

Where: at home, kitchen table When: Saturday afternoon, when the showers come Age: the 7- and 8-year-olds as detectives; the two-year-old may smell and pour; the baby watches from the carrier Price: only what is in the house

Put three things on the table: a coffee bean, some ground coffee and a cup of brewed coffee. The older kids guess how coffee gets from the bush to the cup and draw the path as a comic. If you like, ask the grandparents on the phone how it was done in the old days. That is the best preparation for the exhibition "Relíquias da Nossa Terra" from Monday: then the kids look for the old tools at the Clube that they drew themselves.[4] Afterwards, a blanket fort in the living room and pão de queijo from the oven while the rain drums outside.

Pro: no trip, no money, no storm risk, links play with Monday's exhibition.
Con: hot coffee and small hands: the cup stays with the adults.

Where the sources disagree Both say the same that it will rain. They differ on the amount: today 21.1 mm at climatempo, 3.97 mm at HG Brasil; tomorrow 64.5 versus 43.2 mm. Temperatures also differ by up to 3 °C. The climatempo page also says "Não há previsão de chuva" in one place; that contradicts its own daily values, and we use the daily values.
What is a "temporal"? That is what Brazilians call a short, violent storm: a lot of rain in a short time, often with lightning and wind. Imagine someone tipping a whole bucket of water out at once instead of drop by drop. Streets and ditches then fill up fast.

For planning: put shopping and laundry on Monday and Tuesday. For Sunday, keep one extra layer ready for the baby; it will be up to 8 degrees cooler than today.

Health & Safety [8]

Dengue: no new figures The latest dengue bulletin from Paraná is still the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 29 - 13/10/2026".[8] We could not read the PDF file. So we give no case count and no calendar week. Be careful with search hits: the often-cited 92,000 cases and 145 deaths belong to the 2025 season, not 2026.
Why mosquitoes come after the rain The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the water in small containers: flower saucers, buckets, toys in the yard. When rain fills them, the larvae hatch. After about a week a new mosquito flies. Anyone who empties and turns over everything on dry Monday takes away the mosquito's nursery. The older kids can turn it into a treasure hunt.
  • Baby and mosquitoes: mosquito net over crib and stroller, especially in the weeks after the rain.
  • Thunderstorms: with lightning, no kids in the yard, no swimming in the pool.
  • Wet roads: better postpone trips on Sunday; never drive through streams.
