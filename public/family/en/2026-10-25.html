Health & Safety [1]

Dengue: no current number We found no dengue number for Paraná with a 2026 calendar week today. The Sesa-PR pages show no numbers on the home page. The hits in the search index belong to the 2025 season, so we do not cite them. climatempo rates the mosquito risk today as "alto" and the flu risk as "médio".[1]
Why mosquitoes come after the rain The dengue mosquito lays its eggs just above the water line in small containers: plant saucers, buckets, toys in the yard. When rain fills them, the larvae hatch. After about a week, a new mosquito flies. Anyone who empties and flips everything over on dry Tuesday takes away the mosquito's nursery.
  • Baby and mosquitoes: Mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
  • Thunderstorms: With lightning, no kids in the yard.
  • Wet roads: Never drive through flooded spots and streams.

Flu and RSV data, vaccination campaigns and road closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 were not checked by us today.
