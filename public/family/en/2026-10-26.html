Why two weather services say different things Each service calculates with its own computer model, a bit like two cooks with the same recipe. In clear weather the result tastes the same. If a rain area sits right on the edge, one says "just missed", the other "maybe". When both agree, as for Tuesday and Thursday, the forecast is much more certain.

Health & Safety [4]

Metapneumovirus rising, in Paraná too According to the InfoGripe bulletin of 2026-10-15 (SE 40/2026, Oct 4 to 10), severe acute respiratory illness (SRAG) remains stable nationwide. But: metapneumovirus is "o único vírus respiratório com tendência de crescimento" among confirmed SRAG cases, and Paraná is among the states with an increase.[4] We have no figures for Paraná alone.
What is metapneumovirus? A cold virus, a close relative of RSV. In big kids it causes a runny nose and cough. In babies it can slip into the small airways, and then they breathe fast and with effort. So: wash hands when the big kids come home from school, and keep coughing visitors away from the baby. If the baby breathes fast, pulls in the skin between the ribs when breathing, or drinks poorly, go to the doctor right away.
