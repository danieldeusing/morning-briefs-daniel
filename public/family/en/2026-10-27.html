Health & Safety [4][6]

Metapneumovirus is rising, also in Paraná According to the InfoGripe bulletin of 2026-10-15 (SE 40/2026, Oct 4 to 10), severe respiratory illness (SRAG) remains stable nationwide. But: metapneumovirus is "o único vírus respiratório com tendência de crescimento" among confirmed SRAG cases, and Paraná is among the states with an increase.[4] We found no newer bulletin (SE 41). We have no figures for Paraná alone.
What is metapneumovirus? A cold virus, a close relative of RSV. In big kids it causes a runny nose and cough. In babies it can slip into the small airways, and then they breathe fast and with effort. So: wash hands when the big kids come home from school, and keep coughing visitors away from the baby. If the baby breathes fast, pulls in the skin between the ribs when breathing, or drinks poorly, see a doctor right away.
Dengue: no current figure The latest dengue report from Sesa-PR is No. 29 of 2026-10-13. We could not read the figures in it (PDF), so we give none. Careful when googling: many hits belong to the big 2025 season.[6] It rains again from Thursday. Today, the dry day, it is worth emptying everything in the yard that collects water.
Why empty it? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week, new mosquitoes hatch from them. Whoever empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery.
