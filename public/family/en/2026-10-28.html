Health & Safety [6][7]

Dengue: new report, no readable number Sesa-PR has published the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30 - 27/10/2026".[6] We could not read the numbers in it (PDF), so we give no current figure. The last figure we could read is old: in weeks SE 1 to 14/2026, Paraná counted 2,028 confirmed cases and one death, versus 52,193 cases in the same period of 2025.[7] So the 2026 season was very quiet through April. Careful when googling: many hits belong to the 2024 and 2025 seasons.

Why this matters right now: After Thursday's rain it warms up to 28 °C. The dengue mosquito likes that. On Friday, a short round through the yard and patio is worth it: empty buckets, flowerpot saucers and toys.

Why empty it? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week new mosquitoes hatch from them. If you empty the water first, you take away their nursery.

On the road: For Finados we found no PRF traffic plan, and no reports of closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. On long weekends the federal highways are busy, even more so in the rain. If you drive: leave early, with breaks for the baby.
