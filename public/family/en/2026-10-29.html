Health & safety [7][8][9][10]

Rain through year-end On 2026-10-27 the Defesa Civil of Ribeirão Claro points to Simepar projections: rainfall may be „acima da média" across all of Paraná through the end of 2026, meaning more than normal.[8] Simepar names El Niño as the cause.[10] In an emergency: Defesa Civil 199, fire department (Corpo de Bombeiros) 193.
What is El Niño? The Pacific off South America gets warmer than usual. That shifts the clouds and winds over the whole continent. For southern Brazil it often means more rain, just like right now.

Dengue: The latest figure we could check is old. In weeks SE 1 to 14/2026, Paraná counted 2,028 confirmed cases and one death, against 52,193 cases and 90 deaths in the same period of 2025.[9] The Londrina region then had the most in Paraná, with 534 cases. We did not read a newer number today, so we give none. Careful when googling: many hits belong to the 2024 and 2025 seasons.

Why this matters now: After today's rain it gets warm, up to 27 °C from Friday. The dengue mosquito likes that. On Friday a short round through the yard and terrace is worth it: empty buckets, saucers and toys.

Why empty them? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week new mosquitoes hatch from them. Whoever empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery.
Outubro Rosa: last day today The Prefeitura's Mamamóvel is in Ribeirão Claro on October 28 and 29 and does 140 mammograms there.[7] If you have an appointment or want to ask: today is the last chance.

On the road: For Finados we found no reports of closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Long weekends are busy on the federal highways, and even more so in the rain. If you drive: leave early, with breaks for the baby.
