Is São Paulo worth it? Three days off, and many of the offers there are indoors and free.[6] It is worth it if you are visiting family or friends in SP anyway. Just for Halloween, with a baby and four to six hours each way, with rain on Saturday and Monday: probably not. Not for our kids are the Halloween evening at Museu Catavento (spooky fun from 7 p.m.) and the zombie train to Paranapiacaba (ages 12 and up). All SP details come from a single kids' guide, retrieved today. In Curitiba there is a Halloween party in the children's section of the Biblioteca Pública on Saturday from 9 a.m., free.[5] At about 400 km, though, that is too far for a weekend.

Halloween at home, our suggestion: Both services say it rains on Saturday, and a pumpkin afternoon needs no weather. The big ones (8 and 7) carve or paint a pumpkin and invent costumes from old cloths. The two-year-old hunts for hidden candy in the apartment, the baby watches from our arms. In the evening, a spooky movie for kids with popcorn. If Sunday does turn out dry (that is open, see above), a round outside in the morning fits.

Monday, Finados: Many families in Brazil visit their relatives' graves on this day. It gets busy around the cemeteries, even in small towns. For the kids this can be a good occasion to talk about the grandparents in Germany and the family history.

Health & Safety [7][8]

Dengue, new bulletin: The Paraná health authority (Sesa) published its "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30" on Oct 27, 2026, the 30th bulletin of the 2026 season.[7] We did not read the figures in it today. So we give no new number and project nothing. We will try again tomorrow.

The last verified status is old: In epidemiological weeks SE 1 to 14/2026, Paraná counted 2,028 confirmed cases and one death, against 52,193 cases and 90 deaths in the same period of 2025, so a good 96% fewer.[8] The Londrina region (17ª Regional) then had the most cases in Paraná, with 534. Careful when googling: many hits belong to the outbreak seasons of 2024 and 2025.

Warm and wet: good time for mosquitoes Up to 27 °C and rain on almost every day, which the dengue mosquito likes. On dry Tuesday, a round through the garden and patio is worth it: empty buckets, plant saucers, kiddie pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib or stroller helps.
Why empty them? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week, new mosquitoes hatch from them. Whoever empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery.

On the road: We found no reports of closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. On a long weekend with rain, though, the roads are busy and slippery. If you drive: leave early, with breaks for the baby. In an emergency: Defesa Civil 199, fire department 193.

School & Holidays [3]

Mon Nov 2 is Finados (All Souls' Day), a national holiday.[3] With the weekend, that makes three days off; school starts again on Tuesday. Later in November: Nov 15 (Proclamação da República) falls on a Sunday, Nov 20 (Consciência Negra) on a Friday. So the next long weekend comes just three weeks later. Whether the schools in Ribeirão Claro do anything differently on these days, we did not check; the school group surely knows.

Region & Culture [4][9]

After the holiday, things get lively in the region. Fejacan in Jacarezinho starts on Thu Nov 5; the band Rancore plays on Sun Nov 8 on Praça Rui Barbosa; the article gives no time or price.[9] In Ribeirão Claro, the Festival de Primavera also has sports: a footvolley tournament on Sat Nov 7 from 8 a.m., beach volleyball on Sun Nov 8, R$ 80 per pair.[4] Maybe something for Dad and a friend, while the kids watch from the sidelines.

German-Brazilian Corner

Halloween is young in Brazil, and on October 31 some people also celebrate Dia do Saci, with the figure from Brazilian folklore. And Finados is what All Souls' Day and Totensonntag are together in Germany: a quiet day for the family. A nice word for the kids: a abóbora, the pumpkin.

Our suggestion: Halloween at home, with a pumpkin, costumes, and a candy hunt for the little one.

Have a great long weekend, everyone, and happy Halloween.
