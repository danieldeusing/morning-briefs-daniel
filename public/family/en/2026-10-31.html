Movies in Londrina on Finados? Sounds good for a wet Monday: Bem Paraná writes on Oct 30 of "80 filmes gratuitos e sessões especiais no feriado de Finados".[6] We could not open the article itself (blocked). According to a festival listing, the 28th Kinoarte festival runs from Oct 30 to Nov 8 in Londrina.[5] That the headline means exactly this festival is only very likely, not confirmed. We do not know venues, times or kids' films. It is worth it if you first find a fitting session on the Kinoarte site or Instagram. With a baby: better a short session and an aisle seat.

Is São Paulo worth it? Many Halloween offerings there are indoors and free, often with registration through an app.[7] That is worth it if you are visiting family or friends in SP anyway. Just for Halloween, with a baby and four to six hours each way: probably not. Not for our kids: the "Noite no Museu" at Catavento (spooky fun from 7 pm) and the zombie train to Paranapiacaba (age 12 and up). All SP details come from a single kids' guide, retrieved today.

Our suggestion for today: The dry Saturday is the best of the three days. Go out in the morning before it gets hot: playground, garden, a walk with the stroller. In the afternoon, Halloween at home. The big ones (8 and 7) paint a pumpkin and make costumes from old cloths. The two-year-old hunts for hidden candy, the baby watches from your arms. Sunday and Monday are then days for books, games and maybe the movies in Londrina.

Health & Safety [8][9]

Santa Casa Jacarezinho: status unclear On Oct 18, Tabajara Notícias reported that the Santa Casa de Jacarezinho suspended services "após agravamento da crise e falta de insumos", that is, because of the crisis and lack of supplies.[8] The city wrote on Oct 19: "a Santa Casa de Misericórdia não está fechando", so the hospital is not closing. A local source reports it changed administrators (unconfirmed). We have not checked what the hospital offers today. With an infant at home: before the long weekend, clarify where you would go in an emergency, and call before you drive.

Heavy rain on Monday? The Defesa Civil of Ribeirão Claro published tips for heavy rain and thunderstorms on Oct 27.[9] You get warnings by SMS if you send your postal code (CEP) to 40199. Emergency numbers: Defesa Civil 199, fire department 193. In a thunderstorm: do not stand under trees and do not drive through flooded streets.

Dengue: We have no current figure with an epidemiological week today. So we name none and extrapolate nothing. What is clear: warmth up to 28 °C and rain on many days is what the dengue mosquito likes. The dry Saturday is good for a round through the garden: empty buckets, plant saucers, wading pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib or stroller helps.

Why empty them? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week new mosquitoes hatch from them. If you empty the water beforehand, you take away their nursery.

On the road: We found no closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. On Oct 21 a 20-year-old motorcyclist died on the BR-153 near Jacarezinho in a collision with a truck.[8] On a long weekend with rain the roads are busy and slippery. If you drive: leave early, with breaks for the baby.

School & Holidays

Mon Nov 2 is Finados (All Souls' Day), a national holiday. With the weekend that makes three days off; school starts again on Tuesday Nov 3. Many families visit relatives' graves on Finados. It is busy around the cemeteries then, even in small towns, and in rain parking gets tight there.

Region & Culture [3][4][9]

After the holiday things get lively. Fejacan in Jacarezinho begins Thu Nov 5; on Sun Nov 8 the band Rancore plays at Praça Rui Barbosa; the article gives neither time nor price.[4] The Festival de Primavera in Ribeirão Claro also has sports: on Sat Nov 7 from 8 am a footvolley tournament, on Sun Nov 8 beach volleyball, R$ 80 per pair, with prizes of R$ 250, 150 and 100.[3] Maybe something for dad and a friend while the kids watch from the sideline.

And Ribeirão Claro now has an official dish: in the "Prato Típico" competition the Entrevero Pérola do Norte won.[9] An entrevero is a skillet dish with lots of meat and vegetables. Maybe it will soon show up at the Feira da Lua.

German-Brazilian

Halloween is young in Brazil. On October 31 some prefer to celebrate Dia do Saci, with the one-legged imp from Brazilian folklore. And Finados is what All Souls' Day is in Germany: a quiet day for the family. A good occasion to talk with the kids about the grandparents in Germany. Two words for today: a abóbora, the pumpkin, and o doce, the sweet.

Good morning, it's Saturday, October thirty-first, Halloween. We have three days off ahead of us, because Monday is Finados, a national holiday.

The good news first: Saturday now looks dry. Climatempo says zero percent chance of rain, Open-Meteo's model forty-one percent, but barely two millimeters. Highs up to eighty-two degrees, that's twenty-eight Celsius. So today is the day to be outside, ideally in the morning. On Sunday both services agree rain is coming. Monday brings rain too, they just disagree on the amount: Climatempo says twenty-nine millimeters, Open-Meteo a little over one millimeter. So plan Monday indoors and check again Sunday evening.

Near us, we found no verified kids' event for the long weekend. In Londrina, though, the Kinoarte film festival runs through November eighth. According to Bem Paraná, there are eighty free films over Finados. We don't yet know which ones are for kids. São Paulo has plenty of Halloween, often free and indoors, but with a baby and a four-to-six-hour drive, it's probably too much.

Next week the festivals come to us. On Thursday, Fejacan starts in Jacarezinho. From November sixth through eighth, Ribeirão Claro celebrates the Festival de Primavera at the Parque Ecológico, with the Feira da Lua from seven in the evening, music and volleyball tournaments. Admission is free.

On health: the Santa Casa in Jacarezinho suspended services in mid-October because of a crisis. The city says the hospital is not closing, but we don't know where things stand today. With a baby at home, it's worth settling beforehand where you'd go in an emergency. Civil Defense warnings come by text message if you send your postal code to the number four zero one nine nine. And with warm weather and rain, the usual rule applies: empty standing water in the yard, today on the dry Saturday.

Tomorrow we'll look at the Finados weather again and, hopefully, at the film program in Londrina. Happy Halloween, everyone.
