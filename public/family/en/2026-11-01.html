Health & Safety [7]

Dengue: On Oct 27, 2026 Paraná published the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30".[7] We could not read the cases and deaths in it. So we cite no number today and extrapolate nothing. But one thing is clear: a warm week with rain almost every day is exactly the weather the dengue mosquito likes. After the rain, a walk around the yard is worth it: empty buckets, plant saucers, kiddie pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib or stroller helps.

Why empty them? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week new mosquitoes hatch from them. Anyone who empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery.

Rain and roads: We found no official heavy-rain warning, but could not fully check either. On the holiday with lots of rain the roads are crowded and slippery. If you drive to the cemetery or to Londrina: slowly, with lights on, and not through flooded spots. Emergency numbers: Defesa Civil 199, fire department 193.

Santa Casa Jacarezinho: We did not recheck the current status after the problems in October today. With a baby at home: settle briefly before the holiday where you would go in an emergency.

School & Holidays

Mon Nov 2 is Finados (All Souls' Day), a national holiday. School resumes on Tuesday Nov 3. Many families visit relatives' graves on this day. It is busy around the cemeteries, and in the rain parking gets tight. A look ahead: the next holiday, Proclamação da República on Nov 15, falls on a Sunday this year. So there is no long weekend.

Region & Culture

The Fejacan in Jacarezinho begins on Thu Nov 5. On Sun Nov 8 the band Rancore plays at the Praça Rui Barbosa; the article gives neither time nor price.[4] At the Festival de Primavera in Ribeirão Claro, besides the Feira and music there are also sports tournaments on the weekend.[3] Together this makes the coming week the liveliest in a long time, if the weather cooperates.

German-Brazilian Corner

Finados is what All Souls' Day is in Germany: a quiet day for the family. In Brazil many people go to the cemetery with flowers and candles. A good occasion to talk with the kids about the grandparents in Germany, maybe on a video call. Two words for today: a vela, the candle, and a chuva, the rain.
