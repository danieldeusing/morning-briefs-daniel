Today: Finados under a thunderstorm warning [1][2][3]

Simepar and the Defesa Civil have issued a warning for all of Paraná for the holiday. Band reported it on 2026-10-30: "Simepar aponta risco alto de temporais no Norte e Noroeste e moderado nas demais regiões nesta segunda-feira (2)."[3] Heavy rain, hail, falling trees and flooding are possible. The Band page itself was blocked today, and so was Simepar. So we know the warning only from a search summary of the article.

What this means for today If you plan to visit the cemetery: pick a dry window, do not stand under large trees, and go back to the car or house at the first thunder. Do not drive through flooded streets. Free alerts on your phone: send an SMS with your CEP to 40199.[3] Emergency numbers: Defesa Civil 199, fire department 193.

How much rain? Here the sources are far apart. Climatempo says 21 to 29 °C and 6.3 mm.[1] The Open-Meteo weather model says only 24 °C, but 15.3 mm and a 94% chance of rain.[2] We do not pick a value. With a thunderstorm warning, we prefer to plan for the wetter one.

Our suggestion for today: a day indoors. The big ones (8 and 7) can craft or draw candles for the grandparents, the two-year-old helps build, the baby watches from your arms. A video call to Germany fits Finados well.

The week: hot, then the drop [1][2]

  • Tue, Nov 3 to Thu, Nov 5: both sources see 29 to 31 °C, hottest on Wednesday. Hardly any rain until Wednesday (0.1 to 2.5 mm). Showers are possible again on Thursday: 4.8 mm (Climatempo) or 7.1 mm with 60% (Open-Meteo).
  • Fri, Nov 6 and Sat, Nov 7: disagreement, see box. Climatempo says 30 and 27 °C, Open-Meteo 28.5 °C both days.
  • Sun, Nov 8: the drop. High of 22 °C (Climatempo) or 24 °C (Open-Meteo), 15 to 16 °C at night, both without rain.
Where the sources disagree Today: 6.3 versus 15.3 mm, and 29 versus 24 °C. Friday and Saturday: Climatempo shows 0.0 mm, Open-Meteo 5.8 mm (59%) and 2.7 mm (70%).[1][2] Climatempo gives no percentage on its page, so we cannot compare the chance of rain directly. Simepar, the weather service of Paraná, was blocked. Open-Meteo is a computation model, not a weather service. For the festival weekend this means: check again on Thursday.
Why Sunday gets cooler A temperature drop like this usually comes from a cold front. Cold air from the south pushes under the warm air like a wedge. The warm air has to rise, where clouds form and sometimes thunderstorms. Once the cold front has passed, cool, dry air stays behind. That matches what both sources show for Sunday: fewer degrees and no rain.

At 31 °C with a baby: Stay indoors during the midday heat, do not add an extra layer on the baby, and make sure there is enough nursing or bottle feeding. For the big ones after school: water bottle, hat, shade.

Health & Safety [8]

Respiratory infections: The latest status we could check is old. According to the InfoGripe bulletin of 2026-09-10, Paraná moved from "Alerta" to "Segurança", with a stable trend.[8] We found no newer bulletin for Paraná. We do not report figures without an epidemiological week.

Dengue: We could not read a current bulletin for the new 2026/27 season today. So no figure. One thing is clear: heat after rain is exactly the weather the dengue mosquito likes. After today's thunderstorm it is worth walking through the garden tomorrow: empty buckets, plant saucers, kiddie pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib or stroller helps.

Why empty them? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week new mosquitoes fly out of it. Whoever empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery.

Roads: We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 today. After a thunderstorm day, branches and mud can lie on the road.

School & Holidays

Today is Finados (All Souls' Day), a national holiday. School resumes on Tuesday, Nov 3. Two dates for planning:

  • Sun, Nov 15: Proclamação da República. The holiday falls on a Sunday, so there is no long weekend.
  • Fri, Nov 20: Consciência Negra, a national holiday since 2024. It falls on a Friday. That gives three days off, from Nov 20 to 22. A good chance for Londrina or even São Paulo, for example for Natal by Expoflora.

We did not check the school calendar and vaccination campaigns today.

German-Brazilian Corner

Finados is the Brazilian All Souls' Day. In Germany this day is rather quiet and in many places a working day. Here it is a holiday, and the cemeteries are full of flowers and candles. Two words for today: o temporal, the storm, and a vela, the candle. And for the week: calorão, the big heat.

Good morning. It's Monday, November second, and today is Finados, a national holiday. School starts again on Tuesday.

A thunderstorm warning from Simepar and Defesa Civil is in effect for all of Paraná today, with a high risk in the north of the state. Heavy rain, hail and falling trees are possible. How much rain we'll get depends on who you ask: Climatempo says a little over six millimeters, while the Open-Meteo model says a little over fifteen. If you're heading to the cemetery, pick a dry window, stay away from big trees and flooded streets. You can get free alerts on your phone by texting your CEP to four zero one nine nine.

After that, it turns hot: twenty-nine to thirty-one degrees Celsius from Tuesday through Thursday, hottest on Wednesday. On Sunday temperatures drop to just twenty-two to twenty-four degrees, with cool nights. For Friday and Saturday the sources disagree: one says dry, the other up to seventy percent chance of rain. We'll check again on Thursday.

The festivals start Thursday. In Jacarezinho, the Fejacan begins, and on Sunday the band Rancore plays there. And from Friday to Sunday, Ribeirão Claro hosts the Festival de Primavera at the Parque Ecológico, with the Feira da Lua starting at seven p.m. on Friday and Saturday. Admission is free. That's our pick for the week.

If you happen to be in São Paulo: on Saturday, the free GRAACC festival at Parque do Povo features Palavra Cantada, from eleven a.m. to four p.m.

On health: we don't have current dengue numbers today. But after rain and heat, remember to empty any standing water in the yard, and use a mosquito net for the baby.

And one to plan ahead: November twentieth, Consciência Negra, falls on a Friday. That means a long weekend. Enjoy a calm, dry holiday, everyone.
