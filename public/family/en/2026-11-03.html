Health & safety [7]

Dengue: Paraná has published a new bulletin, the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30 - 27/10/2026".[7] We could not read the case numbers in it today. So we give no number. The search hits only showed old numbers from 2025, which we deliberately leave out.

One thing is clear: first heat, then several days of rain, that is exactly the weather the dengue mosquito likes. After the rain this weekend it is worth a walk around the yard: empty buckets, saucers, kiddie pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib or stroller helps.

Why rain after heat? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. Warmth makes the larvae grow faster. After about a week new mosquitoes fly. If you empty the water first, you take away their nursery.

Roads and storm warnings: We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092, or official warnings from INMET and CEMADEN, today.

German-Brazilian corner

In Germany November is gray and cold, here the rainy season begins with warm thunderstorms. Two words for this week: a garoa, the drizzle, and a pancada de chuva, the short, heavy downpour. If it rains at the Feira on Friday, let's hope it is only a pancada.

Good morning, it's Tuesday, November third. School is back in session today after the holiday.

It's going to be hot today and tomorrow: up to thirty degrees today, and on Wednesday thirty-one, maybe even thirty-three. Whether it rains on Wednesday depends on who you ask: one source says zero percent, the others say sixty and seventy-six percent. From Thursday on, all three agree: rain is likely through Sunday. Starting Monday it cools off, with lows of just thirteen degrees at night.

With the baby in this heat: stay indoors at midday, skip the extra layer, and offer fluids more often.

This weekend Ribeirão Claro celebrates its Festival de Primavera at the Parque Ecológico. On Friday and Saturday the Feira da Lua opens at seven p.m., and admission is free. Since rain is likely, we suggest going early and keeping it short, and heading straight home if it pours. In Jacarezinho the Fejacan festival kicks off on Thursday, with a rock night there on Sunday.

On health: Paraná published a new dengue bulletin on October twenty-seventh. We haven't been able to read the numbers yet. After the rain, it's worth emptying any standing water in the yard.

And for planning ahead: the ENEM exam runs on the Sundays of November eighth and fifteenth, so expect more traffic around midday. November twentieth, Consciência Negra, is a Friday and makes for a long weekend. Have a great Tuesday, everyone.
