Festival weekend with a Saturday thunderstorm: Friday evening is the best window, Fejacan brings music under one roof

One more hot day today, up to 31 °C. Then the rain arrives, and on Saturday probably a proper thunderstorm, right in the middle of the Festival de Primavera. Sunday will be dry and cool.

Health & safety [9]

Dengue: The latest Paraná bulletin is still the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30 - 27/10/2026".[9] We could not read the figures in it today either. So we cite none. Search hits only show figures from the old season, which we deliberately leave out.

What is certain: heat, then lots of rain, which the dengue mosquito likes. After Saturday, Sunday is a good time for a round through the garden and terrace: empty buckets, plant saucers, wading pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller helps.

Why right after the rain? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. In the warmth the larvae grow fast. After about a week new mosquitoes fly. Anyone who empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery.

In a thunderstorm with kids: At the first thunder, go indoors, not under lone trees. For the festival that means: if there is lightning, better to go home. We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 today.

German-Brazilian corner

A word for Saturday: o temporal, the thunderstorm. Climatempo uses it in the forecast. And one for the Fejacan ticket: o ingresso solidário, the solidarity admission ticket. Instead of money, you give something for others. In Germany you know this from Christmas parcel drives, here it comes as early as November.
