Health & Safety [7]

Dengue: The latest bulletin from Paraná is still the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30 - 27/10/2026". It covers weeks SE 01 to 42/2026.[7] We could not read the numbers in it today either; the PDF yielded no text. So we give no case numbers, not even for the Regional Jacarezinho. Search hits often show numbers from the big 2025 outbreak. We leave them out on purpose; they belong to the old season.

What is certain: warmth plus a lot of rain is exactly what the dengue mosquito likes. After the wet Thursday and Saturday, a round through the garden and terrace on Sunday afternoon is worth it: empty buckets, plant saucers, kiddie pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller helps.

Why right after the rain? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, a single bottle cap full is enough. In the warmth the eggs quickly turn into larvae. After about a week new mosquitoes fly. If you empty the water beforehand, you take away their nursery. So: empty it once a week, ideally always on the same day.

Cool nights for the baby: From Sunday to Wednesday both sources see lows of 12 to 15 °C.[1][2] After days at 30 °C that feels cold. A newborn holds heat poorly. One layer more than the parents wear is a good rule of thumb. Feel the neck, not the hands: if it is warm and dry, it fits.

In a thunderstorm with kids: At the sound of thunder, go indoors, not under lone trees. For the festival that means: at lightning, better to go home. We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 today. We also have no flu or RSV numbers for the baby; the season for that is winter.

German-Brazilian Corner

Two words from today's forecast. As aberturas de sol are the gaps of sun between the clouds, literally "openings of the sun". And tempo firme means steady, dry weather, as Climatempo promises for Sunday evening. In German you would say "es klart auf" (it is clearing up). A nice sentence for the kids at the window: O sol abriu!, the sun has come out.
