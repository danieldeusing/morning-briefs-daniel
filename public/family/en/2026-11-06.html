Health & Safety [9][10]

Dengue: The latest bulletin from Paraná is the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30 - 27/10/2026", for weeks SE 01 to 42/2026.[9] Today we could read part of it. From Jan 1 to Oct 23, 2026 the lab tested 12,082 samples, 549 of them positive for dengue. The most common type is still DENV-2, as in 2025. The case numbers appear in the bulletin only in charts, which we could not read out. So we give no case and no death figures for 2026 and no figure for the Jacarezinho Regional.

For context, an older, reliably dated figure: On Apr 15, 2026 the state reported 2,028 confirmed cases for SE 1 to 14/2026, after 52,193 in the same period of 2025. That is a drop of over 96%. The north and northwest of Paraná had the most cases.[10] This figure is seven months old. It only shows that 2026 has so far been a quiet dengue year.

But now the season begins in which it rises again: warm and wet. On Monday, when it is dry, a round through the garden and patio is worthwhile. Empty buckets, plant saucers, wading pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.

Why right after the rain? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap full is enough. In the warmth these quickly become larvae. After about a week new mosquitoes fly. Whoever empties the water beforehand takes away their nursery. After three days of rain, the first dry day is therefore the right day for it.

Cool nights for the baby: From Sunday to Tuesday both sources see lows of 12 to 15 °C.[1][2] After days of almost 30 °C that feels cold. A newborn holds its warmth poorly. Rule of thumb: one layer more than the parents. Feel the neck, not the hands. If it is warm and dry, it fits.

In a thunderstorm with children: At thunder, go indoors, not under lone trees. For the festival in the park that means: at lightning, better to go home. We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 today.
