Health & Safety

Dengue: The latest Paraná bulletin is still the "Informe Epidemiológico nº 30 - 27/10/2026".[7] No newer one has appeared. So we have no new numbers today. The situation from this bulletin was in yesterday's issue.

What is changing is the weather. Heat and heavy rain are exactly the mix mosquitoes like. On Monday, the first dry day, a round through the garden and terrace is worth it: empty buckets, saucers, wading pools and toys. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.

Why empty water after the rain? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap's worth is enough. In the warmth, larvae hatch from them quickly. After about a week, new mosquitoes fly. Whoever pours the water out beforehand takes away their nursery.

Heat today: Up to 33 °C at midday is a lot for a newborn. Keep the baby in the shade, dress lightly, nurse or give the bottle more often. The two-year-old often drinks too little while playing. Remind him.

Cold plunge tomorrow: Going from 33 to 20 °C in one day is very noticeable. For the night into Monday HG Brasil sees 13 °C, for the night into Tuesday 12 °C.[2] A newborn holds heat poorly. Rule of thumb: one layer more than the parents. Feel the neck, not the hands. If it is warm and dry, it is fine.

In a thunderstorm: At the sound of thunder, go indoors, not under single trees. At the park festival that means: at lightning, better go home. We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 today.
