Fittingly, the Prefeitura recalls the Defesa Civil's advice for severe weather, because El Niño raises the chance of heavy rain.[5] We found no current official severe-weather warning for the Norte Pioneiro today.

El Niño: The water in the Pacific off South America gets warmer than normal. That shifts weather patterns far away, including here. Like a big radiator in one corner of the room: the air in the whole room moves differently. In southern Brazil, El Niño often brings more rain.

Health & Safety

Dengue: We could not confirm a current Paraná bulletin with epi week and year today. We therefore give no figure. As soon as a fresh bulletin is readable, we will follow up.

What does not change: after every rain, water collects in buckets, saucers and toys. Monday and Tuesday are dry. That is the best chance for a round through the garden and terrace, ideally together with the older kids as a "mosquito hunt". For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.

Why empty it before the next rain? The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in standing water, even a bottle cap's worth is enough. After about a week, new mosquitoes fly. Anyone who tips out the water beforehand takes away their nursery.

Fresh nights: 12 to 13 °C in the coming nights is a jump after the heat.[2] A newborn holds its warmth poorly. Rule of thumb: one layer more than the parents. Feel the neck, not the hands.

Not checked: closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 as well as vaccination campaigns. The Ribeirão Claro Prefeitura has published no vaccination or school notice in the last two weeks.[5]
