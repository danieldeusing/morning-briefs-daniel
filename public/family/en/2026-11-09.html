For Sunday the site showed 40%.[7] So we do not use it. All values apply to Jacarezinho, about 50 km away.
Percent or millimeters? The percent figure says how likely it is to rain somewhere in the area. The millimeters say how much water falls. 1 mm is one liter on one square meter, barely more than a wet sidewalk. "73% and 1 mm" means: a shower is quite possible, but it is small.

Health & safety [4][5]

Dengue: On 2026-10-27 Paraná published Informe Epidemiológico nº 30.[4] It exists only as a PDF, which we could not read. The epi week is not on the overview page. So we give no current figure.

Season trap The last readable figure is old: 32,927 reported cases in Paraná from SE 01 to 14/2026, versus 156,160 in the same period of 2025.[5] That belongs to the past season. The new dengue season in Paraná typically runs from about epidemiological week 31. If you search yourselves, you will quickly land on such old numbers. Please do not read them as the current state.

What does not change: after every rain, water collects in buckets, saucers and toys. Today and tomorrow are dry. That is the best chance for a round through the yard and patio before the rain comes on Wednesday. The older kids like to join in as a "mosquito hunt". For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.

Epi week (SE): Health departments count the year in weeks, the semanas epidemiológicas. SE 14/2026 means: the 14th week of 2026, so early April. That way you see right away how old a number is.

Crisp mornings: 12 to 15 °C early, up to 25 °C at midday. A newborn holds heat poorly. Rule of thumb: one layer more than the parents, and feel the neck, not the hands.

Not checked: closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092, school dates and vaccination campaigns.

German-Brazilian corner

Two words for this week. Emendar o feriado means taking a bridge day and "stitching" the holiday to the weekend. On Nov 15 you will hear: Esse ano não dá para emendar, "this year there is no bridge", because the holiday is already a Sunday. And previsão do tempo is the weather forecast.
