Percent or millimeters? The percent number says how likely rain is. The millimeters say how much falls. 1 mm is one liter on one square meter, so a wet sidewalk. 20 mm is a real downpour where puddles stay.

Health & safety

Respiratory: The Fiocruz InfoGripe bulletin for EW 43/2026 (Oct 25 to 31) sees metapneumovirus, rhinovirus and Covid-19 rising. Paraná is among the states with more metapneumovirus. Children under two still have the highest rates of severe respiratory infections. RSV, in Portuguese VSR, is falling or stable.[8] These figures come via a trade portal, not directly from Fiocruz.

Metapneumovirus: A cold virus very similar to RSV. In older kids it is usually just the sniffles. In babies it can slip into the small airways, like sand into a thin straw. Warning signs in a baby: fast breathing, pulling in under the ribs, poor feeding. Then see a doctor.

For the baby: people with colds keep their distance, wash hands before picking the baby up, the older kids too after school.

Dengue: No current Sesa PR bulletin found for the 2026/27 season. The last figure comes from the old season, so we do not cite it. Today, before the rain, empty buckets, saucers and toys in the garden. A mosquito net for the baby.

German-Brazilian

One word for this week: temporal. In German it sounds like "zeitlich" (temporal, relating to time); in Brazil it means a severe storm, so heavy rain with wind and thunder. If the app says "noite, temporal", it is better to stay inside in the evening.
