Health & safety

Dengue: There is no fresh figure for the new 2026/27 season. The latest bulletin we found is from April 2026 and belongs to the old season. So we give no number.[6] Sesa PR met with experts in September to prepare for the new season because an El Niño is possible.[7] With the rain from Thursday and the heat after it, mosquitoes find good breeding spots.

Why rain plus heat? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of standing water, even a bottle cap is enough. In heat, an egg becomes a mosquito especially fast. Emptying buckets, plant saucers and toys after every rain breaks this cycle.

For the baby: a mosquito net over the crib or stroller, and plenty of shade on the hot weekend.

Respiratory illness: The latest status we have is InfoGripe EW 43/2026 (Oct 25 to 31). Back then metapneumovirus rose, in Paraná too. RSV fell or held steady.[8] We did not check for a newer bulletin today.

Not checked: closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092, vaccination campaigns.

German-Brazilian corner

A word for this week: pancada de chuva. Literally it is a “rain blow”, meaning a short, heavy shower. After hot days like the weekend, it often comes in the afternoon, suddenly and briefly.
