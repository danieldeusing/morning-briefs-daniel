What does 100% chance of rain at only 4 mm mean? The percentage says how sure it is that it rains at some point. The millimeters say how much. 4 mm is like pouring an egg cup of water onto a small area: wet enough for puddles, but no storm. From about 20 mm a day it becomes a real downpour.

Health & safety

Dengue: The state of Paraná published "Informe Epidemiológico nº 31" on 2026-11-10.[5] We could not read the numbers in it. So we give no number today. Since February 2025, the state counts dengue by calendar year, no longer by a season that ran across the turn of the year.[5] Anyone who sees a number should therefore check whether it applies to 2026.

Why does the year count? A dengue season used to run across the turn of the year, so across two years. Now Paraná counts from January to December. A number from the old system and one from the new do not fit together, even if both say "2026".

Rain today, heat on the weekend: this is exactly the weather in which the Aedes mosquito breeds fast. After the rain, empty buckets, plant saucers and toys in the yard. For the baby: a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.

Not checked today: new InfoGripe bulletin on respiratory viruses, closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092, vaccination campaigns.

Holidays & planning [6][7]

  • Sun Nov 15: Proclamação da República. In 2026 the holiday falls on a Sunday, there is no extra day off.
  • Fri Nov 20: Dia da Consciência Negra, a national holiday since Law 14,759/2023. That makes three days off from Nov 20 to 22.[6]

Is São Paulo worth it? The free expo at Praça das Artes runs on Nov 20 and 21 from 9 a.m.[7] It is aimed at adults, the Prefeitura mentions no children's program. For the expo alone, the 4 to 6 hour trip with four kids is hardly worth it. It is worth it if you are visiting family or friends in São Paulo anyway. HG Brasil already sees rain for Sat Nov 21 and Sun Nov 22, but that is a long-range forecast with low certainty.[2]

German-Brazilian corner

A word for next week: feriadão. It means a "big holiday", meaning a long weekend like Nov 20 to 22. Whoever has a feriadão often "emenda" another day, that is, "patches" a bridge day onto it. This time that is not necessary, the Friday itself is free.
