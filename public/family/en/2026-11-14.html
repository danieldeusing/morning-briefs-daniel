Health & safety

Mosquito season with El Niño The Paraná health department (Sesa) wrote in August: “hora de redobrar os cuidados contra a dengue, a zika e a chikungunya,” that is, time to be doubly careful.[9] According to Sesa, the epidemic years 2016, 2019, 2020 and 2024 show “uma correlação direta entre o El Niño e o aumento de casos.” In the worst year, 2024, 738 people died of dengue in Paraná. With 30 °C and showers, we now have exactly the breeding weather of the Aedes mosquito. After Sunday's shower: empty buckets, plant saucers, kiddie pools and toys in the yard. For the baby: a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
What does El Niño have to do with mosquitoes? El Niño is a warming in the Pacific that changes the weather on half the planet. Here it often brings more warmth and rain. The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs in small puddles, and in warm weather new mosquitoes hatch faster. More puddles plus more warmth means more mosquitoes, and more mosquitoes can bring more dengue.

What is the situation right now? We do not know. We found no current dengue figure with a calendar week for Paraná. The latest figure is old: the report that cases had fallen “de 52.193 para 2.028” compared with the previous year dates from 2026-04-15.[10] The page gives no calendar week, and the figure says nothing about today. Sesa published a new report on Nov. 12 about El Niño and a “Dia D” against dengue, but we could not open the text.[10]

Dia D on Nov. 21? By law, the National Day against Dengue is the second-to-last Saturday in November. In 2026 that would be Nov. 21. We calculated that ourselves; confirmation for 2026 is still missing.[11]

Not checked today: InfoGripe on respiratory viruses, closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092, the Paraná school calendar and the German school holidays.

Holidays & long weekend

  • Sun Nov 15: Proclamação da República. The holiday falls on a Sunday, so there is no extra day off.
  • Fri Nov 20: Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra. Law 14.759/2023 says: “Fica declarado feriado nacional o dia 20 de novembro.”[12] In 2026 that is a Friday, so three days off from Nov. 20 to 22.

Weather for the long weekend? HG Brasil sees rain every day from Nov. 20 to 22, at 20 to 28 °C on Nov. 20.[4] But that is only one source and a forecast six days out. Such values often change. We look again every day.

Is São Paulo worth it? The free expo at the Praça das Artes runs on Nov. 20 and 21 from 9 a.m.[7] The Prefeitura lists no children's program. For that alone, a 4 to 6 hour trip with four kids is hardly worth it. It is different if you are visiting family or friends in São Paulo anyway. We keep looking for something worthwhile closer to home.

German-Brazilian corner

For Nov. 20, one name is worth knowing: Zumbi dos Palmares. In the 17th century he led Palmares, a large settlement of people who had escaped slavery. The holiday carries his name: “Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra.” Consciência means “awareness,” so it is a day to remember the history of Black people in Brazil. For the big kids, this is a good question for the dinner table: what exactly are we celebrating on Friday?
