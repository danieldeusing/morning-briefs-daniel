Health & safety

Dengue season with El Niño The Paraná health department (Sesa) published a new notice on Nov 12: "Saúde alerta para efeitos do El Niño no aumento de casos de dengue e prepara Dia D de combate", that is, a warning of more dengue because of El Niño and preparation of an action day.[6] Back in August Sesa wrote: "hora de redobrar os cuidados contra a dengue, a zika e a chikungunya".[7] Warm and wet, like this week, is the Aedes mosquito's favorite weather. So empty standing water after every shower, and for the baby put a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
Why standing water? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs just above the waterline in small containers, such as a saucer. In warm weather the eggs turn into new mosquitoes in about 7 to 10 days.[12] Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle before a single mosquito can fly.

What is the situation right now? We don't know. We found no current dengue figure with an epidemiological week for Paraná, and we could not open the text of the Nov 12 notice.[6] The latest Sesa notice with figures is from April and belongs to the previous season. It says nothing about today, so we don't cite it.

When is the Dia D? That is not settled yet. The national day against dengue is the second-to-last Saturday in November, which in 2026 would be Nov 21. That is what Law 12,235/2010 sets; the date is not confirmed.[11] In 2025 the action was already on Nov 8, so the date is not fixed. As soon as Sesa names a date, it will appear here.

Not checked today: InfoGripe on respiratory viruses, closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092, the German school holidays.
