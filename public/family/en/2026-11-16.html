Holiday & Long Weekend

Fri 11/20 is the Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra. Law 14.759/2023 says: “Fica declarado feriado nacional o dia 20 de novembro”.[3] In 2026 that is a Friday, so three days off from Nov 20 to 22.

School on Friday? On a national holiday there is usually no school. We did not check the Paraná school calendar, so ask at the school briefly this week.

Health & Safety

Dengue: stay alert This week is warm and wet, the Aedes mosquito’s favorite weather. The health agency Sesa warned on Nov 12 about more dengue due to El Niño.[5] So after every shower, empty standing water, and for the baby, put a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.

What is the situation? There is no current figure. The latest report from Sesa is from 2026-04-15 and covers only EW 1–14/2026: 2,028 confirmed cases in Paraná, after 52,193 in the same period of 2025.[4] That was a very quiet start to the year, but it says nothing about today.

What does “SE 1–14” mean? Brazil counts disease cases by week, the “semanas epidemiológicas”. SE 1–14 are the first 14 weeks of the year, so roughly January to early April.

Dia D against dengue? Under Law 12.235/2010 the national day against dengue is the second-to-last Saturday in November, so Nov 21 in 2026.[7] A date for Paraná is not confirmed.
