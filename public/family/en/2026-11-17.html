Health & Safety

Dengue: El Niño brings risk The health agency Sesa warns that El Niño could push dengue cases up.[5] The next days are warm and wet, the Aedes mosquito's favorite weather. After every shower, empty standing water: plant saucers, buckets, toys in the garden. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
What is El Niño? The water in the Pacific gets warmer than normal, and that changes the weather across half the world. Like a heater running in the next room: you do not see it, but everything gets warmer. For southern Brazil this often means more rain and warmth, so more breeding spots for mosquitoes.

What is the situation? There is a fresh number now. In the first 44 weeks of the year (SE 1–44/2026), Paraná counted 6,380 confirmed dengue cases and 6 deaths. In 2025, 114 people died.[5] So the year has been very quiet. We did not find a number for Ribeirão Claro or Jacarezinho.

Dia D on Nov 28: Sesa now names the date for the big action day against dengue: "será realizado em 28 de novembro".[5] Yesterday we had only calculated Nov 21 from the law; Nov 28 replaces that.
