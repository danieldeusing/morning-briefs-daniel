Health & Safety

Dengue: rain plus heat The health agency Sesa warns: the more frequent and heavier rains from El Niño help the Aedes mosquito. Health Secretary César Neves says, "a curva de casos prováveis está acima do esperado," meaning the curve of suspected cases is above the expected value.[5] This week is exactly mosquito weather: warm and a shower almost every day. After every shower, empty standing water: plant saucers, buckets, toys in the garden. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
Why does water after the rain matter so much? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of still water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week a new mosquito flies out of it. Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle before a mosquito is born.

What is the situation? In the first 44 weeks of the dengue year, Paraná counted 6,380 confirmed cases, 93% fewer than in 2025. There were 6 deaths, versus 114 the year before. This is the data as of Nov 10, 2026.[5] The report does not name the exact calendar week; the dengue year in Paraná began on Jan 13, 2026. We found no figure for Ribeirão Claro or Jacarezinho.

Dia D on Sat Nov 28: According to Sesa-PR, Paraná's state health secretariat, that day is the action day against dengue, "que será realizado em 28 de novembro."[5] With the kids it is a good opportunity: go through the house and garden together and look for every spot with water. The big ones usually find that exciting.
