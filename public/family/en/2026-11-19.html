Weekend Plans: three days, lots of rain

Fri Nov 20 is the Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra, a national holiday since Law 14.759/2023. This year it falls on a Friday, so the family has three days off.[3] Today this comes from search snippets of two news sites; we cross-checked the law and the weekday.

We found no event for Friday through Sunday. We read 14 sources across all four rings, from Ribeirão Claro and Jacarezinho through Ourinhos and Londrina to the kids' guides for São Paulo. Most pages showed no readable dates. We would rather not invent anything. So here is a plan that fits the weather:

  • Out in the morning: The showers mostly come in the afternoon.[1] Playground, walk, garden, a short bike ride: best before noon. For the baby: shade, light clothing, nurse often. At 30 °C it gets too warm quickly.
  • Puddle tour on Friday: After a shower, head out in rubber boots. The big ones (8 and 7) measure with a cup how much rain fell. The two-year-old jumps along. This also fits the dengue topic below.
  • Indoors in the afternoon: Bake cookies, build a blanket cave, a movie with popcorn. Or a little theater with stuffed animals, as a rehearsal for "Casa de Brinquedos" a week later.
  • A holiday with meaning: Talk briefly with the big ones about who Zumbi dos Palmares was and why Brazil remembers him. A children's book or a short video from school helps.
Pro: no long drive with four kids on wet roads; the baby stays in rhythm; flexible with the weather.
Con: three days at home can get long; with no event there is no highlight.

A look at Instagram @secretariadeculturalondrina and at Sympla Londrina in the afternoon is worth it. For Ribeirão Claro and Jacarezinho: check the pages of the Prefeituras and the church communities. Whatever we find goes into tomorrow's brief.

Health & Safety

Respiratory: metapneumovirus is rising The Fiocruz bulletin InfoGripe of Nov 5, 2026 sees more severe respiratory cases from metapneumovirus, "entre eles São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Santa Catarina, Paraná". The data cover the week of Oct 25 to 31, 2026, by the calendar SE 43/2026 (the bulletin itself does not name the week).[6] Most common remains rhinovirus, the common-cold virus: 50.1% of positive samples of the last four weeks. RSV (VSR) is at 10.1%, Covid at 8.5%. Paraná is not on the list with rising Covid. For the one-month-old baby: wash hands, keep visitors with colds at a distance. With fast breathing, weak feeding or fever, see a doctor right away.
What is metapneumovirus? It is a relative of RSV. In older children and adults it usually causes just a cold. In babies, though, it can go deep into the small airways, as RSV does. A baby's small airways are very narrow, so even a little mucus makes breathing hard there.
Dengue: rain plus heat The health agency Sesa warns: the more frequent and heavier rains from El Niño help the Aedes mosquito. According to Sesa, the curve of suspected cases is above the expected value, so prevention must not slack off.[5] These four days are exactly mosquito weather: around 30 °C and a shower every day. After each shower, empty standing water: saucers, buckets, toys in the garden. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
Why does water after rain matter so much? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of still water, even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week a new mosquito flies out. Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle.

What is the situation? In the first 44 weeks of the 2026 dengue year, Paraná counted 6,380 confirmed cases, "93% a menos do que em 2025". There were 6 deaths, 114 the year before.[5] That is the status of the bulletin of Nov 10, 2026, by the calendar through SE 44/2026; the report itself only says "44 weeks". We found no figure for Ribeirão Claro or Jacarezinho.

Dia D on Sat Nov 28: On this day Paraná holds its action day against dengue.[5] With the kids it is a good opportunity: walk through the house and garden together and look for every water spot. The big ones usually find such a "mosquito detective" round exciting.

Not checked: We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092 today. Before a trip, take a quick look in a maps app, especially after heavy rain.

German-Brazilian

November 20 is a good bridge between two cultures for the kids. In Germany there is no holiday for it. In Brazil the day remembers Zumbi dos Palmares, who in the 17th century led a free village of escaped slaves. A nice word for the family to learn: "consciência", awareness.
