Weekend Plans: Londrina Delivered

Fri 11/20 is the Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra, a national holiday since Law 14.759/2023.[4] The business association ACIL published the culture agenda for the long weekend in Londrina on 11/19.[5] For the family, these are the best picks:

  • Today, Dia de Zumbi festival (11 AM–10 PM, Sabor & Ar): "aulão de samba, trancistas, Projeto Crespinhos, Mostra Cultural, Feirinha Black Divas", that is, a big samba class, braiders, a project about curly kids' hair, a cultural showcase and a small market. Free.[5] Leave after breakfast and you arrive by midday. It fits the meaning of the holiday exactly.
  • Today, "Pedro e o Lobo" (10:30 AM, Londrina Country Club): The UEL symphony orchestra plays the classic children's piece "Peter and the Wolf" outdoors.[5] Each animal is an instrument, a great first concert for the big kids. Honestly: from Ribeirão Claro, 10:30 AM is tight, and the price is not listed (info via @londrinacountryclub).
  • Sat 11/21, "Caipira Substantivo Feminino" (5:00 PM, Sesc Cadeião): a musical by an all-women team, a tribute to the Caipira singers. Free.[5] This is more for the big kids and the parents; the two-year-old will probably need breaks.
  • Sun 11/22, "Sarau da Disney" + Artium Festival: our pick, see above. If you still have time on Sunday: at 5:00 PM the ballet "A Praça de Sevilha" dances at the Teatro Mãe de Deus, tickets from R$ 30 via Sympla.[5]

The ACIL agenda gives no age rating for a single event, only "diferentes perfis e idades".[5] The suitability in the table is therefore our take.

Rather stay home? In Ribeirão Claro, Jacarezinho and Ourinhos we found no family event this weekend. Nor for Greater São Paulo. Yesterday's plan then still holds: out in the morning, indoors in the afternoon, a puddle tour after the shower, baking cookies, a blanket fort.

Pro Londrina: lots of program, almost all free, much of it under one roof; a real highlight for the big kids.
Con Londrina: long drive, rain risk on the road on Sunday; a day in the car is tiring for the baby. A compromise: one parent goes with the big kids.

Save the date: On Sat 11/28 and Sun 11/29 the children's theater "Casa de Brinquedos" runs at the Ouro Verde, 4:00 to 5:30 PM each day, rating "LIVRE". Sympla lists no price.[6]

Health & Safety

Respiratory: Metapneumovirus is rising The Fiocruz InfoGripe bulletin of 11/05/2026 sees more severe respiratory cases (SRAG) from metapneumovirus, rhinovirus and coronavirus. Paraná is on the list of states with more metapneumovirus. The data cover the week of 10/25 to 10/31/2026, by the calendar SE 43/2026; the bulletin itself does not name the week.[8] Important for us: "crianças de até 2 anos apresentam as maiores taxas de incidência de SRAG", that is, children up to 2 years old are most often severely affected. For us, that is the baby at one month and the two-year-old. On outings to crowded halls: wash hands, keep distance from coughing people. If the baby breathes fast, drinks weakly or has a fever, see a doctor right away.
What is metapneumovirus? It is a relative of RSV. In big kids it usually causes just a cold. In babies, though, it can go deep into the small airways. In a baby these are as narrow as a drinking straw, so even a little mucus makes breathing hard.

Two sources, two pictures: The Health Ministry writes in its report for SE 44/2026 of a "queda consistente nas internações por SRAG", that is, fewer hospital admissions nationwide.[9] We know this report only from a search snippet. Both can be true: fewer overall, but certain viruses and regions rising. For the baby, caution is the right choice.

Dengue: rain plus heat The health agency Sesa warns that El Niño rains help the Aedes mosquito.[7] This weekend is exactly mosquito weather: around 30 °C and showers. After every shower, empty standing water: plant saucers, buckets, toys in the yard. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
Why does water matter so much after rain? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of still water, even a bottle cap's worth is enough. After about a week a new mosquito flies out. Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle.

What is the situation? Paraná counted 6,380 confirmed cases in 2026 "entre as 44 semanas do período epidemiológico", that is, in the 44 weeks of the current dengue period, 93% fewer than in 2025. There were 6 deaths, versus 114 the year before.[7] This is the status of the survey of 11/10/2026, reported on 11/12; the report names no calendar week. The Londrina region has the most cases; there, Wolbachia mosquitoes have been released since late October. We found no figure for Ribeirão Claro.

Dia D on Sat 11/28: the action day against dengue in Paraná.[7] With the big kids it becomes a "mosquito detective" round through the house and yard.

Not checked: closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Before driving to Londrina, take a quick look at the maps app, especially after rain.

Good morning, it's Friday, November twentieth, the Consciência Negra holiday. The long weekend begins, and at last there's something to do: Londrina has put out its agenda, and almost everything on it is free.

Our pick is Sunday in Londrina. At three in the afternoon, Funcart stages a Disney Sarau with musical scenes from Disney films. On top of that, the Artium Festival, with art and technology, runs all weekend at the Museu Histórico. Both are free.

Today in Londrina there's a big Dia de Zumbi festival, from eleven in the morning to ten at night, with a samba class and a project for kids. On Saturday at five p.m., Sesc Cadeião puts on a musical about Caipira singers, also free.

On the weather, the sources don't agree. Today will be warm, around thirty degrees, with brief showers. For Saturday, two sources say rain and one says mostly dry. On Sunday, all of them see rain. One source shows only twenty-four degrees, the others thirty. So plan flexibly.

On health: according to Fiocruz, there are more severe respiratory cases from metapneumovirus, including in Paraná, and children up to age two are hit hardest. In our house, that's the baby and the little one. So wash hands and keep your distance from anyone coughing. On dengue, Paraná is quiet, but after every shower, please empty any standing water. The dengue action day is on November twenty-eighth.

Have a wonderful holiday and a happy long weekend, everyone.
