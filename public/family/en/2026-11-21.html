Health & safety

Dengue: rain plus heat The health agency Sesa warns of "chuvas mais frequentes e volumosas causadas pelo El Niño" that help the Aedes mosquito. And: "a curva de casos prováveis está acima do esperado," the curve of suspected cases is above expectations.[5] This weekend is exactly mosquito weather: around 30 °C, plus showers. After every shower, empty standing water: saucers, buckets, toys in the garden. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
Why does the water matter so much after rain? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of still water. Even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week a new mosquito flies out of it. Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle.

What is the situation? In 2026 Paraná counted "entre as 44 semanas do período epidemiológico," that is, in the 44 weeks of the current dengue period, 6,380 confirmed cases, 93% fewer than in 2025. There were 6 deaths, versus 114 the year before.[5] That is the Sesa report of 11/12/2026; it names no calendar week (SE), and we found no newer bulletin. A media report gives 5,324 cases through 11/10; we know that figure only from a search snippet and it probably counts differently. We found no figure for Ribeirão Claro, Jacarezinho or the 17th Regional.

Dia D on Sat 11/28: the dengue action day in Paraná.[5] With the big ones it becomes a "mosquito detective" round through house and garden, with a checklist.

Respiratory: Yesterday we reported more metapneumovirus cases according to Fiocruz, including in Paraná. Nothing new on that today. For outings to crowded halls the rule still holds: wash hands, keep distance from coughing people, especially with the baby and the two-year-old.

Not checked: closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Before the drive to Londrina, take a quick look at the maps app, especially after rain.
