Health & Safety

Dengue: a rainy week with warmth The health agency Sesa warns that El Niño rain helps the Aedes mosquito.[5] Four rainy days at up to 30 °C are exactly mosquito weather. After every shower, empty standing water: saucers, buckets, toys in the yard, gutters. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
Why does water matter so much after rain? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of still water. Even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week, a new mosquito flies out of it. Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle.

What is the situation? In 2026, Paraná counted “entre as 44 semanas do período epidemiológico”, that is, in the 44 weeks of the current dengue period, 6,380 confirmed cases, 93% fewer than in 2025, and 6 deaths.[5] That is the Sesa report from 2026-11-12; it gives no epidemiological week (SE), and we found no newer bulletin. Be careful with the year-on-year comparison: the Sesa page gives 114 deaths in 2025, a media report 90. We cannot resolve this gap. We have no figure for Ribeirão Claro or Jacarezinho.

Dia D on Sat Nov 28: the day of action against dengue in Paraná, “que será realizado em 28 de novembro”.[5] With the older kids this becomes a “mosquito detective” round through house and yard, with plenty to find after a rainy week.

Roads: We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Before driving to Londrina, take a quick look at a maps app, especially in the rain.
