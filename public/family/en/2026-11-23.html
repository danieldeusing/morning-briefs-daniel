Health & Safety

Dengue: another rainy week with warmth The health agency Sesa warns: because of El Niño it rains more often and harder, and that helps the Aedes mosquito. The curve of probable cases is "acima do esperado," i.e. above what was expected.[5] Four rainy days at up to 30 °C are mosquito weather. After every shower, empty standing water: saucers, buckets, toys in the yard, gutters. For the baby, a mosquito net over the crib and stroller.
How does a puddle become a mosquito? The Aedes mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of still water. Even a bottle cap full is enough. After about a week, a new mosquito flies out. Whoever empties the water beforehand breaks the cycle.

What is the situation? In the "44 semanas do período epidemiológico," that is, the 44 weeks of the current dengue period, Paraná counted 6,380 confirmed cases in 2026, 93% fewer than in 2025. There were 6 deaths, after 114 the year before.[5] That is the Sesa survey from 2026-11-10, reported on 11/12; it gives no calendar week (SE). We found no newer bulletin, and we have no figures for Ribeirão Claro or Jacarezinho.

Dia D on Sat 11/28: the "Dia D de Mobilização Nacional contra a dengue."[5] For the big ones it becomes a mosquito-detective round: who finds the most water hiding spots in the house and yard? After this rainy week there is plenty to find.
