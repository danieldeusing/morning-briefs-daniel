Health & Safety

Respiratory: Covid rising slightly, keep an eye on the baby The Health Ministry's InfoGripe bulletin (SE 44/2026, 2026-11-07) reports more severe Covid cases in some states in the south and center, "DF, MG, SP, RJ, PR." Hospital admissions "seguem baixas," so they remain low. Influenza A is near zero, RSV at a minimum, "sem nenhum sinal de tendência de aumento."[6] We have no numbers for Paraná alone. We found no InfoGripe bulletin newer than SE 44 (2026-11-07). According to Band (2026-11-10), cases with metapneumovirus are also rising in Paraná and Santa Catarina; we could not open the article.[8]
SRAG: That is what Brazil calls a severe respiratory illness in which someone has to go to the hospital. It is like the difference between a cold and pneumonia: only the severe cases are counted.
Metapneumovirus: A cold virus, a close relative of RSV. In older kids it is usually just a cold; in very small babies it can hit the airways harder.

What does this mean for us? No alarm, but with a one-month-old baby, caution is worthwhile: wash hands when the big ones come home from school, and visitors with colds keep their distance from the baby. If the baby breathes fast or drinks poorly, go to the doctor right away.

Dengue: rainy week at 30 °C Paraná is quiet in 2026: 6,380 confirmed cases in the "44 semanas do período epidemiológico," 93% fewer than in 2025, and 6 deaths versus 114 the year before. But the health agency Sesa warns of El Niño: lots of rain and warmth help the Aedes mosquito, and the curve of probable cases is "acima do esperado."[5] These are the figures from 2026-11-10, reported on 2026-11-12; we found no newer bulletin. After every shower, empty standing water, and use a mosquito net for the baby.

Dia D on Sat 11/28: the "Dia D de Mobilização Nacional contra a dengue."[5] After three rainy days, there is plenty to find in the yard. A mosquito-detective round in the morning, then off to the theater.

Roads: We have not checked closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Take a quick look at the maps app before driving to Londrina.

Good thing our pick is indoors: the children's theater Casa de Brinquedos at Ouro Verde in Londrina, Saturday at four p.m., for all ages. There's a second show Sunday at the same time. Please check the price on Sympla beforehand.

New today is a look at respiratory illness. According to the Health Ministry, severe Covid cases are growing in some states in the south and center, including Paraná, but hospital admissions remain low. Flu and RSV are barely showing up. With a baby in the house, a little caution still makes sense: wash hands when the big kids get home from school, and keep visitors with colds away.
