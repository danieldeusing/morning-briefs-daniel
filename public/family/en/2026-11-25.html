Health & Safety

Dengue: few cases, but the curve is rising Paraná is quiet in 2026: in the "44 semanas do período epidemiológico" (≈ SE 44/2026) the health agency Sesa confirmed 6,380 cases, about 93% fewer than in 2025. There were also 6 deaths, after 114 the year before.[6] Still, Sesa warns that the curve of probable cases is "acima do esperado considerando a avaliação da média histórica," that is, above what is usual for this time of year. And the rain from El Niño is "propícia para a proliferação do Aedes aegypti."[6] The numbers are from 11/10/2026, reported on 11/12; we found no newer bulletin and no numbers for the Norte Pioneiro.
El Niño: One part of the Pacific gets warmer than usual, and that shifts the weather across half the world. Like a heater in the next room that also warms up our room: in southern Brazil, El Niño often brings more rain.
Aedes cycle: The dengue mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of standing water. A forgotten saucer becomes a mosquito nursery in a few days. Whoever empties the water stops the cycle before a single mosquito flies.

What does this mean for us? No alarm, but now is the moment. Sesa advises that "os cuidados devem ser redobrados para evitar o acúmulo de água parada."[6] So after every shower, take a quick walk around the yard, and put a mosquito net over the baby's bed.

Dia D on Sat 11/28: On the "Dia D de Mobilização Nacional contra a dengue," Sesa plans clean-up actions, educational activities and awareness across the country.[6] We found no program from the Prefeitura Ribeirão Claro.

Wolbito in Londrina: According to Sesa, Londrina began releasing "Wolbito" mosquitoes at the end of October.[6]

Wolbito: Mosquitoes that carry a harmless bacterium called Wolbachia. The bacterium blocks the dengue virus inside the mosquito. It is a bit like vaccinating the mosquitoes themselves: they still bite, but hardly pass on the virus.

Respiratory illness: Nothing new today on flu, RSV or Covid. We had the latest status (InfoGripe SE 44/2026) yesterday.

Roads: We did not check closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Before the drive to Londrina, especially in rain, take a quick look at the maps app.
