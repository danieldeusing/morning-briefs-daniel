Health & Safety

Dengue: few cases, but the curve is rising Paraná is quiet in 2026: in the "44 semanas do período epidemiológico" (SE 44/2026), the health authority Sesa confirmed 6,380 cases, about 93% fewer than in 2025. Add 6 deaths, versus 114 the year before.[7] Even so, Sesa warns: "a curva de casos prováveis está acima do esperado", meaning above what is usual for this time of year. More heavy rain from El Niño helps the Aedes mosquito.[7] The figures are from 11/10/2026, reported on 11/12. We found no newer bulletin and no figures for Ribeirão Claro or Jacarezinho.
El Niño: One part of the Pacific becomes warmer than usual, and that shifts the weather across half the world. Like a heater in the next room that also warms our room: in southern Brazil, El Niño often brings more rain.
Aedes cycle: The dengue mosquito lays its eggs at the edge of standing water. A forgotten plant saucer becomes a flying mosquito in a little over a week. Whoever pours out the water stops the cycle before it flies.

What does that mean for us? No alarm, but good timing. According to both portals, a wet week follows the weekend (see below). Every container we empty and turn over on Saturday will not fill up again this week. Climatempo rates the mosquito situation this weekend as "Alto", meaning high.[3] For the baby: a mosquito net over the bed and stroller.

Sunlight: Climatempo shows a UV index of "Extremo" for the weekend.[3] So even between the showers: hat, shade, and keep the baby out of direct sun.

Outlook: a lot of rain around December 3 and 4 Both portals see rain every day next week and a peak at the end. HG Brasil expects 42.9 mm for Ribeirão Claro on Fri 12/4, Climatempo 61.6 mm for Jacarezinho; on Thu 12/3 11.6 mm and 31.7 mm respectively.[1][2] That is a week away, and forecasts that far ahead are uncertain. We check every day. If it holds: better not to schedule trips to Londrina on those days.

Respiratory: Nothing new today on flu, RSV or Covid. We had the latest status (InfoGripe SE 44/2026) on Tuesday.

Roads: We have not checked closures on BR-153, BR-369 and PR-092. Before the drive to Londrina on Sunday, take a quick look in the maps app.

German-Brazilian Corner

After the first Sunday of Advent, St. Nicholas arrives just a week later: on the evening of 12/5, children in Germany put their boots outside the door, and on the morning of 12/6 there is something inside. In Brazil the custom is little known, but it suits children with two cultures. A word for the older ones: "São Nicolau", Saint Nicholas. And whoever lights the first candle on Sunday says in Portuguese: "o primeiro domingo do Advento".

Today stays gray with a bit of rain and a high of twenty-one to twenty-four degrees Celsius. Not much water is coming, though, just one to three millimeters. For tomorrow, Friday, the portals disagree: HG Brasil sees no rain at all, Climatempo a fifty percent chance, but hardly any volume. So an afternoon at the playground is quite possible.

On Saturday, according to Climatempo, the showers arrive in the afternoon and evening, with the chance of rain between eighty-six and one hundred percent. On Sunday it's seventy-three to seventy-seven percent, with brief showers. On temperature the portals part ways, anywhere from twenty-three to twenty-seven degrees.

That's why our pick is now Sunday, the first Sunday of Advent. At the Ouro Verde in Londrina, children from the municipal dance school perform the show Casa de Brinquedos at four p.m., open to all ages. It also runs on Saturday at the same time. Sympla shows no price, so please check before you buy.

Saturday morning, before the rain, is for the national Dengue D-Day. Paraná is quiet this year, with six thousand three hundred eighty confirmed cases, about ninety-three percent fewer than last year. But the health authority warns that the curve is above the usual level, and El Niño rain helps the mosquito. So go out in the yard with the older kids, find standing water and pour it out.

And a look ahead: both portals see rain every day next week, peaking on Thursday and Friday, December third and fourth. On Friday it could be more than forty millimeters. That's still far off and uncertain, and we'll check every day.
