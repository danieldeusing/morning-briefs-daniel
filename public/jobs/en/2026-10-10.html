Rate picture: €90 is the median, not the target [1][2][3][4]

The new number: in September 2026, freelancermap analyzed the hourly rates of 2,337 active freelancer profiles in the DACH region. Result: "Freelance software developers charge a median of €90 net per hour."[2] In Germany and Austria it is €90 each, in Switzerland €129.

The comparison with other IT segments: the Freelancer-Kompass 2026 (July) puts all IT fields at €95/h. Software and web development form the low end at €90; SAP/ERP consultants lead with €120, Data & Analytics sits at €100.[3] The Java profile page at freelancermap shows an average of €87/h, without a cutoff date.[4]

Median: Line up all freelancers by their price. The median is the person exactly in the middle. Half charge more, half charge less — from beginner to architect.

The board: 35 entries carry a researched euro hourly band. The midpoint of these bands is €97.5/h.[1] Not one of these rates comes from the client — the asterisk * means "estimated".

My verdict: the target of €100/h holds, but sits above the middle — so Daniel has to be better than half the market and show that in the pitch. Anyone who offers as a "web developer" lands in the cheapest segment. Anyone who offers backend (Spring), frontend (Angular) and cloud from a single source replaces two people. That is the argument for the premium. Switzerland remains the most expensive market at €129, but on the board it almost always requires on-site work.
