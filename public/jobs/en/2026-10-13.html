Person-day (PD) and reference: A PD is one full working day of one person. 692 PD over three years is almost full-time. A reference is a past project you name as proof — with client, period and technology. Without the required number, a bid is often rejected already in the formal review.

My verdict: The stack fits in the middle (Java, Spring Boot, Angular, Oracle, PostgreSQL, REST), less so at the edges (JSF, Quarkus, JBoss, OpenShift, JMS/MQ). The problem is time, not technology: nobody writes 20 references in three hours. If a reference package is ready, bid — at €95–100/h remote. That is slightly below the rate for One Day Ahead, because the role is called "Intermediate" and brings three years of volume. Beforehand, ask the contact in one line whether twiceD is admitted as a GmbH. Without a finished package: skip it. The €90–115/h* band on the board is an estimate.[1][4]
