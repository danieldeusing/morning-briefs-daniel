Required: Kotlin, Linux in production, BLE, LoRaWAN, Wireless M-Bus, MQTT and Kubernetes. Rate and start date are not in the listing.[3]

Why this is worth more than the fit score: BLE, MQTT and M-Bus are on the CV, plus smart-home references at manufacturers. Kotlin is new, but runs on the same JVM as Java and Spring. LoRaWAN is missing from the CV. The score of 56 counts the rate for only 8 points because none is stated.[1]

Wireless M-Bus: A radio standard that electricity, water and heat meters use to send their readings. Instead of someone reading every meter, the meter "calls in" its reading to a gateway — and the software behind that is exactly the part GROPYUS is looking for.

New: PAR GmbH, Senior Java Backend Developer – Cloud Native & DevOps (Fit 57): Start 11/2026, 25 months, 100% allocation, "60% remote, 40% onsite in Frankfurt am Main". Stack: Java 17+, Spring Boot, Spring Security, Kubernetes or OpenShift, PostgreSQL, ideally AWS. Apply button active, no rate. Contract type: temp-agency staffing (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung).[2]

Temp-agency staffing (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung, ANÜ): The intermediary employs you and lends you out to the client. You are then a temporary employee, not a company sending an invoice. For twiceD that means: no engagement for the company, and 40% presence in Frankfurt does not fit a residence in Brazil.

My verdict: Write to GROPYUS today and set the rate yourself: €110/h as an opening.
