The two exclusions. mund literally states "FREELANCER: NICHT VORGESEHEN!" (freelancers not accepted); the page was last edited on Aug 17, 2026. One Day Ahead (Agentic/Kafka) says: "Aus Compliance-Gründen können nur festangestellte Mitarbeiter von Systemhäusern berücksichtigt werden!" (for compliance reasons, only permanent employees of system houses can be considered)[2] Neither page says whether twiceD, as a GmbH with one managing director, falls under that. I would doubt it.

"Only employees of system houses": The client does not want an individual freelancer, but an IT firm that sends one of its own permanent employees. The reason is usually the risk of false self-employment — if a freelancer works like an employee for a long time, the client may have to pay back social security contributions. With a company in between, the company carries that risk.

Start date passed, page open. According to their pages, four projects should have started between May and September. Either they are unfilled, or the listing was never closed. From the outside you cannot tell the difference.[2]
