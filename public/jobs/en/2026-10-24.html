Bidder consortium: Two companies submit one joint bid and split the work. Each brings the proofs for its own part. twiceD supplies the technology, the partner the government reference — like two tradespeople teaming up for one job.

Scale (my calculation): €88,235.29 is exactly €105,000 divided by 1.19 — probably a gross budget of €105,000 before VAT. At €100/h that is about 880 hours. That is a job one or two people can carry.

The gap: twiceD probably has a reference for private-sector AI applications. A public-sector one is not visible in the CV. That is the part the partner would supply. It remains open whether the documents (Part D 3.2) do name a revenue threshold after all; that part was not opened.[3][6]

My verdict: The best-fitting tender in today's search window — applied AI, web app, small volume. On Monday, pull the tender documents and approach two or three firms with government references. Four weeks is enough for a joint bid, but not if you wait.
