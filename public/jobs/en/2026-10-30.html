The market is stagnating: what that means for your rate [2][3][4][5]

The Freelancer-Kompass is freelancermap's big annual survey. For 2026 it reports an average of €103 per hour, one euro less than the year before.[2] The figures for this profile are lower:

Average or median? Picture ten freelancers in a row, sorted by price. The median is the price in the middle of the row. The average adds up all the prices and divides by ten. One very expensive consultant pulls the average up, but barely moves the median. That is why in IT an average of €97 sits next to a median of €95.[3]

My verdict: €100/h for Senior Java/Angular is still right. That is €10 above the software development median and €13 above the Java average — defensible for 13 years of experience.[3][4] Cutting the rate gains little: the median figures mix junior and senior. For IoT, stay at €110/h, because that niche is rarely advertised.

What changes is the pace. A flat market means more applicants per project. According to a search snippet, monthly project income fell from €8,022 to €6,653.[5] The snippet does not name the cause; I did not open the page. Replying on day one to "ASAP" projects wins more than a €5 discount.
