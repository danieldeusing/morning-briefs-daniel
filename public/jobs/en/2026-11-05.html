€90/h is the median — €100/h is a statement [4][5]

In September, freelancermap analyzed 2,337 active profiles of freelance software developers. Result: the median ask is €90 net per hour, excluding VAT. By technology, almost everything falls between €85 and €90; Java sits in this corridor, with no figure of its own.[4] The Freelancer-Kompass 2026 reports €103/h — but as an average across all fields, down from €104 the year before.[5]

Median vs. average: Line up 100 freelancers in order — the median is the rate of the person in the middle. A few very expensive consultants pull the average up, but hardly move the median. That makes the median the more honest "normal".

Important: these are asking rates from profiles, not paid ones. Yesterday €100/h sat in the middle of a band with no methodology; with the new figure it is €10 above the competition's midpoint.[4]

My verdict: Stay at €100/h for Java/Angular. The justification belongs in the pitch: fullstack from one source, 13+ years, available immediately. For Amoria Bond ask €110/h, because IoT protocols plus Java are rare. €90/h is the floor — below that you are just the median.
