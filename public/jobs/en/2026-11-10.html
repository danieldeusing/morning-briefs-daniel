€95 /h median, weaker order situation — why €100 /h stays the starting figure [3][4]

The Freelancer Compass 2026 gives IT freelancers a median of €95 /h. SAP/ERP leads at €120 /h. 56 % of respondents see the order situation as worse than a year ago.[3] The September profile data show €90 net for software development, almost the same across all common languages.[4]

Weak order situation: Like a market with many stalls and few customers. Customers can pick and push the price down. Whoever offers something rare still sells well — whoever offers the same as everyone else has to get cheaper.

So Java earns no premium: the language alone does not lift the rate above the corridor.[4] The profile makes the difference: full stack from one person, 13+ years, available immediately.

My verdict: Start at €100 /h for Java/Angular — a good 5 % above the IT median. For 100 % remote and a term of 12 months or more, €95 /h is a reasonable close. For IoT projects such as Amoria Bond IoT (71), quote €110 /h. Decline anything below €90 /h.
