€103 average, slightly down — why €100/h stays the starting point [4]

According to the Freelancer Compass 2026, the fee averages €103 per hour. The year before it was €104. The source gives no IT figure and no number of participants. As of March 2026.[4]

Average across all industries: It mixes copywriters, consultants and developers together. One euro less means: prices are no longer rising, but they are not falling sharply either. For senior Java developers the number shows only the direction, not the price.

My verdict: Start at €100/h for Java/Angular. With 100 % remote and a long duration, €95/h is a defensible close. For IoT projects like Amoria Bond (71), quote €110/h. Decline below €90/h. These are my thresholds from the target band, not from a source today.
