Market: rates hold, hours are missing [6][7][8]

Two sources, two numbers: freelancermap gives a median of around €90 net, the Freelancer-Kompass an average of €103.[6][7] That is not a contradiction. The median is the middle; the average is pulled upward by a few top rates. The two numbers also measure different things: the €90 comes from profile data, the €103 from a survey. The Freelancer-Kompass is a survey run by freelancermap itself, and freelancermap’s own blog post repeats the €103 and the −17 %[8] — so these are not independent confirmations. There is no separate, verified value for Java.

Median vs. average: Ten people stand in a row by height. The median is the person in the middle. If a basketball pro joins, the middle barely changes — the average jumps up.

The third number matters more: project revenue per month fell 17 % to €6,653, with a nearly unchanged hourly rate.[8] So freelancers earn less because they sell fewer hours, not because they are cheaper.

My verdict: Do not give way on the rate — €100/h for Java/Angular, €110 for IoT. Be flexible on duration and start: a secure six-month project from January is worth more in this market than €5 more per hour. Accept €95 at 100 % remote and a long duration; decline below €90.
