The start comes "at the latest 2 weeks after award". That sounds like a bidder collecting profiles for a public tender — my reading, not from the page. The start then depends on whether HyPlus wins the contract.[2]

Bund-ID, ELSTER, FitConnect: These are three building blocks that let citizens reach government agencies online. The Bund-ID is the central citizen account for logging in. ELSTER is the tax administration's online portal. FitConnect securely transports applications from the online form to the responsible agency. Anyone who has worked with them knows the interfaces and validation rules.
Temporary agency work vs. service project: With temporary agency work, a company "lends" you out, and the client gives you the instructions. The lender needs a license under the AÜG (German Temporary Employment Act) for this. With a service project, twiceD owes a result and manages the work itself. The page mentions both — that needs to be clarified before any offer.

My verdict: Only reach out if a CV entry honestly shows one year of work with Bund-ID, ELSTER or FitConnect. Otherwise skip it — references are checked in processes like this. If yes: quote €800 per day (€100/h) and ask in the first line whether the contract is a service project without temporary agency work. The rate is my threshold, not from a source.

Skills gap: Bitkom asks companies how many IT positions they cannot fill. The total is the gap. When it shrinks, companies have more choice. Then they reply more slowly, and standard profiles compete harder.

Rates hold up anyway: €103 in 2026 versus €104 in 2025.[4] For Java, freelancermap gives a profile average of €87/h. That is an undated snippet, and profile figures are not closed contracts.[5] So the two numbers do not measure the same thing.

My verdict: €100/h remains a defensible starting point for Java/Angular, but not as a standard profile. More choice for the client means the first sentence of the email has to say what others do not have — IoT protocols, agentic coding, fast start. For a pure Java role without such a lever, €90–95/h is more realistic. That is my assessment from the numbers above, not a source.
