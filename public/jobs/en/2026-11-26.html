The market is getting tighter: −22% IT openings [4][5][8]

At the Federal Employment Agency in 2025 there were "about 13,000 job openings for IT occupations on file", down 22% from the year before. The unemployment rate in ICT occupations rose from 3.7% to 4.5%, according to the agency (cio.de/dpa, Jul 3, 2026).[4]

Bitkom shows the same direction from the other side. On Sep 3, 2026 the association reported 79,000 missing IT specialists. In 2025 it was 109,000, in 2023 still 149,000 — almost half as many in three years.[5] AI has not been a big driver so far: only 10% of companies that use AI in IT have cut IT jobs "here and there". But 32% of companies with IT jobs expect cuts through AI in the future.[5] On Nov 10, 2026, IG Metall writes of "jobs gone, sites closed" at engineering service providers and IT firms — we know that only from a search snippet.[8]

Skills gap: Imagine a market where companies want to buy apples. In 2023, 149,000 apples were missing, today only 79,000. So buyers have to fight less, and sellers can ask for less. For you as a seller, that means a standard apple has a harder time finding a buyer than three years ago.

My verdict: That explains why the board has had hardly any new listings for days. An intermediary has more profiles per project today than in 2023. So the first line of the email matters: IoT protocols, automotive reference or agentic coding up front, not "13 years of Java". That is my reading of the numbers above, not a source.
