Vocabulary for End of Support: abkündigen, descontinuar, umstellen

Tuesday, October 27 — rotation topic Software & Technology, episode thirteen, and the first language issue after the break since August 13. The trigger sits in today's Software brief: Node.js 20 gets no fix for the Brotli vulnerability because that line has reached its end of life. So today we learn the vocabulary of the software lifecycle: what it means when a vendor abkündigt (announces the discontinuation of) something, when Support-Ende (end of support) is, and how to say "we are switching over to the new version" (wir stellen um). Plus a false friend that lurks in every Brazilian ticket: in the dictionary, suportar also means "to bear" or "to put up with". 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 3 refreshers from earlier Software episodes.

  • FALSE FRIEND suportar ≠ to support — the Priberam dictionary gives, as its second meaning among others, "Aguentar ou permitir algo desagradável. = TOLERAR". If you mean "We support Python 3.15", better say damos suporte ao Python 3.15. priberam.org
  • CONJUGATION descontinúo vs. descontinuo — same verb, same stress on the u. Spanish writes the accent (descontinúo, descontinúan), Portuguese does not (descontinuo, descontinuam). reverso.net · conjugacao.com.br
  • CONJUGATION actualicé, but atualizei — in Spanish, z before e becomes c: actualicé, actualice, actualicemos. Portuguese has no such change: atualizei, atualize. reverso.net · conjugacao.com.br
  • CONJUGATION abkündigen and umstellen are separable — der Hersteller kündigt die Version ab, wir stellen auf Node 22 um; in the participle the ge- is wedged into the middle: abgekündigt, umgestellt. Both take haben. verbformen.de · verbformen.de
  • BR-PT a tela, o arquivo — in Portugal the screen is o ecrã and the file is o ficheiro. The Portuguese Priberam dictionary itself names the Brazilian counterpart arquivo under ficheiro. priberam.org · priberam.org
  • IDIOM em time que está ganhando não se mexe — the Brazilian soccer version of "Never change a running system": you don't touch a team that is winning. substack.com

Three Verbs: abkündigen, umstellen, aktualisieren

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

Why Spanish writes an accent and Portuguese does not: In descontinuar, two vowels meet: u and a (or o). Normally u and a following vowel merge into one syllable, as in agua. In yo descontinúo, however, the stress falls on the u, and the two vowels stay apart. Spanish shows this split with the accent: descontinúo, descontinúas, descontinúan. When the stress moves back, the accent disappears: nosotros descontinuamos. Portuguese pronounces it the same way but writes no accent: eu descontinuo, eles descontinuam. Rule of thumb: Same sound, only Spanish makes it visible.
Verb DE weak, separable (ab-) → participle abgekündigt, perfect with haben · PT-BR descontinuar, regular -ar · ES-MX descontinuar, accent u→ú under stress (descontinúo, but descontinuamos)
abkündigen

Der Hersteller kündigt die alte Version zum Jahresende ab.

Das Produkt wurde schon vor zwei Jahren abgekündigt.

Wenn sie die API abkündigen, brauchen wir einen Plan B.

to discontinue

The vendor is discontinuing the old version at the end of the year.

The product was discontinued two years ago.

If they discontinue the API, we need a plan B.

descontinuar

O fabricante vai descontinuar a versão antiga no fim do ano.

O produto foi descontinuado há dois anos.

Se eles descontinuarem a API, precisamos de um plano B.

descontinuar

El fabricante va a descontinuar la versión antigua a fin de año.

El producto se descontinuó hace dos años.

Si descontinúan la API, necesitamos un plan B.

Abkündigen is not abschalten Abkündigen means: the vendor announces that a product is being phased out. It often keeps running for months afterward, just without further development. Abschalten (shutting down) is the last step, when the service is really off. English draws a similar line: to deprecate marks something as outdated, to discontinue ends the offering. For a customer email in PT-BR, descontinuar is usually enough; the participle descontinuado often works like an adjective: um produto descontinuado.

DE: separable, stressed ab- — in a main clause the prefix goes to the end (ich kündige … ab), in the participle the ge- sits in the middle: abgekündigt. Auxiliary haben. Verb DE weak, separable (um-) → participle umgestellt, perfect with haben, usually with auf + acc. · PT-BR mudar (para), regular -ar · ES-MX cambiar(se) (a), regular -ar
umstellen

Wir stellen alle Dienste bis Dezember auf Node 22 um.

Letztes Jahr haben wir schon die Datenbank umgestellt.

Stellst du dein Projekt auch um?

to switch (over)

We are switching all services over to Node 22 by December.

Last year we already switched the database.

Are you switching your project over too?

mudar (para)

Vamos mudar todos os serviços para o Node 22 até dezembro.

No ano passado já mudamos o banco de dados.

Você também vai mudar o seu projeto?

cambiar(se) (a)

Vamos a cambiar todos los servicios a Node 22 antes de diciembre.

El año pasado ya cambiamos la base de datos.

¿Tú también vas a cambiar tu proyecto?

One German verb, many meanings Umstellen also means "to rearrange furniture" or "to change the clock" — always: to move something to another place or into another state. In the software sense, auf almost always follows: auf die neue Version umstellen. PT-BR and ES use the everyday verb for "to change" here: mudar para, cambiar a. Careful: mudar-se means "to move house" in Portuguese (into a new apartment). So Eu me mudei is a house move, not a version switch.

Support, End of Support, Maintenance — and a False Friend

The words from every release note and every customer email about an old version

What "End of Life" means for software: Think of a software version like a car model. As long as it is being built, there are spare parts and recalls. At some point the manufacturer says: we won't do anything more for this model. For software, that day is called End of Life or Support-Ende (end of support). After that, the program keeps running, but new security holes are no longer closed. That is exactly what is happening with Node.js 20 right now: the Brotli vulnerability is being fixed in the current lines, but not in Node.js 20. Whoever stays there stays exposed.
FALSE FRIEND Noun DE m, no pl. (loanword) · EN support (uncountable) · PT-BR o suporte, m · ES-MX el soporte, m
der Support

Ruf bitte den Support an, das Login geht nicht.

support

Please call support, the login isn't working.

o suporte

Liga para o suporte, por favor, o login não funciona.

el soporte

Llama a soporte, por favor, el inicio de sesión no funciona.

Esta versión ya no tiene soporte.

The noun is safe, the verb is not O suporte is technical support, no catch. But according to Priberam, the verb suportar also means (second sense in Priberam) "to bear": „Aguentar ou permitir algo desagradável. = TOLERAR". So Não suporto mais esse sistema means "I can't stand this system anymore". The Portuguese Wiktionary also lists the meaning "fornecer apoio a algo" (to give support to something), but no IT meaning. So phrase it safely with the noun: damos suporte a, tem suporte para, or é compatível com. priberam.org · pt.wiktionary.org

A proverb with an expiry date The Brazilian image comes from soccer: you change nothing about a team (time) that is winning. A blog post on the proverb sums up the criticism: whoever thinks this way only acts once they are already losing. For software that is literally true — after end of support, a "running system" loses a bit of security every day. For Spanish there is no fixed refrán documented here; si funciona, no le muevas is a free rendering. substack.com

It's Tuesday, October twenty-seventh. Today's rotation topic is software and technology, episode thirteen, and it's the first language issue since August thirteenth. We're looking at the software lifecycle: abkündigen, umstellen, and end of support. Ten entries, three of them verbs with full conjugation, plus three refreshers.

The trigger is in today's Software brief. Node.js twenty gets no fix for the Brotli vulnerability, because that line has reached its end of life. In German that's Support-Ende, in Portuguese o fim do suporte, in Spanish el fin del soporte. After that, the software keeps running, but new holes stay open.

The first verb is abkündigen. The vendor announces that a product is being phased out. Portuguese and Spanish both say descontinuar. Same word, same stress on the u, but only Spanish writes it: yo descontinúo, ellos descontinúan. In Portuguese there's no accent: eu descontinuo, eles descontinuam.

The second verb is umstellen, usually with auf: Wir stellen auf Node zweiundzwanzig um, meaning we're switching over to Node twenty-two. Brazil says mudar para, Mexico says cambiar a. There's one trap: mudar-se means to move house in Portuguese. So Eu me mudei is a move to a new apartment, not a version switch.

Today's refresher is aktualisieren, to update. Portuguese atualizar is completely regular. Spanish actualizar has a small spelling rule: before an e, the z becomes a c. That's why it's ayer actualicé and que actualice, but yo actualizo.

Today's false friend is suportar. The noun o suporte is harmless, that's technical support. But according to the dictionary, the verb also means to bear or to tolerate. Não suporto mais esse sistema means: I can't stand this system anymore. If you want to say a product supports something, you're better off with dá suporte a or é compatível com.

Two more words let you tell right away whether a text comes from Brazil or from Portugal. The screen is a tela in Brazil and o ecrã in Portugal. The file is o arquivo in Brazil and o ficheiro in Portugal. Even the Portuguese dictionary names the Brazilian arquivo under ficheiro.

Finally, a proverb. Never change a running system becomes, in Brazil: em time que está ganhando não se mexe, which means you don't touch a team that's winning. After end of support, though, that saying no longer holds. Tomorrow the topic switches to family and daily life.
