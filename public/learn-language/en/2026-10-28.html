Rainy-Day Vocabulary & Feriadão: ausleeren, a fantasia, o feriadão

Wednesday, October 28 — rotation topic Family & Daily Life, installment fourteen. The occasion is in today's Family brief: Thursday will almost certainly bring rain, Saturday is Halloween, and Monday, November 2, is Finados — a national holiday that makes three days off together with the weekend. So we learn this week's vocabulary: to empty out water from a flower pot (because of dengue), to dress up for Halloween, to postpone the outing, plus umbrella, puddle and mosquito. A false friend lurks in the costume shop: a fantasia does not mean fantasy. 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 2 refreshers.

  • FALSE FRIEND a fantasia ≠ die Fantasie — in everyday Brazilian speech a fantasia is the costume. The Dicio dictionary defines it: „Máscara, disfarce ou vestimenta carnavalesca." Qual é a sua fantasia? therefore means: What are you going as? dicio.com.br
  • BR-PT o feriadão — the long weekend around a holiday. In 2026 Finados falls on Monday, November 2; Saturday through Monday are three days off. contabilidade.com · dicio.com.br
  • CONJUGATION vacío, but esvazio — like yesterday's descontinuar: Spanish writes the stress on the i as an accent (vacío, vacían, vacíe), Portuguese does not (esvazio, esvaziam, esvazie). reverso.net · conjugacao.com.br
  • CONJUGATION me disfracé — in disfrazarse the z becomes c before e: me disfracé, que te disfraces. The present stays regular: me disfrazo. reverso.net
  • BR-PT o pernilongo — that is the everyday word for mosquito in Brazil. Dicio: „No Brasil em geral, e particularmente em Minas, designação dada a qualquer mosquito da família dos culicídeos." dicio.com.br
  • CONJUGATION pospongo, pospuse — posponer conjugates like poner: yo pospongo, ayer pospuse, future pospondré. Portuguese adiar, by contrast, is completely regular. reverso.net · conjugacao.com.br
  • IDIOM está chovendo canivete — the refresher for Thursday: it's pouring buckets, and in Brazilian it rains pocketknives.

Three Verbs: to Empty Out, to Dress Up, to Postpone

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

Where the "myself" goes in the sentence: All three languages say "to dress up" with a small reflexive word: mich, me, me. The only difference is the position. German puts it after the verb: ich verkleide mich. Spanish puts it before: me disfrazo. Everyday Brazilian Portuguese does the same as Spanish: eu me fantasio. In Portugal, by contrast, it is attached at the end with a hyphen: fantasio-me. Rule of thumb: In Brazil you place the pronoun just as in Mexico — before the verb.
Verb DE weak, separable (aus-) → participle ausgeleert, perfect with haben · PT-BR esvaziar, regular -ar · ES-MX vaciar, accent i→í under stress (vacío, but vaciamos)
ausleeren

Nach dem Regen leere ich alle Untersetzer der Blumentöpfe aus.

Hast du den Eimer im Garten schon ausgeleert?

Leer bitte die Planschbecken aus, bevor die Mücken kommen.

to empty (out)

After the rain, I empty all the saucers under the flower pots.

Have you already emptied the bucket in the garden?

Please empty the paddling pools before the mosquitoes come.

esvaziar

Depois da chuva, eu esvazio todos os pratinhos dos vasos.

Você já esvaziou o balde no quintal?

Esvazie as piscininhas, por favor, antes que os mosquitos venham.

vaciar

Después de la lluvia, vacío todos los platos de las macetas.

¿Ya vaciaste la cubeta del jardín?

Vacía las albercas inflables, por favor, antes de que lleguen los mosquitos.

Why this verb this week The Family brief advises after Thursday's rain: empty out standing water. The dengue mosquito lays its eggs in small bits of water — saucers, buckets, tires, clogged gutters. In PT-BR standing water is a água parada, and these two words appear on many dengue posters. A side effect for Spanish: vacío is also the adjective "empty" — but in Mexico "the bucket is empty" is usually la cubeta está vacía rather than el balde está vacío.

Als was hast du dich letztes Jahr verkleidet?

Ich verkleide mich nie — ich trage nur einen Hut.

to dress up

On Saturday the kids are dressing up as a witch and a skeleton.

What did you dress up as last year?

I never dress up — I only wear a hat.

fantasiar-se

No sábado, as crianças vão se fantasiar de bruxa e de esqueleto.

Você se fantasiou de quê no ano passado?

Eu nunca me fantasio — só uso um chapéu.

El sábado los niños se van a disfrazar de bruja y de esqueleto.

¿De qué te disfrazaste el año pasado?

"as" is "de" here German and English dress up als and as something. Portuguese and Spanish use the small preposition de instead: fantasiar-se de bruxa, disfrazarse de bruja. The question accordingly is de quê? or ¿de qué?. A literal *como bruxa would be understood, but sounds translated.

to postpone

Same German word, different translation On June 20, verschieben meant "to reschedule an appointment": PT-BR remarcar, ES-MX mover. Today it means "to put off until later," with no new date: adiar and posponer. German covers both with one word. A tip for Mexico: there the cemetery is often called el panteón in everyday speech, not only el cementerio.

Finados, Feriadão, Fantasia — and a False Friend

The words for the long weekend from Oct 31 to Nov 2

Two countries, one day of remembrance: In the Catholic calendar, November 2 is the day to remember the dead. In German it is called Allerseelen (All Souls' Day). Brazil calls it Dia de Finados and makes it a national holiday: many families visit the grave and bring flowers, rather quietly. Mexico celebrates Día de Muertos on the same date, but loudly and colorfully: with altars, sugar skulls and music. According to folk belief, the souls come to visit there between October 27 and November 2. Same date, two very different moods.
Noun DE n, mostly without article · EN All Souls' Day · PT-BR o Dia de Finados, m · ES-MX el Día de Muertos, m (ecclesiastical: Día de los Fieles Difuntos)
Allerseelen

An Allerseelen bringen wir Blumen zum Grab der Großeltern.

Dieses Jahr fällt Allerseelen auf einen Montag.

All Souls' Day

On All Souls' Day we take flowers to our grandparents' grave.

This year All Souls' Day falls on a Monday.

o Dia de Finados

No Dia de Finados, a gente leva flores ao túmulo dos avós.

Este ano, o Dia de Finados cai numa segunda-feira.

el Día de Muertos

En Día de Muertos le llevamos flores a la tumba de los abuelos.

Este año, el 2 de noviembre cae en lunes.

"cai numa segunda" — a date falls As German says "fällt auf einen Montag," Portuguese says cai numa segunda-feira and Spanish cae en lunes. That is exactly how the holiday overview words it: „o Dia de Finados de 2026 cai numa segunda-feira, dia 2 de novembro". contabilidade.com · es.wikipedia.org
BR-PT Noun DE n, pl. long weekends · EN long weekend · PT-BR o feriadão, m (colloquial, augmentative of feriado) · ES-MX el fin de semana largo, m
das lange Wochenende

Am langen Wochenende fahren alle an den Stausee.

Was machst du über das lange Wochenende?

the long weekend

On the long weekend everyone drives to the reservoir.

What are you doing over the long weekend?

o feriadão

No feriadão, todo mundo vai para a represa.

O que você vai fazer no feriadão?

el fin de semana largo

En el fin de semana largo, todos se van a la presa.

¿Qué vas a hacer el fin de semana largo?

The -ão makes it big In Portuguese the ending -ão enlarges: o feriado is the holiday, o feriadão the "big holiday," that is, the extended weekend. Dicio derives the word from feriar. This year Finados adjoins the weekend on its own — no bridge day needed. Incidentally, October 28 is only a ponto facultativo, not a holiday. dicio.com.br · contabilidade.com

Fantasie, fantasia, fantasy The Dicio dictionary also gives fantasia the meaning „Máscara, disfarce ou vestimenta carnavalesca" — the costume for Carnival or, as here, Halloween. In Brazil this is the most common meaning in daily life. To say "kids have a lot of imagination," it is safer to say as crianças têm muita imaginação. Spanish has no such trap: el disfraz clearly means the costume. And beware of the German word: das Kostüm is also the women's skirt-and-jacket suit. dicio.com.br

Rainy Day: Umbrella, Puddle, Mosquito — and a Refresher

For Thursday, Oct 29 — and for the days after, when the water stays standing

Noun DE m, pl. -e · EN umbrella · PT-BR o guarda-chuva, m, pl. guarda-chuvas · ES-MX el paraguas, m, pl. same
der Regenschirm

Pack morgen den Regenschirm in den Schulranzen.

Mein Regenschirm ist im Wind kaputtgegangen.

the umbrella

Put the umbrella in your school bag tomorrow.

My umbrella broke in the wind.

o guarda-chuva

Amanhã põe o guarda-chuva na mochila.

Meu guarda-chuva quebrou com o vento.

el paraguas

Mañana mete el paraguas en la mochila.

Mi paraguas se rompió con el viento.

Two words, one blueprint Guarda-chuva literally means "keeps off rain," paraguas "stops water." Both are built from verb + noun, just as German Regenschirm is built from two nouns. That is why el paraguas already has an -s in the singular, and the plural stays the same: los paraguas.
Noun DE f, pl. -n · EN puddle · PT-BR a poça (d'água), f · ES-MX el charco, m — gender change between PT and ES

a poça

Depois de dois dias, a poça no quintal ainda está lá.

el charco

Después de dos días, el charco del patio sigue ahí.

BR-PT Noun DE f, pl. -n · EN mosquito · PT-BR o mosquito, m, in daily speech often o pernilongo, m · ES-MX el mosquito, m

the mosquito

Last night a mosquito bit me five times.

Essa noite um pernilongo me picou cinco vezes.

Fecha a janela, senão os pernilongos entram.

el mosquito

Anoche un mosquito me picó cinco veces.

Cierra la ventana, si no, se meten los mosquitos.

Mücke, Moskito, Pernilongo The Dicio dictionary describes pernilongo as the word that stands „no Brasil em geral, e particularmente em Minas" for any biting mosquito — literally "long leg." For the dengue mosquito, however, authorities and news outlets almost always say o mosquito da dengue or o Aedes aegypti. So: in the bedroom the pernilongo buzzes, on the poster it is the mosquito. A stumbling block for English speakers: English says to bite, but German says "stechen" (to sting) — and PT picar and ES picar match the German. dicio.com.br

It's Wednesday, October twenty-eighth. Today's rotation topic is family and daily life, installment fourteen. We're looking at the week ahead: a rainy day on Thursday, Halloween on Saturday, and Finados on Monday. Ten entries, including three verbs with full conjugation and two refreshers.

The first verb is the German ausleeren, to empty out. After the rain, you're supposed to empty out standing water, because of the dengue mosquito. In Portuguese that's esvaziar, in Spanish vaciar. As with yesterday's verb, both put the stress on the i, but only Spanish writes an accent: yo vacío. Portuguese has none: eu esvazio.

The second verb is sich verkleiden, to dress up. In Brazil it's fantasiar-se, in Mexico disfrazarse. The little pronoun goes before the verb in both: eu me fantasio, yo me disfrazo. And instead of "as," you say "de": fantasiar-se de bruxa, disfrazarse de bruja. In Spanish, the z turns into a c before an e: ayer me disfracé.

The refresher is verschieben, this time in the sense of putting something off until later. Brazil says adiar, completely regular. Mexico says posponer, which follows poner: yo pospongo, ayer pospuse.

Today's false friend is a fantasia. In Brazil that means a costume, not fantasy. So Qual é a sua fantasia means: What are you going as?

November second is All Souls' Day. In Brazil it's called Dia de Finados and it's a national holiday. Because it falls on a Monday this year, that makes three days off, and Brazil has its own word for that: o feriadão. In Mexico the day is called Día de Muertos and is celebrated loudly and colorfully.

For the rainy day, there are three words: umbrella is o guarda-chuva and el paraguas, puddle is a poça and el charco, and the mosquito, in everyday Brazilian speech, is o pernilongo. One last refresher: "it's raining buckets" in Brazilian is está chovendo canivete, literally, it's raining pocketknives. Tomorrow the topic switches to soccer and sports.
