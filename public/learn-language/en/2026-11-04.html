Three Verbs: Donate, Bring, Cancel

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

What is a solidarity ticket? Normally you pay for a concert with money. At the Fejacan you pay with a gift for another child. Anyone who wants to hear Mel Lisboa on Thursday at the Cine Teatro Iguaçu brings a new toy. The toy goes to the Campanha do Brinquedo of Sesc Paraná. In Portuguese this is "ingressos solidários, mediante a doação de um brinquedo novo". It contains two of today's words: o ingresso and a doação. sescpr.com.br
Verb DE weak, regular, with e-insertion (du spendest, er spendet) → participle gespendet, perfect with haben · PT-BR doar, regular -ar, but eu doo with a double o · ES-MX donar, regular -ar
spenden

Wir spenden ein neues Spielzeug für das Konzert am Donnerstag.

Letztes Jahr haben die Kinder ihre alten Bücher gespendet.

Wer spendet, bekommt eine Eintrittskarte.

to donate

We're donating a new toy for Thursday's concert.

Last year the kids donated their old books.

Anyone who donates gets a ticket.

doar

A gente vai doar um brinquedo novo para o show de quinta.

No ano passado, as crianças doaram os livros velhos.

Quem doa ganha um ingresso.

donar

Vamos a donar un juguete nuevo para el concierto del jueves.

El año pasado los niños donaron sus libros viejos.

Quien dona recibe un boleto.

Show conjugation

DE: The stem ends in -d, so an e goes before the ending: du spendest, er spendet, ihr spendet. Subjunctive II as a würde form, because the synthetic form equals the preterite (spendete). PT: eu doo is written with two o and no accent; present and pretérito of nós are the same (doamos). ES donar: no stem change and no spelling change. verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Verb DE irregular (mixed verb bringen – brachte – gebracht), separable (mit-) → participle mitgebracht, perfect with haben · PT-BR trazer, strongly irregular (trago, trouxe, trarei) · ES-MX traer, irregular (traigo, traje)
mitbringen

Bringst du morgen ein neues Spielzeug mit?

Oma hat zum Abendessen einen Kuchen mitgebracht.

Ich bringe dir die Eintrittskarten am Donnerstag mit.

to bring (along)

Are you bringing a new toy tomorrow?

Grandma brought a cake for dinner.

I'll bring you the tickets on Thursday.

trazer

Você traz um brinquedo novo amanhã?

A vovó trouxe um bolo para o jantar.

Eu te trago os ingressos na quinta.

traer

¿Traes un juguete nuevo mañana?

La abuela trajo un pastel para la cena.

Te traigo los boletos el jueves.

Here or there? The German mitbringen says nothing about direction. PT-BR and ES-MX do. Trazer and traer mean "bring here", toward the speaker. Levar and llevar mean "take along there", away from the speaker. If you are at the Cine Teatro yourself on Thursday, you say: Traga um brinquedo! If you stay home, you say: Leve um brinquedo! On the ENEM, Inep writes: "não é obrigatório levar o Cartão de Confirmação" — participants carry it from home to the exam, so away. poder360.com.br
Show conjugation

DE: separable, stressed mit- — ich bringe … mit, participle mitgebracht. Mixed verb: stem change bring- → brach- like a strong verb, but endings like a weak one. Subjunctive II with umlaut: brächte mit. PT trazer: z → g in eu trago and throughout the subjuntivo (traga); pretérito with trouxe (pronounced with [s]); shortened future trarei; ele traz with no ending. ES traer: yo traigo, subjuntivo traiga, pretérito with j (traje, trajo, trajeron — not *trajieron). verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Verb DE weak, regular, separable (ab-) → participle abgesagt, perfect with haben · PT-BR cancelar, regular -ar · ES-MX cancelar, regular -ar
absagen

Wegen des Gewitters sagen wir das Picknick am Samstag ab.

Die Schule hat den Ausflug gestern abgesagt.

Sag bitte rechtzeitig ab, wenn du nicht kommen kannst.

to cancel

Because of the storm, we're canceling Saturday's picnic.

The school canceled the field trip yesterday.

Please cancel in time if you can't come.

cancelar

Por causa da tempestade, a gente cancela o piquenique de sábado.

A escola cancelou o passeio ontem.

Por favor, cancele com antecedência se você não puder vir.

cancelar

Por la tormenta, cancelamos el picnic del sábado.

La escuela canceló la excursión ayer.

Por favor, cancela con tiempo si no puedes venir.

One verb, two meanings The German absagen needs no object: "Ich sage ab" is enough. Cancelar in PT and ES always needs a thing: cancelar o piquenique, cancelar la cita. In return, cancelar covers more than absagen: it also means "to void" (an order) and "to cancel" (a subscription). In US English, canceled is spelled with one l.
Show conjugation

DE: separable, stressed ab- — ich sage … ab, participle abgesagt. Subjunctive II as a würde form, because the synthetic form equals the preterite (sagte ab). PT and ES: cancelar is completely regular in both languages; in both, present and pretérito of nós/nosotros are the same (cancelamos). verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

It's Wednesday, November fourth. Today's rotation topic is family and everyday life, episode fifteen. We're covering donating, bringing and canceling. Eleven entries, three of them verbs with full conjugation, plus two reviews.

The first occasion is the Fejacan in Jacarezinho. On Thursday, Mel Lisboa sings at the Cine Teatro Iguaçu. Admission doesn't cost money, but a new toy as a donation. To donate in Portuguese is doar, with a small trap: eu doo, with two o's. In Spanish it's donar, completely regular. The ticket itself is o ingresso in Brazil and el boleto in Mexico.

The second verb is to bring. Portuguese trazer and Spanish traer are both irregular: eu trago, eu trouxe, and yo traigo, yo traje. What matters is direction. Trazer and traer bring something toward the speaker. Levar and llevar take something away from the speaker.

A thunderstorm is likely on Saturday, a tempestade in Portuguese, la tormenta in Spanish. Then you may have to cancel something. In both languages that's cancelar, and it's completely regular. Afterward, puddles are left: a poça and el charco.

On November eighth and fifteenth, teenagers across Brazil take the ENEM. The university entrance exam is called o vestibular in Brazil. Today's false friend is in the pencil case. In Portuguese, a borracha is an eraser. In Spanish, borracha means drunk. In Mexico people mostly say la goma or el borrador.

Finally, an idiom for the family. The apple doesn't fall far from the tree is filho de peixe, peixinho é in Portuguese, and de tal palo, tal astilla in Spanish. And if a plan falls through, let someone know: me avisa, or avísame. Tomorrow the topic switches to soccer and sports.
