Vocabulary for an Address to the Nation: anunciar, acalmar, transmitir

Sunday, November 8 — rotation theme Politics, Law & Health, installment ten. Today's hook: according to Agência Estado, Lula speaks to the country this Sunday in a “cadeia nacional de rádio e televisão”. It is his first formal speech since his re-election on October 25. The goal, per the report: calm the market and set an idea of “pacificação” for the fourth term. The sources give no time of day. The numbers are in the Economy brief; here we focus on the words: to announce, to calm, to broadcast, plus address, measure and pacification. 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 2 refreshers from last Sunday.

  • FALSE FRIEND o pronunciamento ≠ el pronunciamiento — in Brazil's press this is the televised address. But the Mexican DEM gives as its first sense: “Rebelión militar en contra de un gobierno legalmente constituido”. dem.colmex.mx
  • BR-PT em cadeia nacional de rádio e TV — all stations carry the same speech at the same time. The report says the speech “vai ao ar”, it goes on air. diariodepernambuco.com.br
  • IDIOM acalmar os ânimos (PT) = “to calm tempers”. That is exactly how the report words it: “para acalmar os ânimos do mercado”. ES knows los ánimos too, DEM: “encendió los ánimos”.
  • CONJUGATION broadcast – broadcast – broadcast — in English the present, past and participle are identical. Broadcasted is common according to Wiktionary, but sometimes considered wrong. wiktionary.org
  • FALSE FRIEND die Befriedung ≠ die Befriedigung — one letter apart. Befriedung is making peace (pacificação), Befriedigung is satisfaction (satisfação).
  • CONJUGATION angekündigt, but beschwichtigt — an- is separable, so the ge- goes in the middle. be- is inseparable, so there is no ge- at all. verbformen.de

Three Verbs for the Speech: to announce, to calm, to broadcast

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

What an “address to the nation” is: Imagine ten radios playing in your house, each tuned to a different station. Suddenly all ten play the same thing: the president is speaking. That is a cadeia nacional — a chain in which all stations link up for a few minutes. The president does three things. He announces something, in PT anunciar. He wants to calm people down, in PT acalmar. And the stations broadcast the speech, in PT transmitir. According to Agência Estado, that is exactly what Lula wants to do today to calm the market. The three verbs below tell the story of this Sunday.

ankündigen ≠ kündigen — and o anúncio means advertising In German, never drop the an-. Ich kündige means: I am ending my contract or my job. In PT that is pedir demissão or cancelar, not anunciar. The second trap is in the noun. O anúncio (PT) and el anuncio (ES) are not only the announcement, but also the ad or the advertising. An anúncio in the newspaper is therefore usually a classified ad. According to Agência Estado, Lula wants to announce “medidas de ajuste” today. acritica.net

to calm

acalmar

Eu acalmei as crianças depois da tempestade.

beschwichtigen or beruhigen? The tone makes the difference Beruhigen is neutral: someone is upset, and you help them. Beschwichtigen often carries a critical undertone: you play down a worry so that things settle. That is why German also has the pejorative Beschwichtigungspolitik. EN has to appease for this. PT acalmar and ES calmar are neutral like beruhigen. For the critical tone there are PT apaziguar and ES apaciguar. The Agência Estado report uses the neutral word: Lula wants to “acalmar o mercado”. In the ES sentence you see calmar al mercado: Spanish often adds the a here, as if the market were a person. acritica.net

cadeia nacional, “ir ao ar” — and why EN switches verbs in the third sentence In a cadeia nacional, all radio and TV stations in Brazil carry the same speech at the same time. The report writes: the speech “vai ao ar em cadeia nacional de rádio e TV”. Ir ao ar means “to go on air”; Mexico says salir al aire. For cadena, the DEM lists only the general sense “Serie de objetos o empresas relacionadas entre sí”, with the example “cadena de radio”. We know the phrase en cadena nacional from Mexican press, not from the DEM. The third sentence shows the second sense of ausstrahlen: to radiate calm. PT and ES stay with transmitir. EN needs a different verb for it, to radiate, because broadcast only applies to stations. diariodepernambuco.com.br · dem.colmex.mx · vanguardia.com.mx

Address, Measure, Pacification — the Words of the Speech

4 nouns and 1 idiom · around Lula's address of November 8

Why a president wants to “calm the market”: Imagine you lend money to a friend. Then you hear that he spends much more than he earns. You get nervous and would rather have your money back. The friend calls you and says: “Don't worry, I'm saving now, here is my plan.” That is exactly the kind of speech Lula is planning, according to the report. The people who lend money to the state are “the market”. The plan consists of measures, in PT medidas. And the promise is: the fiscal targets still apply, in PT as metas fiscais.

“pronunciamento” in Brazil, “pronunciamiento” in Mexico — two different worlds In the Brazilian press, o pronunciamento today is simply the televised address: Lula “vai fazer um pronunciamento para a nação”. But the DEM, the dictionary of Mexican Spanish, gives as the first sense of pronunciamiento: “Rebelión militar en contra de un gobierno legalmente constituido”. Only the second sense is a public statement. Priberam also knows the old sense for PT: “Sublevação militar, sedição”. In Brazil the context makes everything clear. In Mexico, better use el mensaje a la nación for the speech. For a short speech at a party, all three languages say discurso / speech, and the mayor is el presidente municipal in Mexico. dem.colmex.mx · priberam.org · diariodepernambuco.com.br

medida also means “measure” as in size — sob medida, a la medida According to Agência Estado, it is about “medidas de ajuste discutidas com os ministros Dario Durigan (Fazenda) e Bruno Moretti (Planejamento)”. Ajuste here is the adjustment of the budget. But the word medida has a second sense: the measure of size. Roupa sob medida is tailor-made clothing, Mexico says a la medida. And à medida que means “as”, “to the extent that”. In the second sentence you see an everyday difference: the electricity bill is a conta de luz in Brazil, el recibo de luz in Mexico. acritica.net

“bater a meta” — you “beat” the target According to Estadão Conteúdo, Lula wants to say that the government “seguirá perseguindo as metas fiscais”. Literally: it keeps “chasing” the targets, like a hunter. In Brazil you constantly hear bater a meta in offices and stores: to “beat” the target, meaning to hit it. Mexico says alcanzar or cumplir la meta instead. In sports, la meta is also the finish line in Spanish. On November 2, the fiscal rule was already in the brief: the rule is the framework, the meta is the number you want to hit within it. diariodepernambuco.com.br

Befriedung, not Befriedigung A single -ig- changes everything. Die Befriedung comes from Frieden (peace): ending a quarrel, calming a country. Die Befriedigung comes from zufrieden (content) and is PT a satisfação, ES la satisfacción. In a speech about politics, the second word is very awkward. According to Agência Estado, Lula wants to convey “uma ideia de pacificação do País no quarto mandato”. Watch out in EN too: pacification often sounds military there. For political reconciliation, people prefer reconciliation or bringing the country together, which is why that appears in the EN sentence. acritica.net

ânimo in the singular, ânimos in the plural The report states the goal of the speech word for word: “acalmar os ânimos do mercado”. In the singular, o ânimo means “mood, courage”: Ânimo! is “Cheer up!”. Only in the plural do os ânimos become heated tempers. Spanish works the same way. The DEM gives the example “La decisión del árbitro encendió los ánimos” and the phrase caldearse los ánimos, “the mood heats up”. Calmar los ánimos is the counterpart. In football, Mexico says el penal, and for a quarrel el pleito. diariodepernambuco.com.br · dem.colmex.mx

o quarto mandato — and o quarto for sleeping The press speaks of the “quarto mandato”. Here quarto is the ordinal number: fourth. In everyday use, though, o quarto is also the bedroom. O quarto quarto would therefore be “the fourth room”. Spanish has the same doubling: cuarto is “fourth” and in Mexico also the room. English just adds -th: the fourth term. By the way, from pacificação comes the verb pacificar, in German befrieden. acritica.net

It's Sunday, November eighth. Today's rotation theme is politics, law and health, installment ten. According to Agência Estado, Lula speaks today in a nationwide radio and television broadcast. It's his first formal speech since his re-election on October twenty-fifth. Today there are ten entries, three of them verbs with full conjugation, plus two refreshers.

The three verbs tell the story of the speech: to announce, to calm, to broadcast. In Portuguese they are anunciar, acalmar and transmitir, and in Mexican Spanish anunciar, calmar and transmitir. In German, never drop the "an" from ankündigen: someone who kündigt is ending a contract.

With beschwichtigen, the German participle has no "ge": it's simply beschwichtigt. And in English, broadcast is irregular: broadcast, broadcast, broadcast.

Today's false friend is pronunciamento. In Brazil, it means a televised address. But the Mexican dictionary DEM lists as the first meaning of pronunciamiento a military uprising against a legitimate government. In Mexico, it's safer to say mensaje a la nación for a speech.

According to the report, Lula plans to announce measures, in Portuguese medidas. He wants to say the government will keep pursuing its fiscal targets, as metas fiscais. And he wants to calm the market's nerves, in Portuguese acalmar os ânimos.

A second stumbling block is German: Befriedung is not Befriedigung. Befriedung comes from Frieden, or peace, and in Portuguese it's a pacificação. That's the very word the report uses for the fourth term.
