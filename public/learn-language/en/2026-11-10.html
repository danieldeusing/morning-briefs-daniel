  • FALSE FRIEND folder = a pasta = la carpeta — according to Priberam, PT pasta means a portfolio or a computer folder; the dictionary lists no pasta-the-noodle meaning. ES carpeta is likewise the folder, not the carpet. priberam.org · es.wiktionary.org
  • BR-PT o usuário — that is what a computer user is called in Brazil. Priberam marks this meaning with [Brasil] and gives utilizador, the word from Portugal, as the counterpart. priberam.org
  • GENDER die Frist → o prazo → el plazo — feminine in German, masculine in both Romance languages. linguee.de

Three verbs: einspielen, abweisen, abschalten

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

What the KEV catalog is: The US agency CISA keeps a list of flaws that are proven to be under attack already. Picture a bulletin board: "This lock is being picked right now." Once a flaw is listed there, US federal agencies must apply the patch by a fixed deadline. Since Monday evening the OpenSSH flaw CVE-2026-123158, nicknamed "SSHole", has been on it. The deadline ends on Thursday, November 12. GreyNoise already counts 16 successful break-in chains. According to the software brief, only musl systems without PAM and with password login are affected. bleepingcomputer.com
Verb DE weak, separable (ein-) → participle eingespielt, perfect with haben · PT-BR aplicar, c→qu before e (apliquei) · ES-MX aplicar, c→qu before e (apliqué)
einspielen

Wir müssen den OpenSSH-Patch bis Donnerstag einspielen.

Hast du das Update schon eingespielt?

Heute ist Patch Tuesday, morgen spielen wir die Fixes ein.

to apply (a patch)

We must apply the OpenSSH patch by Thursday.

Have you already applied the update?

Today is Patch Tuesday; tomorrow we apply the fixes.

aplicar (um patch)

Precisamos aplicar o patch do OpenSSH até quinta-feira.

Você já aplicou a atualização?

Hoje é Patch Tuesday; amanhã aplicamos as correções.

aplicar (un parche)

Tenemos que aplicar el parche de OpenSSH antes del jueves.

¿Ya aplicaste la actualización?

Hoy es Patch Tuesday; mañana aplicamos las correcciones.

"Einspielen" is jargon In German you "play in" a patch the way you play in a recording. PT-BR and ES-MX are more sober: you apply the patch — aplicar. In everyday speech people also simply say instalar.

Deadline, data leak, reporting obligation — and two traps in the file manager

The words from every security notice and every compliance email

What the CRA reporting obligation requires: The Cyber Resilience Act is an EU law for software and connected devices. Since September 11, a manufacturer must report a flaw that is being attacked, like a smoke detector that calls the fire department on its own. After 24 hours comes an early warning, after 72 hours the actual report. That is why Frist (deadline) and Meldepflicht (reporting obligation) are two words every developer team must know today. digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu
Noun DE f, pl. -en · EN deadline · PT-BR o prazo, m · ES-MX el plazo, m — gender change f → m
die Frist

Die KEV-Frist für SSHole endet am Donnerstag.

Wir mussten hart arbeiten, um die Frist einzuhalten.

the deadline

The KEV deadline for SSHole ends on Thursday.

We had to work hard to meet the deadline.

O prazo do KEV para o SSHole termina na quinta-feira.

Tivemos que trabalhar muito para cumprir o prazo.

el plazo

Tuvimos que trabajar duro para cumplir el plazo.

Meeting a deadline The verb for it in both Romance languages is cumprir / cumplir, literally "to fulfill". Linguee lists exactly this example: "Tuvimos que trabajar duro para cumplir el plazo." A final deadline is o prazo final in PT-BR. linguee.de · linguee.com.br
Noun DE n, pl. -s · EN data leak · PT-BR o vazamento de dados, m · ES-MX la fuga de datos, f (also la filtración de datos)

Why this fits today According to heise, Apiiro examined more than 100,000 copies of agent configuration files such as .claude/settings.local.json. Result: 2,539 real secrets in 1,994 public repos. The image is the same in all languages: something "runs out" — Leck, leak, vazamento, fuga. heise.de · dwds.de · linguee.de

Double trap In Priberam, PT pasta means portfolio, briefcase and, under [Informática], a folder on a storage medium. The dictionary lists no pasta-the-noodle meaning. It's Tuesday, November tenth. Today's rotation topic is software and technology, episode fifteen. We're covering how to apply a patch, reject an email, and shut a system down. Eleven entries, including three verbs with full conjugation and three refreshers.

The first hook is the OpenSSH flaw known as SSHole. It's been in CISA's KEV catalog since Monday evening, and the patch deadline runs out on Thursday. To apply a patch, Portuguese and Spanish both use aplicar. Before an e, the c turns into qu: eu apliquei, yo apliqué. And while "Frist" is feminine in German, o prazo and el plazo are masculine.

The second hook is Outlook. Since around November fifth, Outlook has been rejecting mail from small senders without DMARC. In German, "abweisen" is a strong verb: wies ab, abgewiesen. Portuguese says rejeitar, perfectly regular. Spanish says rechazar, and before an e the z becomes a c: yo rechacé.

The third hook is N-able. The company shut down its hosted platform after code for more than thirty alleged zero-days went public. To shut down is desligar in Brazil and apagar in Mexico. Both switch from g to gu before an e: eu desliguei, yo apagué.

Add three nouns. A data leak is o vazamento de dados and la fuga de datos. The reporting obligation under the Cyber Resilience Act is a obrigação de notificação and la obligación de notificar. Typically Brazilian is o usuário for the user. In Portugal, they say utilizador.

Today's false friend is the folder. In Portuguese it's a pasta, in Spanish la carpeta. Here, pasta is not a noodle, and carpeta is, as a rule, not a carpet. We also refreshed vulnerability, attacker, and end of support. Tomorrow the topic switches to family and daily life.
