Plus a false friend: the German Relegation is not the English relegation. 10 entries, including 3 verbs with full conjugation, 2 of them refreshers.

  • FALSE FRIEND die Relegation ≠ relegation — in German, the Relegation is the decider between two leagues. The English relegation is simply the drop down. de.wikipedia.org
  • BR-PT o rebaixamento · o Z4 — in Brazil people say rebaixamento for relegation, and the relegation zone is called Z4 for short. In Portugal you mostly hear a despromoção. tvtnews.com.br · priberam.org
  • IDIOM o time caiu (PT) = “the team was relegated,” literally “the team fell.” Short, harsh and all over the radio.
  • CONJUGATION ich bin abgestiegen — German absteigen forms the perfect with sein because it expresses movement. Sich retten and verschieben take haben. verbformen.de
  • CONJUGATION desciendo · aplacé — ES descender has an e→ie stem change, but nosotros descendemos stays without the diphthong. ES aplazar writes a c before e: yo aplacé. reverso.net · reverso.net
  • GENDER a Data Fifa · la fecha FIFA — PT a data means “the date” (feminine), not “the data.” Data is os dados.

Three Verbs: absteigen, sich retten, verschieben

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked at verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

What the table below says Twenty clubs play in the Brasileirão. The bottom four, places 17 to 20, drop down to Série B. In Brazil these four places are called Z4 for short, the “zone 4.” In the Nov. 6 table, Internacional in 16th place on 38 points is the first club above the line. Corinthians and Grêmio have 37 and sit below it. A single win can turn everything around. At the very bottom it is already clear: Chapecoense has 21 points after 33 games. Even with five wins they would only reach 36 — Internacional already has 38. According to this table, Chapecoense is therefore certain to go down. valorfinal.com.br · tvtnews.com.br
Verb DE strong, separable (der Verein steigt ab) → past participle abgestiegen, perfect with sein · PT-BR cair (for Série B), irregular; formal ser rebaixado · ES-MX descender, stem change e→ie
absteigen

Verliert der Verein noch zweimal, steigt er ab.

Chapecoense ist so gut wie abgestiegen.

In Brasilien steigen jedes Jahr vier Vereine ab.

to be relegated

If the club loses twice more, it will go down.

Chapecoense has as good as been relegated.

In Brazil, four clubs are relegated every year.

cair

Se o time perder mais duas vezes, ele cai.

A Chapecoense praticamente caiu.

No Brasil, quatro times caem todo ano.

descender

Si el equipo pierde dos veces más, desciende.

El Chapecoense prácticamente descendió.

En Brasil descienden cuatro equipos cada año.

Show conjugation

DE: ablaut ei → ie (stieg, abgestiegen), perfect with sein. In a main clause, ab moves to the end of the sentence. The subjunctive II is shown here as a true form, stiege ab; in everyday speech people also say würde absteigen. EN: as a passive, be relegated; colloquially go down. PT: cair carries the accent on the i where it is stressed: caímos, caí, caía; subjunctive caia. ES: descender diphthongizes only under stress: desciendo, but descendemos. verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Verb DE weak, reflexive, with e-insertion (er rettet sich) → past participle gerettet, perfect with haben · PT-BR escapar (do rebaixamento), regular -ar · ES-MX salvarse, regular, reflexive
sich retten

Mit Siegen kann sich Corinthians noch retten.

Letztes Jahr hat sich der Verein am letzten Spieltag gerettet.

Wir retten uns nur, wenn wir zu Hause gewinnen.

to stay up

With wins, Corinthians can still stay up.

Last year the club stayed up on the final matchday.

We'll only stay up if we win at home.

escapar

Com vitórias, o Corinthians ainda pode escapar do rebaixamento.

No ano passado, o time escapou na última rodada.

A gente só escapa se ganhar em casa.

salvarse

Con victorias, el Corinthians todavía puede salvarse.

El año pasado el equipo se salvó en la última jornada.

Solo nos salvamos si ganamos de local.

Show conjugation

DE: the stem ends in -t, hence du rettest, er rettet, ihr rettetet. The reflexive pronoun is in the accusative (mich, dich, sich). Subjunctive II as a würde form, because rettete looks like the past tense. EN: stay up is the soccer expression; neutrally you say avoid relegation. PT: escapar is regular; the thing escaped takes de: escapar do rebaixamento. You also hear se salvar. ES: salvarse is regular, and the pronoun goes before the verb: me salvo, nos salvamos. verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

REVIEW Verb DE strong, inseparable, ablaut ie → o (verschob, verschoben), perfect with haben · PT-BR adiar, regular -ar · ES-MX aplazar, z→c before e · refreshed from Oct. 28
verschieben

Das Spiel wurde verschoben und wird am 14. November nachgeholt.

Bei Gewitter verschiebt der Schiedsrichter den Anpfiff.

Wir haben das Grillen auf Sonntag verschoben.

to postpone

The match was postponed and will be played on November 14.

In a thunderstorm, the referee postpones the kickoff.

We postponed the barbecue to Sunday.

adiar

O jogo foi adiado e vai ser disputado no dia 14 de novembro.

Com tempestade, o árbitro adia o início da partida.

A gente adiou o churrasco para domingo.

aplazar

El partido se aplazó y se jugará el 14 de noviembre.

Con tormenta eléctrica, el árbitro aplaza el inicio del partido.

Aplazamos la carne asada para el domingo.

Show conjugation

DE: ver- is inseparable, so there is no ge- in the participle: verschoben. The true subjunctive II verschöbe is rare; in everyday speech people say würde verschieben. PT: adiar is regular, eu adio, você adia; the present and the preterite of nós are identical (adiamos). ES: in aplacé and throughout the subjunctive (aplace), z becomes c. In Mexico you hear posponer just as often (it works like poner: pospuse). verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Four new words for the last four matchdays

Acht Vereine stecken noch im Abstiegskampf.

Der Abstiegskampf ist oft spannender als der Titelkampf.

Eight clubs are still in the relegation battle.

The relegation battle is often more exciting than the title race.

Ocho equipos siguen en la lucha por no descender.

La lucha por no descender muchas veces es más emocionante que la pelea por el título.

False friend: die Relegation In German, according to Wikipedia, Relegationsspiele are “qualifying matches” that decide who plays in the higher league and who in the lower league next season. In the Bundesliga, the 16th-placed team plays the 3rd-placed team of the 2. Bundesliga. The English relegation, by contrast, is simply the drop down. “Wir müssen in die Relegation” therefore does not translate as we face relegation but as we face a relegation play-off. The eight clubs in the sentence above are named by TVT News: Botafogo, Coritiba, Vasco, Mirassol, Vitória, Internacional, Corinthians and Grêmio. de.wikipedia.org · tvtnews.com.br

It's Thursday, November twelfth. Today's rotation topic is soccer and sports, episode five. We're looking at the relegation fight: how to say get relegated, stay up, and postpone in German, Portuguese and Spanish. Ten entries, including three verbs with full conjugation and two refreshers.

The Brasileirão is tight. In the table as of November sixth, Flamengo leads with seventy-six points, three ahead of Palmeiras on seventy-three. At the bottom, four clubs are within a single point of each other: Vitória and Internacional on thirty-eight, Corinthians and Grêmio on thirty-seven. In Brazil, the bottom four places are simply called the Z4.

To get relegated is just cair in Portuguese, which means to fall: o time caiu. In Spanish it's descender, with a stem change: yo desciendo, but nosotros descendemos. In German, absteigen forms its perfect with sein: ich bin abgestiegen. To stay up is escapar do rebaixamento in Portuguese and salvarse in Spanish.

Now comes the international break. In Brazil it's called a Data Fifa, in Mexico la fecha FIFA. Heads up: a data means the date, not the data. On Saturday there's a rescheduled match, Chapecoense against Vasco. A postponed game is o jogo adiado in Portuguese and el partido pendiente in Spanish. The league resumes on November eighteenth, with Corinthians against Botafogo.
