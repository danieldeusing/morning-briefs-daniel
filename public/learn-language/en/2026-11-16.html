Three Verbs for the Week: einbrechen, umrechnen, verwässern

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, de.wiktionary.org, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

One story, three verbs: Imagine a flea market. Suddenly nobody wants real coins anymore; everyone wants dollars. The price of the real coin bricht ein (collapses). You have euro invoices. You rechnest them into reais (um) and get more than three weeks ago. Why don't people want the real? The state spends more than it takes in. The government has presented a savings plan. Many traders find it too weak. And they fear Congress will still verwässern it (water it down): less saving, more exceptions.

Der Real ist nach der Stichwahl eingebrochen.

to plunge

The real has plunged since the runoff.

On November 12, the real plunged again: the dollar rose 1.58%.

If the austerity package fails, the real could plunge further.

despencar

O real despencou depois do segundo turno.

Em 12 de novembro, o real despencou de novo: o dólar subiu 1,58%.

El real se desplomó después de la segunda vuelta.

El 12 de noviembre, el real se desplomó otra vez: el dólar subió 1.58%.

Si el paquete de ajuste fracasa, el real podría desplomarse aún más.

"tombo", "dispara", "despenca" — three images for the same day The press likes to describe a currency crash from the opposite side. On Nov. 12, Agência Brasil headlined: "Dólar dispara 1,58% e fecha a R$ 5,65, maior nível em 17 meses". Disparar means "to fire off": the dollar shoots up, so the real collapses. On Nov. 8, Mercado & Consumo wrote of the "tombo do real", the fall of the real. Despencar is the matching verb: to plummet, to crash. In German, einbrechen has two meanings. A rate collapses, perfect with sein: Der Real ist eingebrochen. And a thief breaks into a house; that is an Einbruch, PT arrombar. agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br · mercadoeconsumo.com.br · verbformen.de

umrechnen

Ich rechne 1.000 € zum Freitags-Fix in Reais um: Das sind 6.259,50.

Hast du die Rechnung schon in Reais umgerechnet?

Die Bank rechnete den Betrag zum Kurs vom Montag um.

to convert

I convert €1,000 into reais at Friday's fix: that's 6,259.50.

Have you already converted the invoice into reais?

The bank converted the amount at Monday's rate.

converter

Eu converto mil euros em reais pela cotação de sexta: são R$ 6.259,50.

Você já converteu a fatura para reais?

O banco converteu o valor pela cotação de segunda.

convertir

Convierto mil euros a reales al tipo de cambio del viernes: son 6,259.50.

¿Ya convertiste la factura a reales?

Umrechnen is not umtauschen Umrechnen is pure math: you take the rate and calculate; no money changes hands. Someone who really swaps euros for reais says DE umtauschen or wechseln, PT trocar or fazer o câmbio, ES-MX cambiar. Today's calculation: €1,000 × 6.2595 (ECB fix of Nov. 13) = R$6,259.50. In Spanish, watch the stem. When stressed, e becomes ie (convierto, conviertes), but convertimos stays. In the 3rd person preterite and in the nosotros subjunctive there is an i: convirtió, convirtamos. Portuguese stays completely regular: converto, converteu. ecb.europa.eu · verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br

One verb, two spellings PT and ES write the same verb, but differently. Portuguese adds an accent when the i is stressed: diluí, diluímos, diluíram, but dilui without an accent. Spanish inserts a y where the i falls between vowels: diluyo, diluye, diluyó, diluyeron. Nosotros diluimos carries no accent in Spanish. How a package gets watered down was already shown by the last austerity package: Jovem Pan reported that Congress passed it "com mudanças que reduzem impacto financeiro". conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net · jovempan.com.br

Ausverkauf, Sparpaket, Zinsvorteil — the Words That Go With Them

5 nouns, 1 idiom and 2 reviews · meanings checked against Dicio and DEM (COLMEX)

Why the real pays such high interest: Imagine two piggy banks. The Brazilian one pays you 13.75% interest a year. That is the Zinsvorteil (interest-rate advantage), and it attracts money from all over the world. But the piggy bank has a hole, the Haushaltsloch (budget hole): the state spends more than it takes in. The government wants to plug the hole with a Sparpaket (austerity package). If investors don't believe in it, they pull their money out. That is the Ausverkauf (sell-off). And anyone who has euros auf der hohen Kante (set aside) suddenly gets more reais for them.
PHRASE Noun DE m, pl. Ausverkäufe · EN sell-off · PT-BR a onda de vendas, f (in a store: a liquidação) · ES-MX la ola de ventas, f (in a store: la liquidación)
der Ausverkauf

Der Ausverkauf am 12. November trieb den Dollar auf den höchsten Stand seit 17 Monaten.

Im Kaufhaus ist Ausverkauf: Alles kostet die Hälfte.

the sell-off

The sell-off on November 12 pushed the dollar to its highest level in 17 months.

The department store is having a clearance sale: everything is half price.

a onda de vendas

A onda de vendas de 12 de novembro levou o dólar ao maior nível em 17 meses.

A loja está em liquidação: tudo pela metade do preço.

la ola de ventas

La ola de ventas del 12 de noviembre llevó al dólar a su nivel más alto en 17 meses.

La tienda está en liquidación: todo a mitad de precio.

One German word, two worlds DE Ausverkauf covers the store and the stock market. EN, PT and ES separate the two. On the stock market it is sell-off, onda de vendas, ola de ventas. In a store it is clearance sale, liquidação, liquidación. So anyone in Brazil who says "o real está em liquidação" is making a pun: the real is "on clearance", cheap to pick up. agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br

"economia" here means saving, not running an economy On Nov. 11, Agência Brasil wrote of a package "com potencial de gerar uma economia entre R$ 50 bilhões e R$ 60 bilhões em 2027". Uma economia with an article and an amount is a saving, not the national economy. ES says el ahorro, plural los ahorros. Also watch the gender: DE das Paket is neuter, PT o pacote and ES el paquete are masculine. And Mexico says inversionistas where Spain says inversores. oliberal.com · agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br
