Malware & Login-Wave Vocabulary: infiltrar, bloquear, redefinir a senha

Tuesday, November 17 — rotation topic Software & Technology, installment sixteen. Today's software brief offers two big hooks. First: attackers used a flaw in Optimizely's signup flow to plant malicious code in 188 scripts from 178 organizations, including CNN, BBC and GitLab. Second: a wave of automated login attempts has locked many BundID accounts; password login is switched off, but login via eID still works. So today we learn how to say in PT-BR and ES-MX that you plant code, lock an account and reset a password. Plus a false friend from the soccer pitch that also lurks in the office: o time. 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 3 refreshers.

  • FALSE FRIEND o time ≠ the time — in Brazil, o time means the squad or the team. Priberam marks the word [Brasil] and gives EQUIPA, the word from Portugal, as a synonym. Time (as in the clock) is o tempo. priberam.org
  • BR-PT a equipe, not a equipa — Priberam lists both spellings: „Grafia no Brasil: equipe" and „Grafia em Portugal: equipa". priberam.org
  • BR-PT a senha, not a palavra-passe — Linguee marks senha with [Br.] and palavra-passe with [Port.]. Resetting a password is redefinir a senha. linguee.de
  • CONJUGATION eu bloqueio, yo bloqueo — in Portuguese the stressed e becomes ei (bloqueio, bloqueia, bloqueie). In Spanish it stays: bloqueo, and the preterite has two es: yo bloqueé. conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net
  • CONJUGATION du schleust ein, no double s — the stem ends in -s, so the s of the ending drops out. Perfect tense with haben: hat eingeschleust. verbformen.de
  • GENDER das Team → a equipe / o time → el equipo — neuter in German, feminine or masculine in Portuguese depending on the word, masculine in Spanish. priberam.org
  • PHRASE das Einfallstor = a porta de entrada = la puerta de entrada — PT and ES don't say "gate" but "front door". linguee.de · linguee.de

Three Verbs: einschleusen, sperren, umstellen

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

What a supply-chain attack is: Picture a huge central kitchen that delivers food to 178 cafeterias every day. Whoever poisons the central kitchen hits all 178 cafeterias at once, without setting foot in any of them. That is exactly how it went with Optimizely. Many websites load a script from this vendor for A/B tests. According to BleepingComputer, attackers exploited a flaw in the signup flow and afterwards 188 infected scripts from 178 organizations were found, including CNN, BBC and GitLab. So the malicious code came through the supplier, not through the sites themselves. bleepingcomputer.com
Verb DE weak, separable (ein-) → participle eingeschleust, perfect with haben · PT-BR infiltrar, regular -ar · ES-MX infiltrar, regular -ar
einschleusen

Die Angreifer haben Schadcode in die Test-Skripte eingeschleust.

Über eine präparierte Bibliothek schleust man Malware in tausende Builds ein.

Niemand bemerkte, wie der Code eingeschleust wurde.

to smuggle in / to plant

The attackers have smuggled malicious code into the test scripts.

With a tampered library, you can plant malware in thousands of builds.

Nobody noticed how the code was smuggled in.

infiltrar

Os atacantes infiltraram código malicioso nos scripts de teste.

Com uma biblioteca adulterada, dá para infiltrar malware em milhares de builds.

Ninguém percebeu como o código foi infiltrado.

infiltrar

Los atacantes infiltraron código malicioso en los scripts de prueba.

Con una biblioteca alterada, se puede infiltrar malware en miles de builds.

Nadie se dio cuenta de cómo se infiltró el código.

A Lock for Data A Schleuse (canal lock) moves ships step by step from one water level to another. So einschleusen means: to bring something in secretly and gradually, past the checkpoint. Linguee gives smuggle in, infiltrate and, colloquially, plant as English renderings. PT and ES use the loanword infiltrar. linguee.de

Service Provider, Credentials, Point of Entry — and a False Team

The words from every supply-chain report and every login outage

Why a hard lockout backfires: Imagine someone trying stolen keys on a thousand front doors. Each door locks itself for good after three wrong tries. The burglar doesn't get in, but neither can the real residents. The technical term for this is credential stuffing: attackers test stolen login credentials in bulk and automatically. At BundID, according to heise, locked accounts only come free again by resetting the password or via the eID. heise.de
Noun DE m, pl. - (f die Dienstleisterin) · EN service provider · PT-BR o prestador de serviços, m · ES-MX el proveedor de servicios, m
der Dienstleister

Der Einstieg bei Uber lief über einen Dienstleister.

Jeder Dienstleister mit einem Schlüssel gehört zum eigenen Risiko.

the service provider

The way into Uber went through a service provider.

Every service provider with a key is part of your own risk.

o prestador de serviços

A invasão da Uber começou por um prestador de serviços.

Todo prestador de serviços com uma chave faz parte do seu próprio risco.

el proveedor de servicios

La intrusión en Uber empezó por un proveedor de servicios.

Cada proveedor de servicios con una llave forma parte de tu propio riesgo.

Why this fits today According to BleepingComputer, Uber reports 149,346 affected drivers. An unknown party had taken over a service provider's internal login credentials. Uber does not name the provider; according to BleepingComputer, TechCrunch names the AI training vendor Fireworks. For Dienstleister, Linguee also lists fornecedor de serviços and provedor de serviços in PT, and prestador de servicios in ES. bleepingcomputer.com · linguee.de · linguee.de
Noun DE plural only · EN login credentials · PT-BR os dados de acesso, m pl. · ES-MX los datos de acceso, m pl.

You will receive your login credentials by email.

Gate or door? German thinks of a city gate through which an army „einfällt" (invades). PT and ES are more domestic: the front door. Linguee shows the same image in business texts: „Hong Kong continua a ser uma porta de entrada" and „sigue teniendo el papel de puerta de entrada". So the word also works positively, for example for a market. linguee.de · linguee.de

The noun is safe, the verb is not O suporte is technical support, no catch. So Não suporto mais esse login means "I can't stand this login anymore". priberam.org

It's Tuesday, November seventeenth. Today's rotation topic is software and technology, installment sixteen. We're covering planting code, locking accounts and passwords. Ten entries, including three verbs with full conjugation and three refreshers.

The first hook is Optimizely. According to BleepingComputer, attackers used a flaw in the signup flow to plant malicious code in one hundred eighty-eight scripts from one hundred seventy-eight organizations, including CNN, BBC and GitLab. In Portuguese and Spanish, to plant or smuggle in is infiltrar, completely regular. The point of entry is a porta de entrada and la puerta de entrada, literally the front door.

The second hook is BundID. A wave of automated login attempts has locked many accounts. To lock is bloquear in both languages. In Portuguese the stressed e becomes ei: eu bloqueio. In Spanish it stays: yo bloqueo, and in the preterite you write two e's: yo bloqueé. To get back in, you have to reset your password. In Brazil that's redefinir a senha, in Mexico restablecer la contraseña.

Then there's Uber. According to BleepingComputer, nearly one hundred fifty thousand drivers are affected, and the way in was a service provider's login credentials. The service provider is o prestador de serviços and el proveedor de servicios. Login credentials are os dados de acesso and los datos de acceso.

The false friend of the day is o time. In Brazil that means the team, not the time on the clock. In Portugal people say equipa, in Brazil equipe. We also refreshed sperren, umstellen and support. Tomorrow the topic shifts to family and daily life.
