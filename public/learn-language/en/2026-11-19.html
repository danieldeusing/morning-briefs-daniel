Vocabulary: The Title Race: empatar, apitar, substituir

Thursday, November 19 — rotation topic Soccer & Sports, installment six. Last week was about relegation; today we look to the top. In the ValorFinal table (as of Nov 19, 2 a.m.), Flamengo leads with 79 points ahead of Palmeiras with 73, both after 35 games. We have these two numbers from this one source only; older search hits showed other standings. At the bottom, something is already decided: Chapecoense is confirmed relegated with 21 points. So today we learn how to equalize (ausgleichen), how the referee blows the final whistle (abpfeifen) and how the coach substitutes (auswechseln). Plus a false friend: the German Meister is not the English master. 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 2 reviews.

  • FALSE FRIEND der Meister ≠ the master — in sports, the Meister is the champion. „Meister werden" means to win the title, not to become a master.
  • BR-PT o técnico — in Brazil the coach is usually called técnico, in Portugal treinador. In Mexico people say el director técnico, or el DT for short.
  • IDIOM o artilheiro (PT) = the top scorer, literally "the artilleryman". In Mexico it is el campeón de goleo.
  • CONJUGATION substituo · sustituyo — PT keeps the i, ES inserts a y: sustituyo, sustituyó, sustituya. conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net
  • CONJUGATION pfiff ab · abgepfiffen — abpfeifen is strong: ei becomes i, and the f doubles. Perfect with haben. verbformen.de
  • GENDER a final · o final — PT a final (feminine) is the championship game, o final (masculine) is the end. ES distinguishes the same way: la final and el final.

Verb DE strong, separable (der Gast gleicht aus), ablaut ei → i → participle ausgeglichen, perfect with haben · PT-BR empatar, regular -ar · ES-MX empatar, regular -ar
ausgleichen

Kurz vor dem Abpfiff glich der Gast noch aus.

Wenn wir jetzt ausgleichen, ist alles wieder offen.

Hat Palmeiras schon ausgeglichen?

to equalize

Just before the final whistle, the visitors equalized.

If we equalize now, everything is open again.

Has Palmeiras equalized yet?

empatar

Pouco antes do apito final, o visitante empatou.

Se a gente empatar agora, fica tudo aberto de novo.

O Palmeiras já empatou?

empatar

Poco antes del silbatazo final, el visitante empató.

Si empatamos ahora, todo vuelve a estar abierto.

¿Ya empató el Palmeiras?

Show conjugation

DE: ablaut gleich- → glich → geglichen; in a main clause, aus goes at the end of the sentence. Subjunctive II as a true form: gliche aus; in everyday speech people usually say würde ausgleichen. EN: British equalise; in the US you often hear tie the game. PT and ES: empatar means two things — "to equalize" and "to draw": o jogo terminou empatado, el partido terminó empatado. With nós and nosotros, present and preterite are the same (empatamos). verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Verb DE strong, separable (er pfeift ab), ablaut ei → i with double f → participle abgepfiffen, perfect with haben · PT-BR apitar (o fim do jogo), regular -ar · ES-MX pitar (el final), regular -ar
abpfeifen

Der Schiedsrichter pfeift pünktlich ab.

Er hat das Spiel erst nach 97 Minuten abgepfiffen.

Wann pfeift er endlich ab?

to blow the final whistle

The referee blows the final whistle right on time.

He didn't blow the final whistle until 97 minutes had been played.

When is he finally going to blow the whistle?

apitar

O árbitro apita o fim do jogo na hora certa.

Ele só apitou o fim do jogo depois de 97 minutos.

Quando é que ele vai apitar o fim?

pitar

El árbitro pita el final a la hora exacta.

Pitó el final hasta los 97 minutos.

¿Cuándo va a pitar el final por fin?

Show conjugation

DE: pfeifen → pfiff → gepfiffen, the f doubles after a short i. Subjunctive II as a true form: pfiffe ab. The related noun: der Abpfiff; the opposite is anpfeifen and der Anpfiff. EN: blow – blew – blown; the table shows only the verb, the final whistle is added. PT: apitar comes from o apito (the whistle). In Brazil the referee is also colloquially called o juiz. ES-MX: watch hasta in the second sentence. In Mexico, pitó hasta los 97 means "didn't blow the final whistle until 97 minutes". verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Verb DE weak, separable (der Trainer wechselt ihn aus) → participle ausgewechselt, perfect with haben · PT-BR substituir, -uir verb with accent (substituí) · ES-MX sustituir, y-insertion (sustituyo)
auswechseln

Der Trainer wechselt den Stürmer in der 60. Minute aus.

Nach der Gelben Karte wurde er ausgewechselt.

Warum wechselt er ihn nicht aus?

to substitute

The coach substitutes the striker in the 60th minute.

He was substituted after the yellow card.

Why doesn't he take him off?

substituir

O técnico substitui o atacante aos 60 minutos.

Depois do cartão amarelo, ele foi substituído.

Por que ele não substitui o jogador?

sustituir

El técnico sustituye al delantero al minuto 60.

Después de la tarjeta amarilla, lo sustituyeron.

¿Por qué no lo sustituye?

Show conjugation

DE: stem ending in -el: ich wechsle aus (also wechsele), wir wechseln aus. Subjunctive II as a würde form, because wechselte equals the preterite. The player who comes on is eingewechselt. EN: neutral substitute; in soccer people often say take off (out) and bring on (in). PT: substituir gets an accent where the i is stressed: substituímos, substituí, substituía; but ele substitui has no accent. ES: between u and an ending in o, e, a a y appears: sustituyo, sustituyó, sustituya. In Mexico people just as often simply say cambiar. verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Already know it: ventaja On Nov 12, la ventaja de local came up, the home advantage. So the same word means "advantage" and "lead". DE, by contrast, separates them: the Vorteil helps you, with a Vorsprung you are already ahead. The numbers come from the ValorFinal table of Nov 19. valorfinal.com.br

Who chases, who is second DE thinks of the chase: verfolgen. PT and ES simply name the place: vice-líder and sublíder mean "runner-up". There is also a PT word for "chaser", o perseguidor, but in table language you almost always hear vice-líder.

False friend: der Meister In sports, the German Meister is the champion. The English master never fits here: „Flamengo ist Meister" means Flamengo are the champions. The math in the first sentence: two wins take Flamengo to 85 points, Palmeiras reaches 82 at most. In Brazil, after the title people often say levantar a taça, "to lift the trophy". valorfinal.com.br

Technique instead of training In Brazil the coach is usually o técnico; in Portugal people tend to say o treinador. In Mexico he is el director técnico and on the radio simply el DT ("de-te"). EN: in England, professional soccer often says the manager, in the US the head coach. Note segundo tempo (PT) and segundo tiempo (ES) for the second half.

To equalize is empatar in both Portuguese and Spanish, and the word also means to draw. To blow the final whistle is apitar in Portuguese and pitar in Spanish. The lead is a vantagem in Portuguese and la ventaja in Spanish. The closest challenger is the vice-líder in Brazil and the sublíder in Mexico. The top scorer is o artilheiro in Portuguese, literally the artilleryman, and el campeón de goleo in Mexico. The coach is o técnico in Brazil and el director técnico in Mexico.
