Three Verbs for the Week: ernennen, anheben, dementieren

3 verbs with full conjugation, including 1 review · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

One story, three verbs: Imagine a family that spends more than it earns. The father looks for someone to run the household budget. He appoints a new treasurer. At the same time, the bank in the neighboring country raises its interest rates: parking money there suddenly pays more. Many people take their money out of the family fund and bring it over there. Rumors circulate in the neighborhood about who will become treasurer. The father denies the false ones. That is exactly what this week looks like in Brazil.

nomear

Segundo a Reuters, ele pode nomeá-lo ainda nesta semana.

nombrar

Lula tiene que nombrar a un nuevo ministro de Hacienda.

Según Reuters, podría nombrarlo esta misma semana.

La empresa la nombró jefa del equipo.

Who gets appointed? According to Reuters on Nov. 19, Rafael Fonteles and Geraldo Alckmin are in front; the decision "pode ocorrer já na próxima semana". In Portuguese, watch for the inserted i. Where the stress falls on the stem, you get -ei-: eu nomeio, ele nomeia, que eles nomeiem. Where it falls on the ending, it is missing: nós nomeamos, que nós nomeemos. Spanish stays completely regular. And be careful: ES nombrar also means "to name, to mention", and so does PT nomear. In German, nennen and ernennen are two separate verbs. anheben

Der Markt rechnet damit, dass die Fed am 9. Dezember die Zinsen anhebt.

Hat die Bank die Gebühren schon wieder angehoben?

Ich hob den Koffer an und merkte: viel zu schwer.

to raise

The market expects the Fed to raise rates on December 9.

Has the bank raised its fees again?

I lifted the suitcase and noticed: far too heavy.

elevar

O mercado espera que o Fed eleve os juros em 9 de dezembro.

O banco elevou as tarifas de novo?

subir

El mercado espera que la Fed suba las tasas el 9 de diciembre.

¿El banco volvió a subir las comisiones?

Raising rates, lifting a suitcase — two verbs in PT and ES DE anheben applies to numbers and to objects. EN separates them: raise for interest rates, lift for the suitcase. PT and ES separate them the same way: rates take elevar or subir, the suitcase takes levantar. That is why the third example uses different verbs. According to Trading Economics, on Nov. 23 the market prices in about 93% for a Fed hike of 25 basis points on December 9. After esperar que comes the subjunctive: PT que o Fed eleve, ES que la Fed suba. The preterite hub an is listed at verbformen.de as a variant form; in everyday speech people say hob an. tradingeconomics.com · verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Quick refresher — two stem changes, one verb On Nov. 2 you learned dementieren, along with the false friend demente (= demented). The table is the trap. PT changes e→i only in eu desminto and throughout the subjunctive (desminta, desmintamos); você desmente stays. ES changes e→ie in all stressed present forms (desmiento, desmientes, desmienten) and e→i in desmintió, desmintieron, desmintamos. Mexico says el vocero, Brazil o porta-voz. conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Finance Minister, Chamber, Exchange-Rate Jump — the Words That Go With Them

6 nouns, 1 idiom and 1 review · meanings checked against Dicio, DEM (COLMEX) and the Senado glossary, where linked

Why the euro is worth so much this week: Picture the Brazilian treasury as a water barrel with a hole. The government wants to make the hole smaller: that is fiscal consolidation, PT o ajuste fiscal. The Senate has already signed off on the plan; now the Chamber of Deputies has to agree. At the same time, the man who manages the barrel is missing, the finance minister. And in the US, a rate hike is coming that lures money away from Brazil. As long as all of this is open, fewer people want reais. That is why the euro made an exchange-rate jump.

der Finanzminister

Brasilien wartet auf den neuen Finanzminister.

Ein Finanzminister mit Spar-Signal könnte den Real stützen.

the finance minister

Brazil is waiting for the new finance minister.

A finance minister who signals savings could support the real.

o ministro da Fazenda

O Brasil espera o novo ministro da Fazenda.

Um ministro da Fazenda que sinalize corte de gastos pode sustentar o real.

el secretario de Hacienda

Brasil espera al nuevo ministro de Hacienda.

Un secretario de Hacienda que prometa recortes podría sostener el real.

Fazenda: farm and public treasury In Ribeirão Claro, a fazenda is a farm with cattle or coffee. Dicio gives first "Grande propriedade destinada à lavoura ou à criação de gado". Then comes the second meaning: "Conjunto dos órgãos que, em um governo, são responsáveis pela administração do dinheiro de um país". That is why the finance ministry in Brazil is called a Fazenda and the minister ministro da Fazenda. In Mexico the post is not called minister but secretario de Hacienda; about a foreign country, people there also say ministro de Hacienda. ES hacienda is likewise both an estate and the public treasury — the same double image. dicio.com.br · Reuters via noticiasagricolas

"a Câmara" — which chamber? In the news, a Câmara is enough: it means the Chamber of Deputies in Brasília. Locally, a Câmara Municipal is the city council, and its members are called vereadores. Mexico has the same pair: Cámara de Diputados and Senado. Note the subjunctive after se in PT: se a Câmara mudar is the future subjunctive (Futuro do Subjuntivo), while ES simply says si la Cámara cambia. According to Gazeta do Povo, the text is to be debated "na próxima segunda-feira (23), em sessão extraordinária"; SBT News named Tuesday. gazetadopovo.com.br · sbtnews.sbt.com.br

One long German word, two short Romance ones Where German builds a word monster, PT and ES say ajuste fiscal, literally "budget adjustment". The Senado explains it like this: "Conjunto de medidas que visa equilibrar as contas públicas, fazendo com que o governo gaste menos do que arrecada." On Nov. 19, Reuters headlined about the interest-rate futures: "desconfiança sobre ajuste fiscal do governo". Watch the number word: PT bilhão = DE Milliarde (EN billion). ES-MX says mil millones; a billón there is a trillion. Mexico says inversionistas, not inversores. senado.leg.br · Reuters via noticiasagricolas · gazetadopovo.com.br

el alza, but la nueva alza The DEM lists alza as s f: "Aumento del precio o del valor de algo". Because the word starts with a stressed a, el or un stands directly before it in the singular: el alza, un alza. Everything else stays feminine: la nueva alza, esta alza, las alzas. The same rule applies to el agua. The opposite word in financial Spanish is la baja; the DEM lists the phrase tendencia al alza. PT does not need this rule: a alta, a alta dos juros. One image, four languages — and a stem change Everything fits together here: Gürtel, belt, cinto, cinturón.
