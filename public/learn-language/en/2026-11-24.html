Vocabulary: Attack & Outage: atacar, capturar, agregar

Tuesday, November 24 — rotation topic Software & Technology, episode seventeen. Today's software brief gives us three occasions. The root hole in Cisco Expressway is under attack since a proof of concept went public. At two Norwegian energy companies, attackers siphoned off secrets from Azure API Management. And Microsoft's final report explains the Exchange outage with load tests on old hardware. So today we learn angreifen (to attack), abgreifen (to siphon off) and ergänzen (to add), plus the words from the post-mortems. Today's false friend sits between Brazil and Mexico: capturar. 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 3 reviews.

  • FALSE FRIEND capturar (PT-BR) ≠ capturar (ES-MX) — in Brazil, capturar dados means "to siphon off data". In Mexico, the DEM lists a computing meaning: entering data by keyboard. Capturar los datos there is office work, not an attack. dem.colmex.mx
  • CONJUGATION eu ataquei, yo ataqué — before e, c becomes qu so the sound stays hard. This also applies in the subjunctive: que você ataque, que tú ataques. conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net
  • CONJUGATION yo agregué, que agregue — the same rule with g: before e it becomes gu, otherwise the g would turn soft. reverso.net
  • CONJUGATION ihr grifft ab — greifen and its family are strong verbs: griff, gegriffen, with a double f. So in the preterite it is ihr grifft ab, in the subjunctive II ihr griffet ab. verbformen.de
  • GENDER der Ausfall → la falla — masculine in German, feminine in the Mexican dictionary: "falla de una máquina". Linguee gives the masculine el fallo as the main translation. dem.colmex.mx · linguee.de
  • BR-PT der Patch = a correção [Br.] — Linguee marks correção as the Brazilian translation of Patch. In Spanish it is el parche, literally "the patch (of cloth)". linguee.de · linguee.de
  • BR-PT o usuário, not o utilizador — Priberam marks the computing meaning of usuário with [Brasil]. priberam.org

Three Verbs: angreifen, abgreifen, ergänzen

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

What a public PoC changes: Imagine a lock has a design flaw. As long as only the manufacturer knows about it, little happens. Then someone posts building instructions for the matching lock pick online. From now on, anyone can try it. A proof of concept (PoC) is exactly that kind of instruction for a vulnerability. According to The Register, the attacks on Cisco Expressway began after a PoC went public. According to heise, the flaw CVE-2026-76466 allows commands as root, with no login at all. theregister.com · heise.de
Verb DE strong, separable (an-), ablaut greift an · griff an · hat angegriffen, perfect with haben, + accusative · PT-BR atacar, c→qu before e (ataquei, ataque) · ES-MX atacar, c→qu before e (ataqué, ataque)
angreifen

Seit der PoC öffentlich ist, wird die Lücke in Cisco Expressway angegriffen.

Die Angreifer greifen vor allem Systeme am Rand des Netzes an.

Wer griff zuerst an — ein Bot oder ein Mensch?

to attack

Since the PoC went public, the hole in Cisco Expressway has been under attack.

The attackers mainly attack systems at the edge of the network.

Who attacked first — a bot or a human?

atacar

Desde que a PoC ficou pública, a falha no Cisco Expressway está sendo atacada.

Os atacantes atacam principalmente sistemas na borda da rede.

Quem atacou primeiro: um bot ou uma pessoa?

atacar

Desde que la PoC se hizo pública, la vulnerabilidad de Cisco Expressway está siendo atacada.

Los atacantes atacan sobre todo sistemas en el borde de la red.

¿Quién atacó primero: un bot o una persona?

Why this fits today According to heise, the security firm F6 confirmed the attacks. Cisco itself says it has no direct evidence, but names version X15.5.20 as the fix. You already know the noun for this verb from Nov. 10: der Angreifer = o atacante = el atacante. heise.de

FALSE FRIEND Verb DE strong, separable (ab-), like greifen: griff ab, hat abgegriffen, perfect with haben, colloquial · PT-BR capturar, regular -ar · ES-MX robar, regular -ar — careful: ES-MX capturar = to key in data
abgreifen

Bei zwei norwegischen Energiefirmen haben Angreifer Secrets aus Azure API Management abgegriffen.

Spyware greift alles ab, was du ins Formular tippst.

Die Täter griffen Kartennummern samt CVV ab.

to siphon off / to grab

At two Norwegian energy companies, attackers siphoned off secrets from Azure API Management.

Spyware grabs everything you type into the form.

The criminals siphoned off card numbers along with the CVV.

capturar

Em duas empresas de energia norueguesas, atacantes capturaram segredos do Azure API Management.

O spyware captura tudo o que você digita no formulário.

Os criminosos capturaram números de cartão junto com o CVV.

robar

En dos empresas de energía noruegas, los atacantes robaron secretos de Azure API Management.

El spyware roba todo lo que escribes en el formulario.

Los delincuentes robaron números de tarjeta junto con el CVV.

Capturar is not the same as capturar In Brazil, capturar dados means "to siphon off data". But the Mexican dictionary DEM lists a fifth meaning for capturar: "(Comp) Registrar, por medio del teclado, un dato o un conjunto de datos en el dispositivo de memoria de una computadora". In Mexico, capturar los datos is therefore data entry. For the attack, say robar or interceptar there. Linguee shows exactly that for spyware: "interceptar informações" and "interceptar datos". dem.colmex.mx · linguee.de · linguee.de

Verb DE weak, inseparable (er-) → participle ergänzt without ge-, perfect with haben, etw. um etw. ergänzen · PT-BR acrescentar, regular -ar · ES-MX agregar, g→gu before e (agregué, agregue)
ergänzen

Am 23. November hat Cisco seinen Hinweis um die KEV-Listung ergänzt.

Ergänz bitte die Liste um die alten Testinstanzen.

Wir ergänzen die Doku, sobald der Fix draußen ist.

to add (to) / to supplement

On November 23, Cisco added the KEV listing to its advisory.

Please add the old test instances to the list.

We'll add to the docs as soon as the fix is out.

acrescentar

No dia 23 de novembro, a Cisco acrescentou a listagem no KEV ao seu aviso.

Acrescente as instâncias de teste antigas à lista, por favor.

Vamos acrescentar isso à documentação assim que a correção sair.

agregar

El 23 de noviembre, Cisco agregó la inclusión en el KEV a su aviso.

Agrega a la lista las instancias de prueba viejas, por favor.

Agregaremos eso a la documentación en cuanto salga el parche.

Which one is the object? In German, the whole is the object, and the new part follows um: den Hinweis um die Listung ergänzen. PT, ES and EN flip this. The new part is the object, and the whole follows a or to: acrescentar a listagem ao aviso, agregar la inclusión a su aviso, add the listing to the advisory.

Outage, Load Test, Fix, Vault — the Words of the Post-Mortems

What appears in every final report and every security advisory

Why secrets belong in a vault: Imagine you tape your front-door key to the front door. Convenient, but everyone who walks by sees it. A secret in plain text in a gateway's configuration is exactly that kind of key. According to The Register, attackers searched instances of Azure API Management (Classic tiers only) for secrets. The Norwegian authority NSM names Equinor and Skagerak Energi as victims and advises rotating all tokens. A vault like Azure Key Vault keeps the key separate; the gateway only points to it. theregister.com
GENDER Noun DE m, pl. Ausfälle · EN outage · PT-BR a falha, f (also a indisponibilidade) · ES-MX la falla, f (Linguee main translation: el fallo, m)
der Ausfall

Der Exchange-Ausfall dauerte vom 12. bis zum 14. November.

Bei einem Ausfall springt der zweite Server ein.

the outage

The Exchange outage lasted from November 12 to 14.

In case of an outage, the second server takes over.

a falha

A falha do Exchange durou de 12 a 14 de novembro.

Em caso de falha, o segundo servidor assume.

la falla

La falla de Exchange duró del 12 al 14 de noviembre.

En caso de falla, el segundo servidor toma el relevo.

Why this fits today According to The Register, at the worst moment more than 80% of Exchange Online's North American gateway servers did not respond. The cause was a code change from July, combined with a failover. On gender: the DEM lists la falla with the example "falla de una máquina". For Ausfall in PT, Linguee gives falha, and less often interrupção and indisponibilidade. theregister.com · dem.colmex.mx · linguee.de
Noun DE m, pl. -s · EN load test · PT-BR o teste de carga, m · ES-MX la prueba de carga, f
der Lasttest

Die Lasttests liefen auf älterer Hardware mit weniger Kernen.

Vor dem Release fahren wir einen Lasttest mit zehnfachem Verkehr.

the load test

The load tests ran on older hardware with fewer cores.

Before the release, we run a load test with ten times the traffic.

o teste de carga

Os testes de carga rodaram em hardware mais antigo, com menos núcleos.

Antes do lançamento, fazemos um teste de carga com dez vezes o tráfego.

la prueba de carga

Las pruebas de carga corrieron en hardware más viejo, con menos núcleos.

Antes del lanzamiento, hacemos una prueba de carga con diez veces el tráfico.

Last becomes carga German thinks of a load (Last) that you carry. PT and ES use carga, the cargo or charge. According to The Register, this is the core of Microsoft's report: the bug only got bad on newer machines, but the tests ran on older ones. Microsoft promises load tests on new hardware. For Lasttest in PT, Linguee gives teste de carga. theregister.com · linguee.de
BR-PT Noun DE m, pl. -es (anglicism, synonym der Patch) · EN fix / patch · PT-BR a correção, f [Br.] · ES-MX el parche, m
der Fix

Freie Fixes gibt es laut heise nur für Spring Security 7.0.9 und 7.1.3.

Der Fix ist draußen, aber noch nicht eingespielt.

the fix / the patch

According to heise, free fixes are only available for Spring Security 7.0.9 and 7.1.3.

The fix is out, but it hasn't been applied yet.

a correção

Segundo o heise, as correções gratuitas só existem para o Spring Security 7.0.9 e 7.1.3.

A correção já saiu, mas ainda não foi aplicada.

el parche

Según heise, los parches gratuitos solo están disponibles para Spring Security 7.0.9 y 7.1.3.

El parche ya salió, pero todavía no se aplicó.

Correction or patch of cloth? For the synonym Patch, Linguee gives correção in PT with the label [Br.], less often remendo (a cloth patch). In ES it is parche, plus parche de seguridad for the security patch. In software texts, PT often keeps the English word too: „Se você instalou um patch ou fez um upgrade". linguee.de · linguee.de
Noun DE m, pl. -e · EN safe / vault · PT-BR o cofre, m · ES-MX la caja fuerte, f
der Tresor

Secrets gehören in einen Tresor, nicht in die Konfiguration des Gateways.

Der Schlüssel zum Tresor liegt nicht im selben Repo.

the safe / the vault

Secrets belong in a vault, not in the gateway's configuration.

The key to the vault isn't stored in the same repo.

o cofre

Segredos devem ficar num cofre, não na configuração do gateway.

A chave do cofre não fica no mesmo repositório.

la caja fuerte

Los secretos deben ir en una caja fuerte, no en la configuración del gateway.

La llave de la caja fuerte no está en el mismo repositorio.

One word or two For Tresor in PT, Linguee gives only cofre. In ES, caja fuerte is the main translation, literally "strong box"; less often cámara acorazada and bóveda de banco for the walk-in bank vault. linguee.de · linguee.de

Why this fits today Cleverbridge is a software-store service provider based in Germany. According to The Register, the company noticed unauthorized access on Nov. 4 and informed those affected from Nov. 20. Card numbers, expiry dates and CVV/CVC codes were stolen. The image stays the same in all languages: something runs out — Leck, leak, vazamento, fuga. theregister.com

Brazil or Portugal? Priberam lists the computing meaning of usuário with the label [Brasil]: „Pessoa que faz uso do computador, de programas, sistemas ou serviços informáticos." It gives UTILIZADOR, the word from Portugal, as a synonym. priberam.org

It's Tuesday, November twenty-fourth. Today's rotation topic is software and technology, episode seventeen. We're looking at attacks, siphoned-off data and a big outage. Ten entries: three verbs with full conjugation and three reviews.

The first occasion is Cisco Expressway. According to The Register, attacks on a root flaw began after a proof of concept went public. To attack is atacar in both Portuguese and Spanish. Before an e, the c becomes qu: eu ataquei in Brazil, yo ataqué in Mexico. On November twenty-third, Cisco added the KEV listing to its advisory. To add is acrescentar in Brazil and agregar in Mexico, with the same rule: yo agregué.

The second occasion comes from Norway. According to The Register, attackers siphoned off secrets from Azure API Management at two energy companies. In Brazil, the word for that is capturar. But watch out: in Mexico, capturar on a computer means something quite different, namely keying in data. For the theft, it's better to say robar or interceptar there. And secrets belong in a vault: o cofre, la caja fuerte.

The third occasion is Microsoft's final report on the Exchange outage. According to The Register, at the worst moment more than eighty percent of the North American gateway servers did not respond. The load tests ran on older hardware. The outage is a falha in Portuguese and a falla in Spanish, and in Spanish the word is feminine. A load test is o teste de carga and la prueba de carga.

We reviewed the vulnerability, the data leak and the user. The user is o usuário in Brazil and o utilizador in Portugal. And the fix is a correção in Brazil and el parche in Mexico. Tomorrow the topic switches to family and daily life.
