Rainy-Weekend Vocabulary: combinar, secar, prender

Wednesday, November 25 — rotation topic Family & Everyday Life, episode eighteen. Today's family brief sees little sun: according to HG Brasil, it rains every day from Saturday through Tuesday at 100%. Plan B is still in place. The children's ballet "Casa de Brinquedos" in Londrina runs indoors, on Saturday and Sunday at 4 p.m. And Sunday is the first Sunday of Advent. So today we learn how to make plans, why the laundry won't dry and how to light the first candle. There is also a false friend between Brazil and Mexico: prender. 10 entries, including 3 verbs with full conjugation, among them 3 reviews.

  • FALSE FRIEND prender (ES-MX) ≠ prender (PT-BR) — in Mexico, prender una vela means "to light a candle" and prender la luz means "to turn on the light". In Brazil, prender means "to fasten" or "to arrest". There, "to light" is acender. dem.colmex.mx · priberam.org
  • FALSE FRIEND combinar (PT-BR) ≠ combinar (ES-MX) — in Brazil, combinar um encontro means "to arrange to meet". In Mexico, combinar only means "to combine" or "to match". There people say quedamos en vernos. dicio.com.br · dem.colmex.mx
  • CONJUGATION ich habe getrocknet, es ist getrocknet — trocknen takes two auxiliary verbs. If you dry something, use haben. If something dries on its own, use sein. verbformen.de
  • CONJUGATION eu sequei, yo sequé — before e, c becomes qu so the sound stays hard. This also applies in the subjunctive: que você seque, que tú seques. conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net
  • BR-PT o toró = the downpour — Priberam marks toró with [Brasil]: "Chuva forte". In Mexico, a short, heavy rain is el aguacero. priberam.org · dem.colmex.mx
  • IDIOM chover canivete (PT-BR) = "it's raining cats and dogs", literally "it's raining pocketknives". In Mexico it rains a cántaros, that is, "in jugs". wiktionary.org · dem.colmex.mx
  • GENDER der Kranz → a coroa, la corona — German der Adventskranz is masculine, while PT a coroa and ES la corona are feminine. linguee.de

Three Verbs: to Arrange, to Dry, to Light

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

Why trocknen has two auxiliary verbs: Ask yourself: is someone doing something, or is it happening on its own? If you dry the towels with a hair dryer, you are doing something to them. Then it's ich habe die Handtücher getrocknet. If the towels simply dry overnight, nobody does anything. Then it's die Handtücher sind getrocknet. Verbformen.de puts it this way: the auxiliary is usually haben, "es gibt jedoch auch Zeitformen mit dem Hilfsverb ‚sein‘". Spanish shows the same difference with se: sequé las toallas versus las toallas se secaron. verbformen.de
FALSE FRIEND Verb DE weak, reflexive, inseparable (ver-) → participle verabredet without ge-, perfect with haben, sich mit jdm. für etw. verabreden · PT-BR combinar, regular -ar · ES-MX quedar, regular -ar, quedar en / quedar de + infinitive
sich verabreden

Wir haben uns für Samstag um 16 Uhr zum Ballett in Londrina verabredet.

Verabreden wir uns lieber an einem überdachten Ort — es soll bis Dienstag regnen.

Habt ihr schon verabredet, wer fährt?

to arrange (to meet) / to make plans

We've arranged to go to the ballet in Londrina on Saturday at 4 p.m.

Let's arrange to meet somewhere covered — it's supposed to rain until Tuesday.

Have you already arranged who's driving?

combinar

A gente combinou de ir ao balé em Londrina no sábado às 16h.

É melhor a gente combinar de se encontrar num lugar coberto: deve chover até terça.

Vocês já combinaram quem vai dirigir?

quedar (en / de)

Quedamos de ir al ballet en Londrina el sábado a las 4 de la tarde.

Mejor quedemos de vernos en un lugar techado: va a llover hasta el martes.

¿Ya quedaron en quién va a manejar?

Combinar is not the same as combinar The Brazilian dictionary Dicio gives combinar the meaning "Acordar algo com outra pessoa; pactuar, aprazar: combinar um encontro". The Mexican DEM does not list this meaning. There, combinar means "to combine" or "to match": "El arroz combina con los frijoles". For arranging to meet, the DEM uses quedar: "Quedamos en vernos a las cinco de la tarde" and "Quedamos de comer juntos pasado mañana". dicio.com.br · dem.colmex.mx · dem.colmex.mx

DE: weak and regular, reflexive with an accusative pronoun (mich, dich, sich, uns, euch, sich). The unstressed ver- blocks the ge-: hat sich verabredet. The stem ends in -d, hence du verabredest, ihr verabredetet euch. Subjunctive II = preterite forms. PT combinar and ES quedar: fully regular. For nós / nosotros, the present and the preterite are the same (combinamos, quedamos). verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

trocknen

Bei 100 % Regen trocknet die Wäsche draußen nicht.

Ich habe die Handtücher im Bad getrocknet.

Die Gummistiefel sind über Nacht nicht getrocknet.

to dry

With a 100% chance of rain, the laundry won't dry outside.

I dried the towels in the bathroom.

The rubber boots didn't dry overnight.

secar

Com 100% de chance de chuva, a roupa não seca lá fora.

Sequei as toalhas no banheiro.

As botas de borracha não secaram durante a noite.

secar / secarse

Con 100% de probabilidad de lluvia, la ropa no se seca afuera.

Sequé las toallas en el baño.

Las botas de hule no se secaron en la noche.

Why this fits today The family brief lists only one drier day for Ribeirão Claro: Friday, with 20% rain according to HG Brasil. Saturday through Tuesday are each at 100%. So whoever does laundry on Friday has the smallest drying problem. Note: in PT, a roupa não seca is enough. In ES, intransitive drying needs a se: la ropa no se seca. hgbrasil.com

FALSE FRIEND Verb DE weak, separable (an-), perfect with haben, stem in -d → du zündest an · PT-BR acender, regular -er, participle acendido / aceso · ES-MX prender, regular -er — careful: PT-BR prender = to fasten, to arrest

Am Sonntag zünden wir die erste Kerze am Adventskranz an.

Zünde die Kerze bitte nicht neben dem Vorhang an.

Hast du die Kerze schon angezündet?

to light

On Sunday we light the first candle on the Advent wreath.

Please don't light the candle next to the curtain.

Have you lit the candle yet?

acender

¿Ya prendiste la vela?

Prender is not the same as prender The Mexican DEM lists "Hacer que arda, se queme o produzca fuego alguna cosa" first for prender, with prender un cerillo. The third meaning is "Poner a funcionar un aparato": prender la luz, prender la televisión. Priberam lists no transitive sense of lighting a candle for prender. It gives "Atar, ligar" (to tie up) and "Capturar, encarcerar" (to arrest). So if you say prendi a vela in Brazil, you have fastened the candle, not lit it. dem.colmex.mx · priberam.org

Toró, Varal, Adventskranz — Words for a Wet Weekend

Rain, laundry and the first candle

What Advent is: Picture a countdown to Christmas, but only on Sundays. Advent begins on the fourth Sunday before December 25. In 2026, December 25 falls on a Friday, so the first Sunday of Advent is Sunday, November 29. The next ones are December 6, 13 and 20. The Advent wreath holds four candles. On each Sunday of Advent, you light one more. smart-rechner.de
BR-PT Noun DE m, pl. Platzregen · EN downpour · PT-BR o toró, m [Brasil] — neutral o aguaceiro, m · ES-MX el aguacero, m
der Platzregen

Am Nachmittag kam ein Platzregen, und alle rannten unters Dach.

Nach dem Platzregen stand die Straße unter Wasser.

the downpour

In the afternoon there was a downpour, and everyone ran for cover.

After the downpour, the street was under water.

o toró

À tarde caiu um toró, e todo mundo correu para debaixo do telhado.

Depois do toró, a rua ficou alagada.

el aguacero

En la tarde cayó un aguacero, y todos corrieron a cubrirse.

Después del aguacero, la calle quedó inundada.

Short and heavy Priberam lists toró with the label [Brasil]: "Chuva forte. = CHUVADA, CHUVARADA". Linguee gives aguaceiro as the main translation of Platzregen. The Mexican DEM captures the core of the German word exactly: aguacero is "Lluvia muy intensa, en especial la que se precipita de manera repentina y durante poco tiempo". Sudden and brief, so a downpour and not steady rain. priberam.org · linguee.de · dem.colmex.mx
IDIOM Idiom DE colloquial, figurative · EN it's raining cats and dogs · PT-BR está chovendo canivete [Brasil] · ES-MX está lloviendo a cántaros
es regnet Bindfäden

Schau mal raus — es regnet Bindfäden.

Es hat den ganzen Samstag Bindfäden geregnet.

it's raining cats and dogs

Look outside — it's raining cats and dogs.

It rained cats and dogs all Saturday.

está chovendo canivete

Olha lá fora: está chovendo canivete.

Choveu canivete o sábado inteiro.

está lloviendo a cántaros

Mira afuera: está lloviendo a cántaros.

Llovió a cántaros todo el sábado.

Strings, knives, jugs The DWDS explains es regnet Bindfäden as "regnet sehr stark", figurative and colloquial. Wiktionary lists chover canivete as a Brazilian idiom: "to rain cats and dogs (to rain heavily)". It also gives a second meaning: Não saio daqui nem que chova canivetes — "I'm not leaving here, no matter what happens." The DEM lists a cántaros as "En gran cantidad, en abundancia: llover a cántaros". We had a similar expression on Oct. 28: es gießt wie aus Eimern. dwds.de · wiktionary.org · dem.colmex.mx
Noun DE f, pl. -n · EN clothesline · PT-BR o varal, m · ES-MX el tendedero, m
die Wäscheleine

Häng die Wäsche heute nicht an die Wäscheleine im Garten.

Wir spannen eine Wäscheleine im Flur.

the clothesline

Don't hang the laundry on the clothesline in the yard today.

We're putting up a clothesline in the hallway.

o varal

Hoje não pendure a roupa no varal do quintal.

Vamos esticar um varal no corredor.

el tendedero

Hoy no tiendas la ropa en el tendedero del patio.

Vamos a poner un tendedero en el pasillo.

Line or place? Priberam describes varal as "Estrutura, geralmente feita de corda ou de varas metálicas, onde se coloca a roupa para secar", with the synonym estendal. The DEM takes tendedero more broadly, as a place: "Lugar en donde se tiende o se pone a secar la ropa". Its example there is typically Mexican: "El tendedero está en la azotea" — on the flat roof. The matching verb is tender la ropa. priberam.org · dem.colmex.mx
GENDER Noun DE m, pl. Adventskränze · EN Advent wreath · PT-BR a coroa do Advento, f (also a grinalda de Advento) · ES-MX la corona de Adviento, f
der Adventskranz

Der Adventskranz hat vier Kerzen, eine für jeden Sonntag.

Die Kinder basteln den Adventskranz am Samstag, weil es sowieso regnet.

the Advent wreath

The Advent wreath has four candles, one for each Sunday.

The kids are making the Advent wreath on Saturday, since it's raining anyway.

a coroa do Advento

A coroa do Advento tem quatro velas, uma para cada domingo.

As crianças vão montar a coroa do Advento no sábado, já que vai chover mesmo.

la corona de Adviento

La corona de Adviento tiene cuatro velas, una para cada domingo.

Los niños van a hacer la corona de Adviento el sábado, total va a llover.

A wreath becomes a crown PT and ES use the word for "crown" for the wreath: coroa, corona, both feminine. Linguee gives grinalda de Advento as the main PT translation, but its example sentence uses a vela da coroa do Advento. In ES, corona de Adviento is the main translation. Watch the vowel: PT Advento, ES Adviento. linguee.de · linguee.de
