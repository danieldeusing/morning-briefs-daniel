Final Vocabulary: virar o jogo, torcer, converter

Thursday, November 26 — rotation topic Football & Sports, episode seven. On Saturday, 11/28, at 5 p.m. Brasília time, Palmeiras and Estudiantes play the Libertadores final at the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo. It is a single match on neutral ground. Both finalists only got through the semifinal on penalties. So today we learn how to convert a penalty, how to turn a game around and who you root for. An honest note on the league: the last table we could verify is from 11/18 (Flamengo 79, Palmeiras 73). We did not confirm the result of the head-to-head on 11/21. 10 entries, 3 of them verbs with full conjugation, including 2 refreshers.

  • FALSE FRIEND torcer (PT) ≠ torcer (ES) — in Brazil, torcer pelo Palmeiras means "to root for Palmeiras". In Spanish, torcer only means "to twist": me torcí el tobillo = "I sprained my ankle".
  • FALSE FRIEND das Finale ≠ the finale — in sports, English says the final. The finale is the big ending of a show or series.
  • IDIOM virar o jogo (PT) = "to turn a game around", literally "to flip the game". The result is called a virada. In Mexico people say remontar and la remontada.
  • BR-PT o pênalti · el penal — Brazil spells pênalti with ê, Portugal penálti. Mexico says el penal, Spain el penalti. "In the penalty shootout" is nos pênaltis and en penales.
  • CONJUGATION convierto · convirtió — ES convertir changes its stem twice: e → ie in the present, e → i in convirtió and convirtamos. PT converter stays regular. reverso.net · conjugacao.com.br
  • CONJUGATION torço · torça — before o and a, PT torcer writes ç so the sound stays soft: eu torço, que eu torça. conjugacao.com.br
  • CONJUGATION hält · hielt · gehalten — halten is strong: umlaut in du hältst, er hält, ablaut a → ie in the past tense. verbformen.de

Three Verbs: to Turn Around, to Convert, to Root For

3 verbs with full conjugation · DE · EN · PT-BR · ES-MX · cross-checked against verbformen.de, conjugacao.com.br and conjugator.reverso.net

Why only one match? The Libertadores final used to have two legs, one in each finalist's stadium. Today it is a single match at a fixed location, similar to the Champions League final. On 11/28 that is the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo, Uruguay. Palmeiras comes from Brazil, Estudiantes from Argentina. So nobody plays at home. If it is level at the end, a penalty shootout decides. That is exactly how both finalists reached the final: Palmeiras against Fluminense, Estudiantes against Flamengo. oliberal.com · Article from 10/31/2026
Verb DE weak, regular, participle gedreht, perfect with haben · PT-BR virar (o jogo), regular -ar · ES-MX remontar (el partido), regular -ar
ein Spiel drehen

Nach dem 0:1 drehte die Mannschaft das Spiel noch.

Wer in einem Finale zurückliegt, muss das Spiel drehen.

Wir haben das Spiel in zehn Minuten gedreht.

to turn a game around

After going 1-0 down, the team still turned the game around.

If you fall behind in a final, you have to turn the game around.

We turned the game around in ten minutes.

virar o jogo

Depois do 0 a 1, o time ainda virou o jogo.

Quem sai atrás numa final precisa virar o jogo.

A gente virou o jogo em dez minutos.

remontar el partido

Después del 0-1, el equipo todavía remontó el partido.

Si vas abajo en una final, tienes que remontar el partido.

Remontamos el partido en diez minutos.

Show conjugation

DE: fully regular; in the future you also hear colloquial werde drehn. The object goes in between: wir drehen das Spiel. PT: virar also means "to turn over" and "to become" (virou moda = "it became fashionable"); the comeback is called a virada. ES-MX: also darle la vuelta al marcador. For nós and nosotros, present and preterite are the same (viramos, remontamos). verbformen.de · conjugacao.com.br · reverso.net

Verb DE weak, inseparable (ver-), participle without ge-: verwandelt, ich verwandle without e, perfect with haben · PT-BR converter, regular -er · ES-MX convertir, stem change e → ie and e → i
verwandeln

Er verwandelte den entscheidenden Elfmeter.

Wenn ich diesen Elfmeter verwandle, sind wir im Finale.

Wer verwandelt am Samstag, wenn es wieder ins Elfmeterschießen geht?

to convert

He converted the decisive penalty.

If I convert this penalty, we're in the final.

Who will convert on Saturday if it goes to penalties again?

converter

Ele converteu o pênalti decisivo.

Se eu converter este pênalti, a gente está na final.

Quem converte no sábado, se for para os pênaltis de novo?

convertir

Él convirtió el penal decisivo.

Si convierto este penal, estamos en la final.

¿Quién convierte el sábado si se va otra vez a penales?

Show conjugation

DE: verbs in -eln drop the e in ich verwandle; the prefix ver- means no ge- in the participle. Outside football, verwandeln means "to transform", just like converter and convertir (a file, money). PT: Se eu converter is the Futuro do subjuntivo, identical to the infinitive. ES: e → ie under stress (convierto), e → i in convirtió, convirtieron and convirtamos. Final, Venue, Supporters — Saturday in Montevideo

Three new entries and one refresher for the final

Noun DE n, pl. Finale · EN the final · PT-BR a final, f · ES-MX la final, f
das Finale

Das Finale beginnt am Samstag um 17 Uhr Brasília-Zeit.

Palmeiras steht im Finale, Flamengo ist draußen.

the final

The final kicks off on Saturday at 5 p.m. Brasília time.

Palmeiras is in the final, Flamengo is out.

a final

A final começa no sábado, às 17h de Brasília.

O Palmeiras está na final, o Flamengo está fora.

la final

La final empieza el sábado a las 17:00, hora de Brasilia.

El Palmeiras está en la final, el Flamengo quedó fuera.

False friend: finale English finale is the big ending of a concert or a season. The deciding match in sports is always the final. PT and ES tell them apart by gender: a final and la final are the final match, o final and el final the ending. According to O Liberal: “A partida será disputada no dia 28 de novembro, às 17h (de Brasília)”. oliberal.com
Noun DE m, pl. Austragungsorte · EN the venue · PT-BR a sede, f · ES-MX la sede, f

One word, two meanings A sede and la sede also mean "company headquarters" (a sede da empresa). Heads up, PT: a sede with an open é is the seat, a sede with a closed ê is thirst. Same spelling, different sound.
Noun DE m, no pl. in the sense of "fans" · EN the supporters · PT-BR a torcida, f · ES-MX la afición, f

Anhang = fans and attachment In the office, the Anhang is the file attached to an e-mail (PT o anexo, ES el archivo adjunto). In the stadium, it means the fans. A torcida comes from torcer, the verb from above. In Mexico, an organized group of fans is also colloquially called la porra; festejar is more common there than celebrar.

Neutral ground On 11/12 it was about home advantage in everyday league play. The final is the opposite: a stadium in Uruguay, for one club from Brazil and one from Argentina. PT calls this campo neutro, ES-MX cancha neutral.

It's Thursday, November twenty-sixth. Today's rotation topic is football and sports, episode seven. We're looking at the Libertadores final: turning a game around, converting a penalty and rooting for a club. Ten entries, three of them verbs with full conjugation and two refreshers.

On Saturday, November twenty-eighth, Palmeiras and Estudiantes play the final at five p.m. Brasília time. According to O Liberal, the venue is the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo. It's a single match on neutral ground, so neither side has home advantage. Both finalists only got through the semifinal on penalties.

To turn a game around is virar o jogo in Brazil, and the comeback is a virada. In Mexico, you say remontar el partido. To convert is converter in Portuguese and convertir in Spanish. In Spanish the stem changes: yo convierto, él convirtió. Brazil calls the penalty o pênalti, and Mexico calls it el penal.

To root for a club is torcer para in Portuguese. Careful: in Spanish, torcer only means to twist, like an ankle. In Mexico, you ask instead: A qué equipo le vas? The fans together are a torcida and la afición. In German, you can also say der Anhang.

On the league, we're going by the table from November eighteenth. Back then Flamengo led with seventy-nine points, and Palmeiras had seventy-three. We could not confirm the head-to-head from November twenty-first. In the relegation battle, Grêmio and Internacional stood at thirty-eight points each.
